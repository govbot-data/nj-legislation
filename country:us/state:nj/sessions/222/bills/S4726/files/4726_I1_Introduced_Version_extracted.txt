STATEMENT This bill transfers responsibility for disbursing Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF) revenues from the Legislature to the Cannabis Regulatory Commission (CRC). Under current law, the CRC may impose the SEEF on cultivators, which is calculated based on the retail market price of cannabis in this State, and which may be adjusted annually.  SEEF revenues are collected by the Division of Taxation in the Department of the Treasury and deposited into the Cannabis Regulatory, Enforcement Assistance, and Marketplace Modernization Fund.  SEEF monies are dedicated to annual appropriations by the Legislature for investing in social equity programs in order to create, expand, or promote educational and economic opportunities and activities, and the health and well-being of both communities and individuals. Under current law, the CRC holds public hearings annually in order to make recommendations to the Governor and the Legislature concerning the appropriation of SEEF funds in the annual State budget.  These recommendations can include grants, loans, reimbursements of expenses, and other financial assistance to private and public entities, as well as qualifying individuals, to fund such investments as educational, literacy, and GED programs; economic development, job skills, and apprenticeship programs; social support services including food assistance, mental health and substance abuse treatment, and reentry services; and legal aid services.  Following receipt of the CRCs recommendations, the Legislature provides the CRC with a list of the SEEF investments funded in the State budget. For 2026, the SEEF rate is $2.50 per ounce.  According to the CRCs 2026 SEEF report, the CRC has estimated that the SEEF will generate $12.4 million in Fiscal Year 2026, and $14.1 million in Fiscal Year 2027.  According to the 2026 report, as of March 2026, $11.924 million in SEEF funds have been disbursed by the Legislature in Fiscal Year 2026 appropriations, with $3.667 million remaining unallocated.  In Fiscal Year 2026, the $11.924 million appropriation from SEEF funds appears to have been allocated through the annual appropriations act, P.L.2025, c.74, entirely to the Department of Corrections to support Purchase of Community Services grants as determined by the Commissioner of Corrections.  It should be noted that, according to the CRCs 2025 SEEF report, the Legislature did not make any SEEF appropriations in Fiscal Years 2023, 2024, and 2025.  Thus, according to the 2025 report, at the beginning of 2025, there was $5.129 million in SEEF funds accumulated from prior years. Under the bill, the CRC will become directly responsible for disbursing SEEF funds, through a public competitive application process.  The bill provides for the CRC to receive annual appropriations of SEEF funds from the State budget.  The CRC will be required to award SEEF grants, loans, and other financial assistance to the same types of social equity investments set forth under current law.  The CRC will also be required to provide an annual report to the Governor and the Legislature with a list of funding recipients, type of funding (grants, loans, or other assistance), and amounts awarded, together with a statement for each recipient describing how the funding is intended to support and advance social equity.
