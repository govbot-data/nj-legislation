STATEMENT

 

     This bill concerns penalties for assault of motorbus and passenger rail service employees.  Specifically, the bill amends current law to allow the New Jersey Transit Corporation (corporation), an entity that provides rail passenger service (entity), and an autobus company to prohibit from using transportation services provided by the corporation, an entity, or an autobus company any person who assaults an employee of the corporation, entity, or an autobus company for up to five calendar years.  However, the bill permits any such person, after having been barred from the corporations, entitys, or autobus companys transportation services, to petition the corporation, entity, or autobus company for relief to reduce or end the period of suspension for good cause based on change in circumstances, substantial need for the transportation service, or proof of rehabilitation.
