S3234 SENATE, No. 3234 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED FEBRUARY 2, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  SHIRLEY K. TURNER District 15 (Hunterdon and Mercer) SYNOPSIS Requires maintenance of roadways surrounding State capitol complex. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act requiring maintenance of the roadways that surround the State capitol complex and supplementing Title 27 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    The Department of Transportation shall maintain the roadways that surround the State capitol complex, including: West State Street from Calhoun Street to South Warren Street; West Lafayette Street from Barrack Street to South Warren Street; Barrack Street from West State Street to Memorial Drive; and Memorial Drive. Maintenance of these roadways shall include road paving and snow plowing.  To assist in fulfilling the requirements of this section, the department may consult or contract with any State agency, county, or municipality that is responsible for maintaining the adjoining portion of each of the roadways. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires the Department of Transportation (department) to maintain the roadways that surround the State capitol complex.  Specifically, the bill requires the maintenance of specific sections of West State Street, West Lafayette Street, Barrack Street, and Memorial Drive within the City of Trenton.  Maintenance of these roadways is required to include road paving and snow plowing.  To assist in fulfilling the bills requirements, the department may consult or contract with any State agency, county, or municipality that is responsible for maintaining the adjoining portion of each of the roadways.
