     Following a vehicle ahead too closely, including violations of R.S.39:4-89;

STATEMENT

 

     This bill amends the definition of commercial motor vehicle under the New Jersey Commercial Driver License Act to revise the minimum passenger capacity threshold. 

     Under current law, a person is prohibited from operating a commercial motor vehicle in this State without possession of a commercial driver license (CDL).  A commercial motor vehicle is currently defined in statute as a vehicle that:  (1) is designed to transport eight or more but less than 16 persons, including the driver, which is used to transport such persons for hire on a daily basis to and from places of employment; (2) is designed to transport 16 or more passengers, including the driver; (3) has a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of 26,001 or more pounds or displays a GVWR rating of 26,001 or more pounds; (4) has a gross combination weight rating of 26,001 or more pounds inclusive of a towed unit with a GVWR of more than 10,000 pounds; (5) is transporting, or is used to transport, hazardous materials and is required to be placarded in accordance with federal regulations or displays a hazardous material placard; or (6) is operated by, or under contract with, a public or governmental agency or a religious or other charitable organization or corporation, or is privately operated, and is used to transport children to and from certain locations.

     The bill, while maintaining all other provisions of the definition of commercial motor vehicle:  (1) makes a technical change to clarify that a vehicle is to be designated as a commercial motor vehicle if the vehicle is designed to transport 15 or more passengers, in addition to the driver; and (2) removes a provision designating a vehicle as a commercial motor vehicle if the vehicle is designed to transport eight or more but less than 16 persons, including the driver, and is used to transport such persons for hire on a daily basis to and from places of employment.  Under this revised definition, a person is no longer required to possess a CDL to operate a vehicle designed to transport less than 16 passengers, including the driver.
