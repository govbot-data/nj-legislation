STATEMENT This bill expressly includes a clean energy product manufacturer among the companies that may sell certain unused tax benefits under the corporation business tax benefit certificate transfer program (program) administered by the New Jersey Economic Development Authority.  The bill also modifies certain requirements of the program. Under current law, the program, which is established within the New Jersey Emerging Technology and Biotechnology Financial Assistance Program, allows new or expanding technology and biotechnology companies in this State to sell a tax credit certificate to another taxpayer.  Specifically, the company may sell its unused research and development tax credits or its net operating loss, which may then be used by the purchasing taxpayer. The bill expressly provides that a clean energy product manufacturer qualifies as a technology company that may participate in the program, provided that all other applicable requirements are met.  However, the bill exempts a clean energy product manufacturer from the requirement that the company have fewer than 225 employees in the United States on June 30 of the year in which the company applies under the program and on the date the tax benefit certificate is sold. As defined in the bill, a "clean energy product manufacturer" is a business engaged in the production or assembly of goods by transforming raw materials or sub-components into components for renewable energy, such as offshore wind, solar, geothermal, green hydrogen, nuclear energy, fuel cells, battery storage, or other clean energy manufacturing.  "Clean energy product manufacturer" does not include businesses engaged in retail, wholesale, packaging, software development, resource extraction, or waste incineration. Additionally, the bill raises the maximum lifetime value of tax benefits that a technology or biotechnology company may sell through the program from $20 million to $40 million.
