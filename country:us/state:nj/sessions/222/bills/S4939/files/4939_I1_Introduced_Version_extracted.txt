S4939 SENATE, No. 4939 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  KRISTIN M. CORRADO District 40 (Bergen, Essex and Passaic) Senator  PATRICK J. DIEGNAN, JR. District 18 (Middlesex) Co-Sponsored by: Senator Tiver SYNOPSIS Eliminates fee to reissue motor vehicle certificate of ownership following death of co-owner of motor vehicle. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning motor vehicle certificate of ownership fees and supplementing Title 39 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    Notwithstanding any law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission shall not charge a fee to reissue a certificate of ownership to a surviving owner of a motor vehicle who presents to the commission the death certificate of the deceased co-owner of the motor vehicle. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill prohibits the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (commission) from charging a fee to reissue a certificate of ownership to a surviving owner of a motor vehicle who presents to the commission the death certificate of the deceased co-owner of the motor vehicle.
