S3475 1R [First Reprint] SENATE, No. 3475 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED FEBRUARY 12, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  JAMES BEACH District 6 (Burlington and Camden) Senator  RAJ MUKHERJI District 32 (Hudson) Co-Sponsored by: Senator Amato SYNOPSIS Permits DVA to receive voluntary contributions for veteran suicide prevention public service announcements. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Senate Military and Veterans' Affairs Committee on May 21, 2026, with amendments. An Act permitting the Department of Veterans Affairs to accept moneys for veteran suicide prevention public service announcements and supplementing chapter 22 of Title 38A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    The Department of Veterans Affairs shall be permitted to accept moneys received by the department as gifts, grants, or donations from the public and private sectors for the purpose of public service announcements for the prevention of veteran suicide. Moneys received by the department pursuant to this section shall be deposited into a special nonlapsing fund maintained by the State Treasurer.  Moneys credited to such account shall be appropriated solely to fund public service announcements for the prevention of veteran suicide. 1The Department of Veterans Affairs and the Department of the Treasury shall adopt rules and regulations pursuant to the "Administrative Procedure Act," P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.), concerning the receipt of monies by the Department of Veterans Affairs to implement the provisions of this act, P.L. , c.   (C.   ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).1 2.    This act shall take effect immediately.
