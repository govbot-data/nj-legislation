SYNOPSIS

     Establishes "Data Center Standards Task Force" in DCA.  

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act establishing a task force in the Department of Community Affairs to conduct a study and issue standards for data centers. 

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    a.  There is established in the Division of Codes and Standards in the Department of Community Affairs the "Data Center Standards Task Force."  The purpose of the task force shall be to conduct a study and issue health, welfare, and safety standards for data center development in New Jersey. 

     b.    The seven-member task force, established pursuant to subsection a. of this section, shall consist of: (1) one member appointed by the Governor; (2) one member appointed by the Senate President; (3) one member appointed by the Speaker of the General Assembly; (4) two members from the union labor industry appointed by the Director of the Division of Codes and Standards; (5) one member appointed by a member of the Senate who represents a district where a data center is proposed; and (6) one member appointed by a member of the General Assembly who represents a district where a data center is proposed.  The Senate President shall designate which member of the Senate shall make the appointment and the Speaker of the General Assembly shall designate which member of the General Assembly shall make the appointment pursuant to this subsection. 

     c.     Vacancies in the membership of the task force shall be filled in the same manner as the original appointments were made. 

     d.    The members of the task force shall serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for travel and other miscellaneous expenses necessary to perform their duties, within the limits of funds made available to the task force for its purposes. 

     e.     The Commissioner of Community Affairs shall provide professional and clerical staff to the task force as necessary to effectuate the purposes of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill). 

 

     2.    a.  The task force shall complete its first meeting and appoint a chairperson within 60 days of the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).  The task force may meet and hold hearings at the place or places as it shall designate.

     b.    A majority of the membership of the task force shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of task force business. 

 

     3.    a.  It shall be the duty of the task force to study the development of data centers and identify appropriate health, welfare, and safety standards for data centers.  The task force shall make recommendations for legislation or other action as it deems appropriate with regard to data centers. 

     b.    The task force shall present a report of its findings and recommendations to the Governor, and to the Legislature pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), within one year of the organization of the task force.  The task force shall dissolve following the presentation of the report.  

 

     4.    This act shall take effect immediately and expire upon the issuance of the report by the task force pursuant to section 3 of this act. 

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill establishes the "Data Center Standards Task Force" (task force) in the Division of Codes and Standards in the Department of Community Affairs.  The purpose of the task force is to conduct a study and issue health, welfare, and safety standards for data centers in New Jersey.  The bill provides for the appointment of membership of the task force. 

     The task force is required to complete its first meeting and appoint a chairperson within 60 days of the effective date of the bill. 

     It is the duty of the task force to study the development of data centers and identify appropriate standards for data centers.  The task force is required to make recommendations for legislation or other action as it deems appropriate with regard to data centers.  The task force is required to present a report of its findings and recommendations to the Governor and to the Legislature within one year of the organization of the task force.  The bill is to take effect immediately and expire upon the issuance of the report by the task force.
