SYNOPSIS

     Stop Sepsis Act; requires hospitals to establish sepsis recognition and treatment protocols, train staff, and establish quality measures.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As reported by the Assembly Health Committee on May 11, 2026, with amendments.

  

An Act concerning sepsis protocols in hospitals 1, amending P.L.2007, c.196,1 and supplementing chapter 2H of Title 26 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     11.   (New section)  This act shall be known and may be cited as the Stop Sepsis Act.1

     g.    As used in this section, hospital means a general or special hospital licensed pursuant to P.L.1971, c.136 (C.26:2H-1 et seq.).1
