SYNOPSIS

     Automatic Utility and Communications Service Credit Act; requires certain telecommunications utilities to provide credits for service outages of longer than three hours.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning bill credits for telecommunications service outages, designated as the Automatic Utility and Communications Service Credit Act, amending P.L.1987, c.422 and P.L.1991, c.428, and supplementing Title 48 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

     1.    a.  [The] Prior to the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), the director, with the approval of the board, shall adopt rules and regulations providing for a credit or rebate for outages of cable television service [which] that are six or more hours in duration.  The amount of the credit or rebate shall be in one day units, prorated on the basis of the subscriber's monthly rate for each service so interrupted.  In order to obtain a credit or rebate, a subscriber shall notify by telephone or in writing the cable television company, the Office of Cable Television or other designated complaint officer within 30 days after any such outage.  The regulations may provide for appropriate exceptions and limitations.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill, which is designated as the Automatic Utility and Communications Service Credit Act, establishes and amends certain telecommunications bill credits for service outages.  Under the bill, every:  (1) cable television company; (2) direct broadcast satellite service provider; (3) internet service provider; (4) local exchange telecommunications company; (5) wireless telephone provider; and (6) interexchange telecommunications carrier (collectively, telecommunications companies), is required to provide a bill credit to a customer or subscriber that experienced a complete outages of service for three or more hours in duration, which bill credit is to be prorated at one thirtieth of the customers or subscribers monthly bill for every three hours of complete outage of service and is to be automatically applied to the customers or subscribers next bill statement.

     The bill further specifies that a failure to apply a bill credit by a telecommunications company is to be a violation of the New Jersey Consumer Fraud Act.  Additionally, the bill provides that a telecommunications company is to be liable, in addition to any legal or equitable remedy under the New Jersey Consumer Fraud Act, to a civil penalty of $1,000 per affected customer or subscriber and up to $25,000 per each instance of complete outage, which is to be collected by the Board of Public Utilities in a summary proceeding before a court of competent jurisdiction.

     Under the bill, a complete outage means a total interruption of service that prevents the customer or subscriber from using the service for the services intended purpose but does not include outages due to:  non-emergency inclement weather; customer or subscriber equipment failure; customer or subscriber nonpayment; scheduled maintenance with advance notice to the customer or subscriber; and acts of war or terrorism.
