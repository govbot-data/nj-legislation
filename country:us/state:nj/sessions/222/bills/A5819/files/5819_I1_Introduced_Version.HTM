SYNOPSIS

     Interstate Voter Assistance Act; requires interstate voter registration communications for voter list maintenance purposes.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act establishing the Interstate Voter Assistance Act and supplementing Title 19 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    This act shall be known and may be cited as the Interstate Voter Assistance Act.

 

     2.    a.  Notwithstanding the provisions of section 16 of P.L.1974, c.30 (C.19:31-6.4) or any law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, the Secretary of State shall require the voter registration application of this State to be updated to include:

     (1)   a space or box for the applicant to indicate whether the applicant has previously been registered to vote in a different state;

     (2)   a space or box for the applicant to provide the address where the applicant was registered to vote at the time of submitting the application, including the street number, municipality, state, and zip code, or a detailed description of the location if no street address is available; and

     (3)   a statement, and corresponding space for the applicant to sign acknowledging and agreeing to the following:

     By completing and submitting this signed application, and upon its approval, I request the cancellation of the registration listed above by the former state pursuant to section 20507 of the National Voter Registration Act of 1993, (52 U.S.C. s.20507) and direct election officials to forward my request to the chief election official of that state.

     b.    If an individual registers to vote in this State and that individual's voter registration application indicates the individual has previously been registered to vote in a different state, the county commissioner of registration shall cause the following information, as made available by the processed voter registration application, to be recorded and transmitted to the Secretary of State:

     (1)   the voter's full legal name;

     (2)   the voter's date of birth;

     (3)   the voter's Social Security number or the last four digits of the Social Security number;

     (4)   the voter's driver's license or non-driver identification card number;

     (5)   any unique voter identification number assigned to the voter;

     (6)   the voter's current address of residence;

     (7)   the voter's previous address of residence; and

     (8)   a copy of the voter's registration application.

     c.     The Secretary of State shall prescribe the manner and frequency in which county commissioners of registration shall record and transmit the information collected under subsection b. of this section, but, at a minimum, the transmissions shall occur at least once every quarter in each calendar year.

     d.    If a registered voter's information is transmitted to the Secretary of State pursuant to this section, a record of the transmission and the information therein shall be included in the corresponding voter's registration record.  This record shall be treated as a Statewide voter registration system maintenance record and shall be stored with the voter's registration record for the duration of time the voter continues to be registered in this State.

 

     3.    a.  After receiving a registered voter's information provided under section 2 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), the Secretary of State shall send a notice to the chief election official in the jurisdiction where the individual was previously registered to vote informing the chief election official that:

     (1)   the identified individual was successfully registered to vote in this State;

     (2)   the individual listed a residential address located in this State on the individuals voter registration application; and

     (3)   the individual has requested cancellation of the individuals voter registration in the recipient official's state.

     The Secretary of State shall include with this notice a copy of the voter's registration application, or the information obtained pursuant to subsection b. of section 2 of P.L.    , c.   (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) and shall provide any other relevant information requested by the recipient official's state pursuant to section 20507 of the National Voter Registration Act of 1993, (52 U.S.C. s.20507).

     b.    The Secretary of State shall cause a report to be issued publicly at least once every quarter in each calendar year that includes:

     (1)   the total number of notices sent pursuant to subsection a. of this section since the last report was issued, the states to which the notices were sent, and the number of notices sent to each included state; and

     (2)   the total number of records received indicating that voters registered in this State have applied to register in another state and notice of the disposition of their voter registration.

     c.     Information received by a county commissioner of registration from an election official in another state indicating that a registered voter in this State has registered to vote in that other state shall be deemed a request from the voter to have the voter's name removed from the Statewide voter registration system.  The Secretary of State shall periodically make the information available to county commissioners of registration.  Upon receipt of the information, the voter shall be immediately removed from the Statewide voter registration list.

     4.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the fourth month next following enactment, except that the Secretary of State and any other administrative agency or officer may take anticipatory administrative action in advance as shall be necessary for the implementation of this act.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill, which is designated as the Interstate Voter Assistance Act, requires interstate communication among election officials for voter list maintenance purposes. The purpose of this bill is to ensure that voters registered in this State have the opportunity to cancel their registration in another state while registering in New Jersey, and that New Jersey voter registration records are updated if that voter registers to vote in another state.  This bill expands the requirements of the voter registration application and requires interstate communication among election officials to update the voter registration records of individuals filing a new voter registration application in New Jersey.

     This bill requires the Secretary of State to update the voter registration application to include a space or box for the applicant to identify if they were previously registered to vote in a different state, a space for the applicant to disclose the out-of-state residences to which they were registered to vote, and an acknowledgment for the applicant to sign allowing for interstate communication to request the cancellation of the applicants out-of-state registration.  This bill also requires each county commissioner of registration to notify the Secretary of State of all voter registrants who have indicated prior out-of-state voter registrations and requires the Secretary of State to keep and maintain all records of those voters.  After receiving a registered voter's information from a county commissioner of registration, the Secretary of State will send notice to the chief election official in the jurisdiction where the individual was previously registered to vote informing them that the identified individual was successfully registered to vote in this State and that the individual has requested cancellation of the individuals voter registration in the recipient official's state.  

     In addition, the bill provides that if a county commissioner of registration receives information from an election official in another state indicating that a registered voter in this State has registered to vote in that other state, that information will be deemed a request from the voter to have the voter's name removed from the Statewide voter registration system in this State.  The bill requires the Secretary of State to periodically make the information available to county commissioners of registration.  Upon receipt of the information, the voter will be immediately removed from the Statewide voter registration list.

     Under the bill, the Secretary of State will issue a report publicly at least once every quarter in each calendar year that reports the number of notices sent and received in accordance with the provisions of this bill.
