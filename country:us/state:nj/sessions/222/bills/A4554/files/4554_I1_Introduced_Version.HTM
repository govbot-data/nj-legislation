STATEMENT

 

     This bill would appropriate $111.6 million in dedicated natural resource damages revenues, collected from various legal actions enumerated in the bill, to the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) for certain habitat restoration and enhancement projects, land acquisition projects, restoration oversight activities, and administrative costs.  The bill would authorize the DEP to utilize the funds for State costs, as grants or loans to local governments, or as grants or loans to nonprofit organizations.
