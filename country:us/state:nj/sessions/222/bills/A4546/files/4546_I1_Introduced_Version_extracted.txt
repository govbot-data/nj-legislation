A4546 ASSEMBLY, No. 4546 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED MARCH 10, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  BRIAN BERGEN District 26 (Morris and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Prohibits NJTA and SJTA from charging and collecting tolls. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning toll collections in New Jersey, supplementing Title 27 of the Revised Statutes, and revising various parts of the statutory law. STATEMENT This bill prohibits the New Jersey Turnpike Authority (NJTA) and the South Jersey Transportation Authority (SJTA) from charging and collecting tolls for the use of any highway project under the NJTAs or the SJTAs jurisdiction, as appropriate.  To effectuate this provision, the bill amends and repeals various sections of current law.
