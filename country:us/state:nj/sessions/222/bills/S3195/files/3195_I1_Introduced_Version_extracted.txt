S3195 SENATE, No. 3195 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED JANUARY 28, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  MICHAEL L. TESTA, JR. District 1 (Atlantic, Cape May and Cumberland) SYNOPSIS Designates New Jersey and You  Perfect Together Again as Division of Travel and Tourism slogan. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act designating New Jersey and You  Perfect Together Again as the Division of Travel and Tourism slogan, and supplementing P.L.1977, c.255 (C.34:1A-45 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.  New Jersey and You  Perfect Together Again is designated as the Division of Travel and Tourism slogan. 2.  This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill designates New Jersey and You - Perfect Together Again as the Division of Travel and Tourism slogan. The division has utilized various marketing slogans over the years, including, but not limited to:  New Jersey and You: Perfect Together; New Jersey: Come See For Yourself; and Little State, Lotta Wow.  This bill will designate New Jersey and You - Perfect Together Again as the permanent slogan for the division.
