SYNOPSIS

     Establishes New Jersey Rural Health Access Act and Mobile Health Partnership Program.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning mobile health care services in rural and geographically underserved communities, and supplementing Title 26 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1. This act shall be known and may be cited as the New Jersey Rural Health Access Act.

 

     2. The Legislature finds and declares that:

     a. Residents of rural and geographically isolated communities may face barriers to obtaining primary and preventive health care, including limited transportation, distance from health care facilities, and limited availability of local health care providers.

     b. Mobile health care services provided through existing nonprofit and community-based organizations can improve access to preventive care, health education, and referrals for follow-up care.

     c. It is in the public interest to support partnerships that expand access to health care in rural and geographically underserved communities without requiring the State to purchase mobile units or establish new State-operated health care facilities.

 

     3. As used in P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill):

     Commissioner means the Commissioner of Health.

     Community health worker means an individual trained through a program recognized by the department to provide community outreach, assist individuals in accessing health care and related services, and provide culturally responsive health education.

     Department means the Department of Health.

     Eligible community means a rural or geographically underserved community designated by the department pursuant to section 4 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) in which residents face barriers to obtaining primary or preventive health care because of geographic isolation, limited transportation, or limited availability of local health care providers.

     Mobile health services means nonemergency health care services provided through a motor vehicle or other movable unit, including primary care assessments, preventive screenings, vaccinations, health education, and access to telemedicine or telehealth services, to the extent authorized by applicable law.

     Participating organization means a nonprofit or community-based organization that operates a mobile health service unit and enters into a contract with the department to provide mobile health services under the program.

     Program means the Mobile Health Partnership Program established pursuant to section 4 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).

 

     4. a. The department shall establish a Mobile Health Partnership Program to support and contract with qualified nonprofit and community-based organizations that operate mobile health service units to provide mobile health services in eligible communities.

     b. The department shall establish criteria for participation in the program.  A participating organization shall demonstrate the capacity to provide or administer:

     (1) primary care assessments and screenings;

     (2) preventive health services, including screening for high blood pressure, diabetes, and high cholesterol;

     (3) vaccinations; and

     (4) health education and services to improve health literacy.

     c. The department shall designate service regions throughout the State and identify eligible communities within those regions. In making these designations, the department shall consider population density, distance and travel time to health care providers, transportation availability, and the availability of primary and preventive health care services.

     d. Each contract shall identify the eligible communities to be served, the services to be provided, and the outreach and reporting requirements applicable to the participating organization. The department shall coordinate service coverage among participating organizations to promote equitable access within designated service regions.

     e. A participating organization shall comply with all applicable federal and State requirements governing the services provided, including professional licensure, scope of practice, facility licensure, medication administration, telemedicine and telehealth, and patient privacy. Nothing in this act shall be construed to waive an otherwise applicable licensure requirement or expand the scope of practice of a health care provider.

 

     5. a. The department may designate a program coordinator from among its existing staff to coordinate service coverage, serve as a liaison with community leaders and participating organizations, and oversee the collection of program data.

     b. Each participating organization shall submit an annual report to the department, in a form and manner and by a date prescribed by the department, concerning the preceding years activities. The report shall include the locations at which services were provided, the number and characteristics of individuals served, the types of services provided, and available information concerning health outcomes and referrals for follow-up care. Patient information submitted under this subsection shall be aggregated or de-identified in accordance with applicable federal and State law.

     c. No later than one year after the effective date of this act, and annually thereafter, the department shall prepare and submit to the Governor and, pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), to the Legislature a report on the program. The report shall summarize service coverage and utilization, evaluate the programs effectiveness in improving access to health care in eligible communities, identify gaps in coverage and barriers to implementation, and provide recommendations for improving the program. The report shall not disclose individually identifiable health information or information otherwise made confidential by federal or State law.

 

     6. A participating organization may collaborate with community health workers to support patient outreach, access to services, follow-up care, and culturally responsive health education.

 

     7. a. The department may use funds appropriated or otherwise made available to it for the purposes of this act and may apply for and accept federal grants and private grants, gifts, and donations to support the program, subject to applicable law and any restrictions governing the use of those funds.

     b. Nothing in this act shall be construed to require the purchase of mobile health service units or the establishment of new State-operated health care facilities.

 

     8. The commissioner shall adopt, pursuant to the Administrative Procedure Act, P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.), such rules and regulations as are necessary to effectuate the provisions of this act.

 

     9. This act shall take effect on the first day of the State fiscal year next following the date of enactment.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill establishes the New Jersey Rural Health Access Act and requires the Department of Health (department) to establish a Mobile Health Partnership Program (program) to support and contract with qualified nonprofit and community-based organizations that operate mobile health service units.  The program would provide nonemergency health care services in rural and geographically underserved communities.

     Under the bill, the department would establish criteria for participation, designate service regions, and identify eligible communities based on population density, distance and travel time to health care providers, transportation availability, and the availability of primary and preventive health care services.  Participating organizations would be required to demonstrate the capacity to provide or administer primary care assessments and screenings, preventive health services, vaccinations, and health education.  Mobile health services may also include access to telemedicine or telehealth services, as permitted by applicable law.

     The bill authorizes the department to designate a program coordinator from among its existing staff.  Participating organizations would submit annual reports concerning service locations, individuals served, services provided, and available information on health outcomes and referrals.  The department would report to the Governor and Legislature no later than one year after the bills effective date and annually thereafter on program activities, effectiveness, gaps in coverage, and recommendations for improvement.  The bill protects confidential patient information and preserves applicable licensure and scope-of-practice requirements.

     Participating organizations may collaborate with community health workers trained through programs recognized by the department to support outreach, access to services, follow-up care, and culturally responsive health education.

     The bill authorizes the department to use funds appropriated or otherwise made available for the program and to apply for and accept federal and private funding, subject to applicable law and funding restrictions.  The bill does not require the State to purchase mobile units or establish new State-operated health care facilities.

     The Commissioner of Health is required to adopt implementing rules and regulations.  The bill takes effect on the first day of the State fiscal year next following enactment.
