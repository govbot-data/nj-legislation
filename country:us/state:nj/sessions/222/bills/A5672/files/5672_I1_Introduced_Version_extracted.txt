A5672 ASSEMBLY, No. 5672 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED OCTOBER 19, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  KENYATTA STEWART District 35 (Bergen and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Prohibits suspension or revocation of drivers license or motor vehicle registration certificate in certain circumstances. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning toll enforcement and amending various parts of the statutory law. STATEMENT This bill revokes the authority of the Department of Transportation, New Jersey Turnpike Authority, South Jersey Transportation Authority, and New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission to suspend or revoke a drivers license or motor vehicle registration certificate or prohibit an individual from obtaining a motor vehicle registration certificate due to unpaid tolls.
