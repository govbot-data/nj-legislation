SYNOPSIS

     Authorizes funding for on-demand micro transit service under Senior Citizen and Disabled Resident Transportation Assistance Program.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As reported by the Senate Budget and Appropriations Committee on June 28, 2026, with amendments.

  


An Act concerning on-demand micro transit service funding and amending P.L.1983, c.578.
