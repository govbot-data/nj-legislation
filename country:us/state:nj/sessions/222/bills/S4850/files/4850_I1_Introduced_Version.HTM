SYNOPSIS

     Requires municipalities to specify in ordinance implementing eminent domain particular use intended for property.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill requires municipalities to specify, in any ordinance implementing the use of eminent domain, the explicit particular use intended for that property.  The requirement, under the "Local Lands and Buildings Law," P.L.1971, c.199 (C.40A:12-1 et seq.), is to apply to acquisition of any real property, capital improvement, or personal property within its jurisdiction to be acquired under the "Eminent Domain Act of 1971," P.L.1971, c.361 (C.20:3-1 et seq.).

     The bill also supplements the "Eminent Domain Act of 1971" to specify that when any condemnor that is a municipality seeks to acquire property by condemnation, the ordinance authorizing the action is to specify the exact public use or uses for which the property is being acquired.

     In Borough of Seaside Park v. Shree Jyoti, LLC, Docket No. A-2-25, decided on August 10, 2026, the New Jersey Supreme Court ruled 6-1 that municipalities do not have to state a specific public use or purpose in an ordinance when starting a condemnation action because such a requirement is not specified under current law.  This bill amends current law to require municipalities to specify in any ordinance implementing eminent domain the particular use intended for that property.

     The bill also makes technical corrections.
