STATEMENT

 

     This bill establishes a uniform prevailing wage contract threshold amount for all public work in the State.

     Under current law, the prevailing wage threshold for municipalities is adjusted every five years for changes in the Consumer Price Index.  In the case of any other public work, the prevailing wage threshold is set at $2,000.

     The bill eliminates the separate prevailing wage contract threshold amount applicable to nonmunicipal public work and applies the prevailing wage contract threshold amount to all public work, which amount will be adjusted on July 1 every five calendar years thereafter in proportion to the Consumer Price Index.
