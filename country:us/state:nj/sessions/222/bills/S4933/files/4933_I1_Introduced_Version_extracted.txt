S4933 SENATE, No. 4933 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  KRISTIN M. CORRADO District 40 (Bergen, Essex and Passaic) Senator  ANDREW ZWICKER District 16 (Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex and Somerset) SYNOPSIS Requires county prosecutors offices be notified of sexual assault complaints. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning sexual assault and supplementing Title 52 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  Every State, county, and municipal law enforcement agency and every campus police department at an institution of higher education in this State that appoints police officers pursuant to P.L.1970, c.211 (C.18A:6-4.2 et seq.) shall notify the appropriate county prosecutors office within 24 hours of receiving a report or complaint of a sexual assault pursuant to N.J.S.2C:14-2 or otherwise becoming aware of a sexual assault that occurred in the jurisdiction of the law enforcement agency or campus police department. Each county prosecutors office shall develop and implement protocols that assist law enforcement agencies and campus police departments in complying with this requirement. b.    A supervisor in the Special Victims Unit or the Juvenile Unit or an equivalent unit of each county prosecutors office shall review and approve all final dispositions in sexual assault cases, including decisions regarding whether to bring or decline to bring criminal charges.  In conducting the review, the supervising prosecutor shall ensure that any investigating officer and prosecutor complied with any applicable standards. c.     In addition to the requirements set forth in section 1 of P.L.2003, c.137 (C.2C:14-2.1), when a county prosecutors office declines to prosecute a sexual assault case, the assistant prosecutor assigned to the case shall provide the victim with an opportunity for an in-person meeting, during which the assistant prosecutor shall explain the basis for declining prosecution. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires county prosecutors offices be notified of sexual assault complaints. Specifically, under the provisions of this bill, every State, county, and municipal law enforcement agency and every campus police department is required to notify the appropriate county prosecutors office within 24 hours of receiving a report or complaint of a sexual assault or otherwise becoming aware of a sexual assault that occurred in the jurisdiction of the law enforcement agency or campus police department. In addition, the bill requires a supervisor in the Special Victims Unit or Juvenile Unit or an equivalent unit of each county prosecutors office to review and approve all final dispositions in sexual assault cases, including decisions regarding whether to bring or decline to bring criminal charges. Finally, when a county prosecutors office declines to prosecute a sexual assault case, the assistant prosecutor assigned to the case is required to provide the victim with an opportunity for an in-person meeting, during which the assistant prosecutor is required to explain the basis for declining prosecution.  This requirement is in addition to the requirement under current law to advise a victim of sexual assault of the right to be notified of the prosecuting agencys decision to file, or decline to file, criminal charges and, if appropriate, provide this notification. This bill codifies certain provisions of the Attorney General Law Enforcement Directive No. 2018-5.
