SYNOPSIS

     Requires owners of certain new electric transmission facilities to join regional transmission organizations approved by Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As reported by the Senate Economic Growth Committee on June 8, 2026, with amendments.

  

An Act concerning electric transmission facilities and supplementing Title 48 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

      11.  The Legislature finds and declares that: 

      a.   The reliable operation of the electric transmission system is essential to the health, safety, and economic welfare of the residents of the State and to the States ongoing response to the electricity affordability emergency declared by the Governor;

      b.   Membership in an RTO, as defined in section 2 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), for electric transmission facilities placed in service on or after January 1, 2027, is necessary to provide regulatory certainty for transmission planning, protect the States investment in regional transmission infrastructure, and ensure that changes in the ownership or business strategy of any individual transmission owner do not disrupt the reliable operation of the States electric transmission system; and

      c.   It is therefore in the public interest of the State to require that each electric transmission facility located in New Jersey and placed in service on or after January 1, 2027 is owned and operated by a transmission owner that has transferred operational control of those facilities to an entity approved by the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to serve as a regional transmission organization, independent system operator, or functionally-similar entity.1
