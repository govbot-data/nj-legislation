A5775 ASSEMBLY, No. 5775 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  ROY FREIMAN District 16 (Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex and Somerset) Assemblyman  BALVIR SINGH District 7 (Burlington) Assemblywoman  ROSAURA "ROSY" BAGOLIE District 27 (Essex and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Requires carriers to provide covered persons with option to electronically send and receive correspondence. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning health benefits plans and supplementing P.L.1997, c.192 (C.26:2S-1 et al.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  Notwithstanding P.L.1999, c.154 (C.17B:30-23 et al.), a carrier that offers a health benefits plan in this State shall provide a covered person with the option to electronically send and receive correspondence related to the health benefits plan of the covered person, including, but not limited to correspondence regarding the following: (1)   enrollment in a health benefits plan; (2)   standard medical, dental, and behavioral health claims; (3)   prescription drug claims; (4)   explanation of benefits; (5)   coordination of benefits; (6)   reports and itemized bills related to a claim; (7)   management of private information; (8)   change and termination requests; (9)   continuity of care requests; (10) continuity of practitioner care requests; (11) transition of care benefit requests; (12) requests for the continuance of enrollment for a disabled dependent; (13) medical records transfer requests; and (14) negotiation requests. b.    As used in this section, carrier means an insurance company, health service corporation, hospital service corporation, medical service corporation, or health maintenance organization authorized to issue health benefits plans in this State, and shall include the State Health Benefits Program and the School Employees' Health Benefits Program. 2.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the sixth month next following enactment, except that the Commissioner of Banking and Insurance may take any anticipatory administrative action in advance as shall be necessary for the implementation of this act. STATEMENT This bill requires a health insurance carrier to provide a covered person with the option to electronically send and receive correspondence related to the health benefits plan of the covered person.  Under the bill, carrier means an insurance company, health service corporation, hospital service corporation, medical service corporation, or health maintenance organization authorized to issue health benefits plans in this State, and shall include the State Health Benefits Program and the School Employees' Health Benefits Program.
