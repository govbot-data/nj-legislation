A5820 ASSEMBLY, No. 5820 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  AURA K. DUNN District 25 (Morris and Passaic) Assemblywoman  MARGIE DONLON, M.D. District 11 (Monmouth) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblyman Azzariti Jr. SYNOPSIS Requires parents or guardians of students to submit dental health certificate. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning oral health screenings for students and supplementing chapter 40 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  The parent or guardian of each student enrolled in a school district in grades kindergarten through eight shall annually submit a dental health certificate certifying that the student has undergone an oral health screening conducted by a licensed dentist or dental hygienist. b.  The dental health certificate shall: (1)   describe the dental health condition of the student when the assessment was made; (2)   provide whether the student is in fit condition of dental health to permit attendance in the district; and (3)  occur no more than 12 months prior to the start of the school year. c.  A student shall be exempt from the requirements of this section upon written request from the students parent or guardian. d.  Each school district shall annually provide the parent or guardian of each student with information on local dental care, low-cost dental programs, and information on free or low-cost dental care that may be covered under the NJ FamilyCare program. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately and shall first apply to the first full school year next following the date of enactment. STATEMENT This bill requires the parents or guardians of students to submit a dental health certificate. Under the bill, the parent or guardian of each student enrolled in a school district in grades kindergarten through eight is required to submit a dental health certificate certifying that the student has undergone an oral health screening conducted by a licensed dentist or dental hygienist.  The bill provides requirements for information that will be included in the dental health certificate. A student would be exempt from the requirement to submit a dental health certificate upon written request from the students parent or guardian. Finally, the bill requires a school district to annually provide the parent or guardian of each student with information on local dental care, low-cost dental programs, and information on free or low-cost dental care that may be covered under the NJ FamilyCare program. The sponsor notes that a study conducted by the National Institute of Health found that children with poor oral health are nearly three times more likely to miss school due to dental pain and that students in elementary school miss an average of 2.1 days per year for dental issues.
