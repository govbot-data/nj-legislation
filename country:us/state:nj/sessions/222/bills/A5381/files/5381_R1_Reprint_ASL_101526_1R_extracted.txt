A5381 1R [First Reprint] ASSEMBLY, No. 5381 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  WILLIAM F. MOEN, JR. District 5 (Camden and Gloucester) Assemblyman  ROY FREIMAN District 16 (Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex and Somerset) Assemblyman  CLINTON CALABRESE District 36 (Bergen and Passaic) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblyman Sampson SYNOPSIS Requires certain State contractors to provide members of public with option to submit certain information electronically. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Assembly State and Local Government Committee on October 15, 2026, with amendments. An Act requiring certain State contractors to provide members of the public with the option to submit certain information electronically and supplementing Title 52 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.  a.  1[The Division of Purchase and Property in the Department of the Treasury, and any] Any1 State agency having authority to contract for the purchase of goods or services1[,]1 shall require that, when the value of a contract is not less than $1 million, the contractor for such contract provides members of the public with the option to submit any required information electronically, when such information is required for the member of the public to establish eligibility to receive any goods, services, or benefits provided by the contractor pursuant to the contract. b.  As used in this section: Contractor means a business organization that has entered into a contract with a State agency. State agency means any of the principal departments in the 1[Executive Branch] executive branch1 of the State government, and any division, board, bureau, office, commission, or other instrumentality within or created by such department, and any independent State authority, commission, instrumentality, or agency which is authorized by law to award public contracts. 2.  This act shall take effect immediately.
