STATEMENT

 

     This bill makes a FY2027 supplemental appropriation of $400,000 to the Division of Mental Health and Addiction Services in the Department of Human Services for NJ 2-1-1 Partnership Operating Aid.  In doing so, the bill increases the FY2027 appropriation for this purpose from $400,000 to $800,000.  This line item was first funded in the FY 2024 Appropriations Act at $1.02 million. 

     NJ 2-1-1 is a free, confidential helpline - available 24 hours, seven days a week - that connects New Jersey residents with essential community, health, and human services.  NJ 2-1-1 is operated by the NJ 2-1-1 Partnership, an independent Statewide nonprofit corporation created through the leadership of the United Ways of New Jersey.
