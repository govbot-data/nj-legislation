ASSEMBLY CONCURRENT RESOLUTION No. 161

STATE OF NEW JERSEY

222nd LEGISLATURE

 

INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026

 

 

Sponsored by:

Assemblyman  SEAN T. KEAN

District 30 (Monmouth and Ocean)

 

 

 

 

SYNOPSIS

     Urges Congress to pass Jewish American Security Act.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


A Concurrent Resolution respectfully urging the United States Congress to pass the Jewish American Security Act.

 

Whereas, Antisemitism is a serious and growing danger for the Jewish community in the United States and around the world; and

Whereas, Holocaust denial and distortion, including intentional efforts to excuse or minimize the impact of the Holocaust and dishonor Holocaust victims and survivors, reinforce the need for advancing accurate and comprehensive Holocaust education globally; and

Whereas, In 2024, the Federal Bureau of Investigation identified the highest number of anti-Jewish hate crime incidents ever recorded by the Bureau since it began collecting data in 1991, which number represented an annual increase of approximately six percent in those incidents; and

Whereas, Jewish Americans have faced an unprecedented rise in antisemitic incidents following the October 7, 2023, Hamas terrorist attacks on Israel; and

Whereas, Antisemitic incidents have increased dramatically in many educational settings over the past several years, with many Jewish students facing discrimination or a hostile environment at schools, yet antisemitic incidents in schools remain underreported; and

Whereas, The federal Department of Educations Office for Civil Rights is tasked with ensuring that all students' civil rights are protected at school, yet there is a backlog of discrimination complaints that remain pending before the Office for Civil Rights; and

Whereas, While antisemitism most directly and intensely threatens those who identify or are perceived as Jewish Americans, it also undermines democracy and threatens the safety and rights of all Americans and

Whereas, In response to the rising instances of antisemitism in educational settings and across the United States, Congress introduced the bipartisan Jewish American Security Act, which is currently pending before the United States Senate as S.4576 and the United States House of Representatives as H.R.9211; and

Whereas, The Jewish American Security Act would: 1) provide funding to protect synagogues, community centers, and schools; 2) strengthen the enforcement of civil rights protections in federally funded schools and institutions of higher education; and 3) require large social media companies to submit transparency reports on how they moderate antisemitic content; and

Whereas, It is in the best interest of New Jerseyans and Jewish communities throughout the State for Congress to pass the Jewish American Security Act; and

Whereas, It is altogether fitting and proper for the Legislature to urge Congress to pass the Jewish American Security Act; now, therefore,

     Be It Resolved by the General Assembly of the State of New Jersey (the Senate concurring):

 

     1.    The Legislature of New Jersey respectfully urges the United States Congress to pass the Jewish American Security Act, which is pending before the United States Senate as S.4576 and the United States House of Representatives as H.R.9211, to combat rising antisemitism through enhanced physical security funding, educational civil rights enforcement, and digital platform accountability.

 

     2.    Copies of this resolution, as filed with the Secretary of State, shall be transmitted by the Clerk of the General Assembly or the Secretary of the Senate to the Majority and Minority Leaders of the United States Senate, the Speaker and Minority Leader of the United States House of Representatives, and each member of Congress elected from this State.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This resolution respectfully urges Congress to pass the Jewish American Security Act.

     In response to the rising instances of antisemitism in educational settings and across the United States, Congress introduced the bipartisan Jewish American Security Act. The Jewish American Security Act would: 1) provide funding to protect synagogues, community centers, and schools; 2) strengthen the enforcement of civil rights protections in federally funded schools and institutions of higher education; and 3) require large social media companies to submit transparency reports on how they moderate antisemitic content.

     New Jerseyans and Jewish communities throughout the State would benefit from this federal bipartisan legislative effort to combat rising antisemitism.  Thus, the Legislature respectfully urges Congress to pass the Jewish American Security Act.
