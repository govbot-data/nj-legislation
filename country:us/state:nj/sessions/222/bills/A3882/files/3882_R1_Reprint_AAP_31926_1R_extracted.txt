CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Assembly Appropriations Committee on March 19, 2026, with amendments. An Act concerning free and reduced-priced meal applications and supplementing P.L.2022, c.104 (C.18A:33-3.2 et al.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1. In addition to the requirements of section 16 of P.L.2022, c.104 (C.18A:33-21b1), a participating school, which is a public school, shall post a link to the subsidized school meals application1, as well as a link to at least one informational video produced by the New Jersey Office of the Food Security Advocate regarding the school meals application,1 in a prominent location on the schools Internet website. 2. This act shall take effect immediately.
