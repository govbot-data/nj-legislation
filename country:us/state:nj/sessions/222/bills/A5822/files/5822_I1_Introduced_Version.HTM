SYNOPSIS

     Requires DCA to develop training course on handling and response procedures of suspected child abuse and neglect activities for owners, operators, and staff of hotels and motels.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning child abuse and neglect training for certain hotel and motel staff and supplementing chapter 27D of Title 52 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    a.  The Department of Community Affairs, in consultation with the Department of Law and Public Safety, shall develop, approve, and provide for a one-time training course on the identification, handling, and response procedures of suspected child abuse and neglect activities.  The one-time training course shall be required for owners, operators, and staff of any hotel, as that term is defined in section 3 of the "Hotel and Multiple Dwelling Law," P.L.1967, c.76 (C.55:13A-3).  Alternatively, the department shall approve a substantially similar one-time training course for use by hotels in providing training to owners, operators, and staff.  The department shall, in accordance with the "Administrative Procedure Act," P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.), define by regulation which staff positions are to be required, as a condition of employment, to attend the one-time training course.  The Department of Community Affairs shall adopt rules and regulations pursuant to the "Administrative Procedure Act," P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.) to require verifiable completion of the training course by required staff as a condition of issuance, maintenance, or renewal of any license, permit, certificate, or approval required, permitted to be granted, or issued to owners or operators under the provisions of the "Hotel and Multiple Dwelling Law," P.L.1967, c.76 (C.55:13A-1 et seq.).  The training course shall include, at a minimum, information regarding the identification of known signs and indicators of child abuse and neglect, and the proper response procedures, including reporting procedures, for instances of suspected child abuse and neglect.  The Department of Community Affairs shall review the training course at least once every two years and shall, in consultation with the Department of Law and Public Safety, modify the course from time to time as the Department of Community Affairs deems necessary.

     b.    The Department of Community Affairs, through its oversight and enforcement authority provided under the "Hotel and Multiple Dwelling Law," P.L.1967, c.76 (C.55:13A-1 et seq.), shall ensure that all hotel and motel owners, operators, and required staff attend the one-time training course within one year of the enactment of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) in the case of all current owners, operators, and required staff engaging in their respective profession on the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), and within six months of the first day of ownership, operation, or employment for all new owners, operators, and required staff who initially engage in their respective profession after the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).

     c.     (1)        The Department of Community Affairs shall make available the training materials for the one-time training course to hotel owners, operators, and required staff in order for the owners, operators, and required staff to fulfill the one-time training requirement set forth in P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).

     (2)   The Department of Community Affairs shall provide for the expenditures of monies for any costs associated with the development, maintenance, revision, and distribution of training course materials for the course developed in accordance with P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) and the operation of the training course.

 

     2.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the fourth month next following enactment, except that the Department of Community Affairs may take anticipatory action as necessary to effectuate the provisions of this act.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill requires the Department of Community Affairs (DCA), in consultation with the Department of Law and Public Safety, to develop, approve, and provide for a one-time training course on the identification, handling, and response procedures of suspected child abuse and neglect activities.  The one-time training course is to be required for owners, operators, and staff of any hotel, as defined in the "Hotel and Multiple Dwelling Law," P.L.1967, c.76 (C.55:13A-1 et seq.) (HMDL) to include any hotel, motor hotel, motel, or established guesthouse.  Alternatively, the department is to approve a substantially similar one-time training course for use by hotels in providing training to owners, operators, and staff.  The department is required to define by regulation which staff positions are required, as a condition of employment, to attend the one-time training course.  Under the bill, verifiable completion of the training course by required staff is made a condition of issuance, maintenance, or renewal of any license, permit, certificate, or approval required, permitted to be granted, or issued to owners or operators under the provisions of the HMDL. 

     The training course is required to include, at a minimum, information regarding the identification of known signs and indicators of child abuse and neglect, and the proper response procedures, including reporting procedures, for instances of suspected child abuse and neglect.  The DCA is required to review the training course at least once every two years and modify the course as the DCA deems necessary.

     Under the bill, the DCA is responsible for ensuring that all hotel and motel owners, operators, and required staff attend the one-time training course within one year of the enactment of the bill in the case of all current owners, operators, and required staff, and within six months of the first day of ownership, operation, or employment for all new owners, operators, and required staff who initially engage in their respective profession after the effective date.
