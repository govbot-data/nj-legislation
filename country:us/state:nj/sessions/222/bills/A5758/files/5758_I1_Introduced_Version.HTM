STATEMENT

 

     This bill requires the Division of Consumer Affairs in the Department of Law and Public Safety to establish a web portal for consumers to opt out of certain data sharing.

     Current law provides that a consumer may exercise certain rights directly with a data controller.   These rights include the right to delete personal data and opt out of the processing of personal data for the purposes of: targeting advertising; the sale of personal data; or, profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.  Data controllers and brokers that engage in selling or licensing of personal data in New Jersey are currently required to register with the State.  Under the bill, the Director of Consumer Affairs is required to create a portal that permits consumers or their agents to annually submit a single request to exercise these rights with all registered data controllers.  That information is then to be conveyed to registered data controllers within 45 days.  Requests made via the portal would not preclude a consumer from exercising other statutory rights related to the use or sale of their data directly with a controller.
