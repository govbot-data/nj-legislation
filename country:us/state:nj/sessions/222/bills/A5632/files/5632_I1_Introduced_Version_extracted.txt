A5632 ASSEMBLY, No. 5632 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED OCTOBER 15, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  ROY FREIMAN District 16 (Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex and Somerset) SYNOPSIS Protecting New Jersey from Predatory Drug Pricing Act. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act prohibiting retail pharmacy permits for pharmacy benefits managers and supplementing P.L.2015, c.179 (C.17B:27F-1 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    A pharmacy benefits manager, licensed pursuant to P.L.2015, c.179 (C.17B:27F-1 et seq.), shall be prohibited from obtaining a permit to operate a pharmacy practice site pursuant to the New Jersey Pharmacy Practice Act, P.L.2003, c.280 (C.45:14-40 et seq.). 2.    This act shall take effect immediately and shall apply to pharmacy benefits managers licensed on or after the effective date. STATEMENT This bill establishes the Protecting New Jersey from Predatory Drug Pricing Act. Under the bill, a pharmacy benefits manager licensed under the laws of this State will be prohibited from obtaining a permit from the New Jersey State Board of Pharmacy to operate a pharmacy practice site under the New Jersey Pharmacy Practice Act.
