STATEMENT This bill establishes a presumption of legal parentage under certain circumstances. Under the bill, when a natural parent or an intended parent and the spouse of, or the partner in a civil union with, such parent appear on the childs birth certificate and possess a marriage or civil union certificate issued prior to the childs birth there is established a presumption that both persons are the legal parents of the child. Under current law, as established by the Legal Parentage Act, P.L.2019, c.323, a natural parent or person treated in State law as a legal parent of the child and the current or former partner in a civil union or the current or former spouse of the natural parent or person treated in State law as a legal parent may jointly file a complaint for a judgement of adoption.  As part of such complaint, the parties must submit proof of a valid civil union or marriage certificate issued prior to the birth of the child, an original birth certificate with both names appearing, and a written declaration signed by both parties detailing how the child was conceived and identifying any other parties involved so that the court may determine whether those individuals have parental rights. This bill would remove such requirements to obtain an order of adoption when the childs birth is a result of a gestational carrier agreement or artificial insemination, if: the couple was married or entered into a civil union prior to the childs birth; and both names appear on the birth certificate. The presumption of legal parentage established under this bill is intended to confer legal parental rights to parents who are married or have entered into a civil union and have conceived children through a gestational carrier agreement or artificial insemination by virtue of a birth certificate and to summarily extinguish or terminate the parental rights of any other individual.  The intent of this bill is to eliminate additional barriers to legal parentage for same-sex couples.
