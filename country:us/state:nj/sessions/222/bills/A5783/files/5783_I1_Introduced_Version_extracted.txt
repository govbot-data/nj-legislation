A5783 ASSEMBLY, No. 5783 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  STERLEY S. STANLEY District 18 (Middlesex) SYNOPSIS Requires Commissioner of Banking and Insurance to develop standard prior authorization form for prescription drug benefits for use by network providers. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning prior authorization of prescription drug benefits and supplementing P.L.2005, c.352 (C.17B:30-48 et al.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.  The Commissioner of Banking and Insurance shall develop a standard prior authorization request form to be utilized by all network providers for the purpose of submitting requests for utilization review determinations for coverage of prescription drug benefits.  A payer shall make the standard prior authorization request form available to, and shall accept it from, network providers in paper and electronic form. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires the Commissioner of Banking and Insurance to develop a standard prior authorization request form to be utilized by all network providers for the purpose of submitting requests for utilization review determinations for coverage of prescription drug benefits.  A payer must make the standard prior authorization request form available to, and must accept it from, network providers in paper and electronic form.
