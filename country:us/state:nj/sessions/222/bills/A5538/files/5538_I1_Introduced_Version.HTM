STATEMENT

 

     This bill makes a FY2027 supplemental appropriation of $455,000 to the Department of Health for the NJ Center for Tourette Syndrome and Associated Disorders.  In doing do, the bill restores the FY2026 appropriation of $455,000 that was that was cut from the current fiscal years appropriations act for the center.  Founded in 2004, the centers mission is to offer programs and resources to ensure children and adults with Tourette Syndrome and associated disorders are empowered and accepted.
