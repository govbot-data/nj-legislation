SYNOPSIS

     Requires free admission to Old Barracks at Trenton for NJ residents; appropriates $50,000.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning admission fees for entrance into the Old Barracks at Trenton, supplementing Title 28 of the Revised Statutes, and making an appropriation.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    The Legislature finds and declares that:

     a.     The United States was founded 250 years ago when the Second Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776.

     b.    New Jersey played a central role in the Revolutionary War and the founding of the United States.  Two days prior to the signing of the Declaration of Independence, the Provincial Congress of New Jersey met in Burlington and adopted the States first Constitution, which established a framework for representative government that would guide the newly independent State.  The first Legislature faced the extraordinary challenge of establishing a functioning government while the State served as a principal battleground of the Revolutionary War, with legislators forced to relocate multiple times to avoid British occupation.  The new, democratically elected State Government maintained all civilian authority over the States people, thereby creating a stable base in which the Continental Army could operate.

     c.     The Old Barracks at Trenton was used for a variety of purposes during the Revolutionary War by both the British troops and the American patriots, including a holding place for prisoners of war and an army hospital for the Continental Army.

     d.    Through the dissemination of national and local history, people can strengthen their civic identity, community engagement, and historical understanding.

     e.     As American independence enters its 250th year and people across the country and New Jersey reflect on the pivotal moments and events surrounding the birth of this nation through public celebrations, educational programs, and initiatives, it is fitting and proper for the Legislature to provide increased access to the rich history of New Jersey for the residents of this State by offering such residents free admission to the Old Barracks at Trenton.

 

     2.    An admission fee for entrance to the Old Barracks at Trenton shall not be charged to any resident of New Jersey.

 

     3.    Upon the effective date of this act, P.L.    , c.     (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), there is appropriated from the General Fund to the Old Barracks at Trenton the sum of $50,000 to waive the admission fee for entrance into the Old Barracks at Trenton for New Jersey residents.  For each fiscal year thereafter, there shall be appropriated from the General Fund to the Old Barracks at Trenton a sum based on the price of admission charged as of the effective date of this act, which shall be adjusted for inflation each fiscal year thereafter, times the number of visitors from New Jersey during the previous fiscal year, plus an additional 10 percent above that sum.

 

     4.    This act shall take effect immediately.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill requires that free admission to the Old Barracks at Trenton be provided to residents of New Jersey (NJ).

     The United States was founded 250 years ago when the Second Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776.  NJ played a central role in the Revolutionary War and the founding of the United States.  Specifically, the Old Barracks at Trenton was used for a variety of purposes during the Revolutionary War by both the British troops and the American patriots, including a holding place for prisoners of war and an army hospital for the Continental Army.

     Through the dissemination of national and local history, people can strengthen their civic identity, community engagement, and historical understanding.  As American independence enters its 250th year and people across the country and NJ reflect on the pivotal moments and events surrounding the birth of this nation through public celebrations, educational programs, and initiatives, it is appropriate to provide increased access to the rich history of NJ for the residents of this State by offering such residents free admission to the Old Barracks at Trenton.

     This bill provides that the admission fee for entrance to the Old Barracks at Trenton will not be charged to residents of NJ.  The bill appropriates $50,000 for this purpose and provides a formula for funding in future years.
