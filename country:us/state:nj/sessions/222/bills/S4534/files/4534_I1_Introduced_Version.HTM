STATEMENT

 

     This bill reduces business formation fees for certain recognized business structures in the State.  Specifically, the bill stipulates a $25 reduction in business formation fees.

     The Governors Fiscal Year 2027 budget recommendations include the enactment of this bill.  The Department of the Treasury estimates that this proposal will reduce revenues by $4.1 million.
