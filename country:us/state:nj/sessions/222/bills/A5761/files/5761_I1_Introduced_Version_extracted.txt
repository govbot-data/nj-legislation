A5761 ASSEMBLY, No. 5761 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  CODY D. MILLER District 4 (Atlantic, Camden and Gloucester) Assemblywoman  SHANIQUE SPEIGHT District 29 (Essex and Hudson) Assemblywoman  ANNETTE QUIJANO District 20 (Union) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblywoman Bagolie SYNOPSIS Sensory-Inclusive Haircare Act; requires training in providing certain sensory-inclusive cosmetology-hairstyling services to neurodivergent individuals. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning certain sensory-inclusive cosmetology and hairstyling services for neurodivergent individuals and supplementing P.L.1984, c.205 (C.45:5B-1 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    This act shall be known and may be cited as the Sensory-Inclusive Haircare Act. 2.    a.         The board shall establish requirements, as part of the current curriculum provided by schools licensed pursuant to section 6 of P.L.1984, c.205 (C.45:5B-6), to teach and train all students preparing for licensure in cosmetology-hairstyling, beauty culture, or barbering on providing sensory-inclusive services to neurodivergent individuals with a sensory dysfunction or a sensory processing disorder. b.    The training required pursuant to this section shall include a total of six hours of class and subject-related instruction and practical instruction. 3.    The board shall file a notice of intent with respect to the establishment, pursuant to this act, of requirements for curriculum on providing sensory-inclusive cosmetology-hairstyling services to neurodivergent individuals at least 180 days prior to filing a formal rule proposal in accordance with subsection a. of section 4 of the Administrative Procedure Act, P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-4). The notice of intent shall provide for four public hearings to be held by the board to elicit the input of interested parties on the establishment of requirements for the curriculum.  In addition to the requirements of subsection e. of section 4 of the Administrative Procedure Act, P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-4), the board shall allow interested persons to attend each public hearing in-person or virtually. 4.    This act shall take effect immediately, except that section 2 shall take effect on the first day of the thirteenth month next following enactment. STATEMENT This bill requires cosmetology-hairstyling, beauty culture, and barbering students to complete training in providing sensory-inclusive services to neurodivergent individuals. Specifically, the bill requires the New Jersey Board of Cosmetology and Hairstyling to expand the curriculum provided by schools licensed in the State when teaching and training students in cosmetology-hairstyling, beauty culture, and barbering to include six hours of instruction in providing sensory-inclusive services for neurodivergent individuals with a sensory dysfunction or a sensory processing disorder.  The bill additionally requires the board to hold at least four public hearings to elicit the input of interested parties on the establishment of requirements for the curriculum on providing these sensory-inclusive services.
