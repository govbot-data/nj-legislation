STATEMENT

 

     This bill would permit a court to issue a protective order against an unemancipated minor.

     Generally, in situations where a survivor is not eligible to receive a restraining order under the Prevention of Domestic Violence Act of 1991 because they lack the required relationship to the defendant, the Victims Assistance and Survivor Protection Act (VASPA) permits a court to issue a protective order to a survivor of:

-         nonconsensual sexual contact;

-         sexual penetration;

-         lewdness;

-         an attempt at nonconsensual sexual contact, sexual penetration, or lewdness;

-         stalking; or

-         cyber-harassment.

     Currently, a court may not issue a protective order under the VASPA against a defendant unless the defendant is at least 18 years of age or is an emancipated minor.  Instead, an unemancipated minor must be charged with a complaint of delinquency under the New Jersey Code of Youth Justice before a court may issue a protective order pursuant to that statute.

     Under the bill, a survivor of one of the above-listed acts, where the alleged perpetrator is an unemancipated minor, could seek a protective order under the VASPA against the minor, bring a complaint under the New Jersey Code of Youth Justice against the minor, or both.

     The bill would take effect on the first day of the seventh month following enactment.
