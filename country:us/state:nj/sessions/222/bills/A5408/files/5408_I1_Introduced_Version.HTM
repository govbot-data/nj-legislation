     2.    (New section) a. For the purposes of this section:

     "Economic development subsidy" means the provision of an amount of funds to a recipient by or from a State entity, including, but not limited to, the New Jersey Economic Development Authority and the Board of Public Utilities, with a value of greater than $25,000 for the purpose of stimulating economic development in New Jersey, including, but not limited to, any investment, bond, grant, loan, loan guarantee, matching fund, tax credit, or other tax expenditure.  "Economic development subsidy" does not include any contract under which a State public body purchases or otherwise procures goods, services, or construction on an unsubsidized basis, including any contract solely for the construction or renovation of a facility owned by a State public body.

     "Hackensack meadowlands" means the same as that term is defined in subsection h. of section 3 of P.L.1971, c.137 (C.5:10-3).

     "Meadowlands sports and entertainment project" means a project for development within the Hackensack meadowlands, for which project the developer seeks an economic development subsidy, and which project is to be used for live sports or entertainment performances, games, contests, or other events, or is to be used as a convention center facility.

     b.    Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, before any State entity may award an economic development subsidy for a Meadowlands sports and entertainment project, the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority shall, in consultation with the Department of Community Affairs, commission a comprehensive impact study and publish a written report assessing the anticipated effects of the Meadowlands sports and entertainment project on the economic viability and competitiveness of the Atlantic City convention center project, constructed pursuant to paragraph (9) of subsection a. of section 6 of P.L.1971, c.137 (C.5:10-6), including, but not limited to, impacts related to tourism, job creation and retention, and regional competitiveness with respect to other states.

     c.     For any Meadowlands sports and entertainment project for which an application is pending approval by a State entity on the effective date of P.L.    , c.     (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), the authority shall publish the report required pursuant to subsection b. of this section within 180 days after the effective date of P.L.    , c.     (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), or prior to the approval of the application, whichever occurs earlier.

     d.    Upon the completion of each report required pursuant to this section, the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority shall submit to the Governor and, pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), to the Legislature, the written report on any findings of the comprehensive impact report.  The New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority shall provide a copy of the written report to the New Jersey Economic Development Authority.

 

     3.    (New section) a. The New Jersey Economic Development Authority may, in consultation with the Casino Reinvestment Development Authority and the Atlantic County Improvement Authority, provide financial assistance for capital projects that improve or modernize the Atlantic City convention center project, as defined pursuant to paragraph (9) of subsection a. of section 6 of P.L.1971, c.137 (C.5:10-6).  The cumulative value of the financial assistance provided pursuant to this section shall not exceed $50,000,000, subject to appropriation by the Legislature.  To receive financial assistance, a developer shall demonstrate to the satisfaction of the New Jersey Economic Development Authority that the improvement or modernization will preserve or enhance the competitiveness, functionality, and visitor experience of the Atlantic City Convention Center and contribute to the growth of tourism and economic development of the local community.

     b.    Prior to the issuance of any financial assistance pursuant to this section, the developer shall evaluate the need, order of priority, approximate cost, and funding strategies for any of the authorized expenses set forth in subsection c. of this section and shall report its findings to the authority in such form and manner as the New Jersey Economic Development Authority may require.

     c.     The financial assistance authorized pursuant to this section may include a grant, loan, or loan guarantee to support any costs associated with the following expenses:

     (1)   the repair or replacement of floors, carpeting, ceilings, entrances, or interior finishing, such as wall paint;

     (2)   any exterior paving, roadway repair, painting, or infrastructure improvements;

     (3)   any improvements to accessibility features, including, but not limited to, building access;

     (4)   the installation or improvement of energy-efficient technology, including, but not limited to, energy-efficient Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design (LEED) lighting systems;

     (5)   the improvement of wayfinding systems, such as signage;

     (6)   the implementation of a comprehensive preventive maintenance program;

     (7)   the installation of aesthetic enhancements, such as plants or artwork; and

     (8)   any other improvement or modernization necessary to the long-term viability of the Atlantic City Convention Center.

 

     4.    This act shall take effect immediately.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill authorizes the New Jersey Economic Development Authority to provide financial assistance for capital projects that improve or modernize the Atlantic City Convention Center, subject to appropriation.  The bill also requires a comprehensive impact study, and a written report on any findings, of the anticipated effects of a Meadowlands sports and entertainment project prior to the approval of such project.

     The bill provides that before any State entity may award an economic development subsidy for a Meadowlands sports and entertainment project, the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority, in consultation with the Department of Community Affairs, is required to commission a comprehensive impact study and publish a written report assessing the anticipated effects of the project on the economic viability and competitiveness of the Atlantic City Convention Center.  The bill defines "Meadowlands sports and entertainment project" as a project for development within the Hackensack meadowlands, for which project the developer seeks an economic development subsidy, and which project is to be used for live sports or entertainment performances, games, contests, or other events, or is to be used as a convention center facility.

     Prior to the approval of an economic development subsidy for any Meadowlands sports and entertainment project, this bill requires the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority to submit the written report on any findings of the comprehensive impact report to the Governor and the Legislature.  The bill also requires the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority to provide a copy of the written report to the New Jersey Economic Development Authority.

     This bill also authorizes the New Jersey Economic Development Authority, in consultation with the Casino Reinvestment Development Authority and the Atlantic County Improvement Authority, to provide financial assistance for capital projects that improve or modernize the Atlantic City Convention Center.  The maximum cumulative value of financial assistance under the bill may not exceed $50 million, subject to appropriation by the Legislature.  To receive financial assistance, a developer is required to demonstrate to the satisfaction of the authority that the improvement or modernization will preserve or enhance the competitiveness, functionality, and visitor experience of the Atlantic City Convention Center and contribute to the growth of tourism and economic development of the local community.

     Under the bill, financial assistance may include a grant, loan, or loan guarantee to support costs associated with:  (1) the repair or replacement of floors, carpeting, ceilings, entrances, or interior finishing, such as wall paint; (2) any exterior paving, roadway repair, painting, or infrastructure improvements; (3) any improvements to accessibility features, including, but not limited to, building access; (4) the installation or improvement of energy-efficient technology, including, but not limited to, energy-efficient LED lighting systems; (5) the improvement of wayfinding systems, such as signage; (6) the implementation of a comprehensive preventive maintenance program; (7) the installation of aesthetic enhancements, such as plants or artwork; and (8) any other improvement or modernization necessary to the long-term viability of the Atlantic City Convention Center.
