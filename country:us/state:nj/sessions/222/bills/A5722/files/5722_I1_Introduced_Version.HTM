SYNOPSIS

     Requires hotels and harm reduction service providers to cooperate in providing opioid antidotes and harm reduction supplies at hotels.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning opioid harm reduction and overdose prevention at hotels, and amending various parts of statutory law.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill designates hotels as recognized places of public access for the purpose of being required to maintain opioid antidotes and harm reduction supplies on-site, together with required staff training on administering opioid antidotes.  Under the bill, hotels may enter into agreements with registered harm reduction services providers to fulfill these obligations.
