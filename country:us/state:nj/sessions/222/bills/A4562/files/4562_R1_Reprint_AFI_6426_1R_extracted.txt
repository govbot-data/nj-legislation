A4562 1R [First Reprint] ASSEMBLY, No. 4562 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED MARCH 10, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  ROSAURA "ROSY" BAGOLIE District 27 (Essex and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Requires automobile insurance carriers offer policies with 12-month term. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Assembly Financial Institutions and Insurance Committee on June 4, 2026, with amendments. An Act concerning automobile insurance carriers and supplementing P.L.1972, c.70 (C.39-6A-1 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    An automobile insurer that offers a standard or basic automobile insurance policy shall provide the option to purchase the policy with a 12-month term.  This requirement shall not be construed to restrict the insurer's ability to offer insurance policies with shorter or longer terms. 2.    This act shall take effect 1[120 days] one year1 after the date of enactment.
