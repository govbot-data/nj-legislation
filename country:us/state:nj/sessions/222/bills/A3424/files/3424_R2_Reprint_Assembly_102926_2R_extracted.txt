A3424 2R [Second Reprint] ASSEMBLY, No. 3424 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE PRE-FILED FOR INTRODUCTION IN THE 2026 SESSION Sponsored by: Assemblywoman HEATHER SIMMONS District 3 (Cumberland, Gloucester and Salem) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblyman Schnall SYNOPSIS Grants priority course registration to certain student parents attending institutions of higher education. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As amended by the General Assembly on October 29, 2026. An Act concerning priority registration at institutions of higher education and supplementing chapter 62 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1. a. 1[A] An undergraduate1 student enrolled in an institution of higher education that provides priority registration for 1any other undergraduate1 students shall be granted priority in registering for courses at the institution provided that the student is: (1)  the parent or legal guardian of a child who is under 18 years of age; and (2)  a resident of the State. b.  Priority course registration shall occur using the same procedures used by other segments of the student population that are offered priority registration by the institution. c. Prior to the registration period for each academic semester, an institution shall provide notice to students who may be eligible for priority registration pursuant to this section. 2d. Within six months of the date of enactment of this act, the Secretary of Higher Education shall issue guidelines and criteria for verifying that a student qualifies for priority registration pursuant to this section. e.  The provisions of this section shall comply with all applicable federal and State anti-discrimination and privacy laws.2 2.  This act shall take effect immediately.
