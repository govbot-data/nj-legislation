SYNOPSIS

     Establishes additional penalties for crimes and offenses committed in or near designated transit facilities.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning additional penalties for certain offenses committed on or near designated transit facilities and amending and supplementing Title 2C of the New Jersey Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1. (New section) As used in this act:

     Designated transit facility means a passenger rail station, train station, bus terminal, bus rapid transit station, light rail station, ferry terminal, or other major transportation facility designated pursuant to section 3 of this act.

     Transit-related activity means activity occurring on, at, or in connection with the operation, use, or provision of public transportation services, including activity involving a transit passenger, transit employee, transit vehicle, transit property, transit equipment, or transit service.

     Transit safety zone means the area located on or within 500 feet of the boundary of a designated transit facility.

 

     2. (New section) a.  In addition to any penalty provided by law, a person who commits a crime enumerated in subsection b. of this section if committed in a transit safety zone shall be subject to the following:

     (1) a person convicted of a qualifying offense that constitutes a crime of the first or second degree shall be subject to an additional fine of not less than $5,000 and not more than $25,000.

     (2) a person convicted of a qualifying offense that constitutes a crime of the third degree shall be subject to an additional fine of not less than $2,500 and not more than $10,000.

     (3) a person convicted of a qualifying offense that constitutes a crime of the fourth degree shall be subject to an additional fine of not less than $1,000 and not more than $5,000.

     (4) person convicted of a qualifying offense that constitutes a disorderly persons offense or petty disorderly persons offense shall be subject to an additional fine of not less than $500 and not more than $1,000.

     b.    The additional penalty pursuant to this section shall be imposed on a person who commits one of the following crimes or offenses within a transit safety zone and in connection with a designated transit facility or transit-related activity:

     (1) assault or aggravated assault in violation of N.J.S.2C:12-1;

     (2) terroristic threats in violation of N.J.S.2C:12-3;

     (3) lewdness in violation of N.J.S.2C:14-4;

     (4) robbery in violation of N.J.S.2C:15-1;

     (5) criminal mischief in violation of N.J.S.2C:17-3;

     (6) burglary in violation of N.J.S.2C:18-2;

     (7) criminal trespass in violation of N.J.S.2C:18-3;

     (8) theft as set forth in chapter 20 of Title 2C of the New Jersey Statutes;

     (9) shoplifting in violation of N.J.S.2C:20-11;

     (10) obstruction of the administration of law or other governmental function in violation of N.J.S.2C:29-1;

     (11) resisting arrest or eluding in violation of N.J.S.2C:29-2;

     (12) disorderly conduct in violation of N.J.S.2C:33-2;

     (13) harassment in violation of N.J.S.2C:33-4;

     (14) manufacturing, distributing, or dispensing, or possessing or having under control with intent to manufacture, distribute, or dispense, a controlled dangerous substance or controlled substance analog in violation of N.J.S.2C:35-5;

     (15) unlawful possession or use of a controlled dangerous substance or controlled substance analog in violation of N.J.S.2C:35-10;

     (16) possession or use of drug paraphernalia in violation of N.J.S.2C:36-2, when applicable;

     (17) possession of a weapon for an unlawful purpose in violation of N.J.S.2C:39-4;

     (18) unlawful possession of a weapon in violation of N.J.S.2C:39-5;

     (19) possession of a weapon while committing a controlled dangerous substance offense in violation of section 1 of P.L.1998, c.26 (C.2C:39-4.1);

     (20) possession of a weapon by a person prohibited from possessing a weapon in violation of section 6 of P.L.1979, c.179 (C.2C:39-7); and

     (21) any other crime or offense designated by the Commissioner of Transportation pursuant to rules and regulations adopted pursuant to section 5 of this act, when the Attorney General determines that the offense presents a substantial threat to the safety of persons using or working at a designated transit facility.

     c.  The additional penalty imposed pursuant to this section shall be in addition to, and shall not replace, any fine, term of imprisonment, period of probation, community service, assessment, or other penalty authorized or imposed pursuant to the underlying offense.

     d.  The court may waive or reduce the additional fine imposed pursuant to this section upon a finding on the record that imposition of the minimum additional fine would result in a substantial injustice.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill establishes Transit Safety Zones around designated major transit facilities in New Jersey and establishes additional penalties for certain criminal offenses committed within those zones and in connection with a designated transit facility or transit-related activity.  The bill defines a Transit Safety Zone as the area located on or within 500 feet of a designated transit facility.

     The bill designates major transportation facilities, including Newark Penn Station, Hoboken Terminal, Secaucus Junction, Trenton Transit Center, Metropark Station, Newark Broad Street Station, Elizabeth Station, New Brunswick Station, Princeton Junction Station, Hamilton Station, Atlantic City Rail Terminal, Journal Square Transportation Center, Exchange Place Station, and other specified transit facilities as designated transit facilities.  The Commissioner of Transportation may designate additional transit facilities based on factors including passenger volume, transit services, transfer activity, geographic location, and documented public safety concerns.

     Under the bill, certain drug, weapons, assault, robbery, burglary, theft, shoplifting, criminal mischief, terroristic threats, disorderly conduct, lewdness, harassment, trespass, obstruction, and resisting arrest or eluding offenses committed within a Transit Safety Zone constitute qualifying offenses.

     A person convicted of an enumerated offense within a Transit Safety Zone would be subject to an additional penalty in addition to the penalties imposed under current law.  The additional fine would range from $500 to $25,000, depending on the degree of the offense.  The bill permits a court to waive or reduce the additional fine upon finding that imposing the minimum fine would result in a substantial injustice.

     The bill directs the Commissioner of Transportation to maintain an official list and map of designated transit facilities and their corresponding 500-foot Transit Safety Zones and authorizes the commissioner to adopt rules and directives necessary to implement the bill.

     This bill also amends current law concerning places where the possession of a firearm or destructive device is prohibited to include designated transit facilities, in addition to airports and transportation hubs currently covered under the law.
