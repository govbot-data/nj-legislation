AJR200 ASSEMBLY JOINT RESOLUTION No. 200 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  SEAN T. KEAN District 30 (Monmouth and Ocean) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblywomen Flynn and Fantasia SYNOPSIS Urges Congress and US President to enact Pray Safe Act." CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. A Joint Resolution respectfully urging Congress and the President of the United States to enact the Pray Safe Act. Whereas, Nationwide we are witnessing an increase in religious and political violence that is becoming a normal part of daily existence; and Whereas, The Violence Prevention Project Research Center (VPP Research Center) has recorded 399 homicide incidents resulting in 512 homicides in the United States at a Christian, Jewish, and Islamic house of worship between calendar years 2000 and 2025; and Whereas, Houses of worship and faith-based institutions are cornerstones of their communities, serving as gathering places, schools and childcare centers, and providers of critical social services that offer much-needed assistance to the communities they serve; and Whereas, Every American deserves to be safe and should be able to pray without fear for their safety; and Whereas, A single attack can reshape congregational life, suppress attendance, and generate fear across an entire faith community; and Whereas, The VPP Research Center indicated that nearly half of active shooter incidents involved ideological motivation and indicate that prevention efforts should focus on identifying escalations in grievance, fixation, and threat communication, which are often expressed before such attacks through harassment, disruption of services, or online signaling aimed at the group; and Whereas, Research indicates the most effective approach to supporting security at houses of worship is not to physically fortify every house of worship, but instead, to combine early identification, coordinated intervention, and low-cost preparedness in order to preserve openness; and Whereas, Members of the United States Senate and House of Representatives introduced the Pray Safe Act, Senate Bill No. 2947 and House of Representatives Bill No. 5645 respectively, to authorize the creation of a single resource center inside the United States Department of Homeland Security to provide safety and security best practices; and Whereas, This bipartisan, bicameral legislation will help connect houses of worship, faith-based organizations, and other nonprofits with information on safety and security best practices including available federal security grant programs; and Whereas, Specifically, this bill establishes a federal clearinghouse on safety and best practices for nonprofit organizations, faith-based organizations, and houses of worship within the United States Department of Homeland Security; and Whereas, Congress is respectfully urged to pass, and the President is urged to sign, the Pray Safe Act to support and protect the right of the citizens of our great country in practicing their faith; now, therefore, Be It Resolved by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    The State of New Jersey respectfully urges Congress and the President of the United States to enact Senate Bill No. 2947 and House of Representatives Bill No. 5645, the Pray Safe Act. 2.    Copies of this resolution, as filed with the Secretary of State, shall be transmitted by the Clerk of the General Assembly or the Secretary of the Senate to the President of the United States, the Speaker and the Minority Leader of the United States House of Representatives, the Majority and Minority Leaders of the United States Senate, and each member of Congress elected from this State. 3.    This joint resolution shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This joint resolution urges Congress to pass, and the President of the United States to sign, the Pray Safe Act, which is pending before the United States Senate as Senate Bill No. 2947 and the House of Representatives as House Bill No. 5645. Specifically, S.2947 and H.R.5645 authorize the creation of a single resource center inside the United States Department of Homeland Security to provide safety and security best practices for nonprofit organizations, faith-based organizations, and houses of worship. The joint resolution urges Congress and the President of the United States to move forward with the enactment of S.2947 and H.R.5645 to support and protect the right of the citizens of our great country in practicing their faith.
