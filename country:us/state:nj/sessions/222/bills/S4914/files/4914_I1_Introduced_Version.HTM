SYNOPSIS

     Requires public body to make minutes of each meeting available to public within 30 days of meeting.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning the availability of public body meeting minutes and amending P.L.1975, c.231.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    Section 9 of P.L.1975, c.231 (C.10:4-14) is amended to read as follows:

     9.    Each public body shall keep reasonably comprehensible minutes of all its meetings showing the time and place, the members present, the subjects considered, the actions taken, the vote of each member, and any other information required to be shown in the minutes by law, which shall be promptly made available to the public, but not later than 30 days after the meeting, to the extent that making such matters public shall not be inconsistent with section 7 of [this act] P.L.1975, c.231 (C.10:4-12).

(cf: P.L.1975, c.231, s.9)

 

     2.    This act shall take effect immediately.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     Under current law, each public body is required to keep reasonably comprehensible minutes of all its meetings and to make those minutes promptly available to the public.  This bill amends the law to specify that the minutes of each meeting are to be made available to the public not later than 30 days after each meeting.
