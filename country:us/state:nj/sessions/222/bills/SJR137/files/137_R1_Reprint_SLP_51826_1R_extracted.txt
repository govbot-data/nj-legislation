SJR137 1R [First Reprint] SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION No. 137 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED MAY 11, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  LATHAM TIVER District 8 (Atlantic and Burlington) Senator  TROY SINGLETON District 7 (Burlington) Assemblyman  BALVIR SINGH District 7 (Burlington) Assemblyman  ANTHONY ANGELOZZI District 8 (Atlantic and Burlington) Assemblywoman  CAROL A. MURPHY District 7 (Burlington) Co-Sponsored by: Senators Henry, Moriarty, Polistina, Amato, Holzapfel, Testa, McKnight, Greenstein, Assemblyman Hutchison, Assemblywoman Katz, Assemblymen Sauickie, Guardian, Miller, Moen, McClellan and Assemblywoman Park SYNOPSIS Designates June 10 of each year as Christina Grimmie Day in NJ. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Senate Law and Public Safety Committee on May 18, 2026, with amendments. A Joint Resolution designating June 10 1[, 2026] of each year1 as Christina Grimmie Day in New Jersey. Whereas, Christina Grimmie was a proud New Jersey native who grew up in Marlton, New Jersey and rose to national prominence as a talented singer and songwriter; and Whereas, Christina began her journey with music at a young age and loved to sing and write music; and Whereas, Christina first built a devoted following in the early days of YouTube by sharing her music online, eventually amassing hundreds of millions of views and a global fan base; and Whereas, Christinas accomplishments also included successfully releasing her first independent extended play, Find Me, which debuted at number two on iTunes; amassing almost 3.5 million YouTube subscribers; finishing top three on season six of NBC's The Voice; performing on multiple television shows including The Ellen Show and Dancing with the Stars; having multiple album and single releases in the top 10 on iTunes; and winning an American Music Award in 2011 for New Media: Best Female, among many others; and Whereas, Tragically, Christinas life was taken by a gunman following a concert in Orlando, Florida in June 2016; and Whereas, In the wake of this tragedy, Christinas family established the Christina Grimmie Foundation to support individuals and families facing similar unexpected hardships and crises, specifically families who have lost a loved one to gun violence; and Whereas, Christinas artistry, voice, and connection with her audience continue to resonate with fans around the world; and Whereas, Christinas music and performances remain widely celebrated and continue to inspire new generations; and Whereas, June 2026 will mark the 10-year anniversary of Christinas passing; and Whereas, It is altogether fitting and proper to designate June 10 1[,  2026] of each year1 as Christina Grimmie Day to recognize Christinas lasting cultural impact, honor her legacy as a New Jersey artist, and promote awareness of efforts to support victims of unexpected tragedies; now, therefore, Be It Resolved by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    June 10 1[, 2026] of each year1 is designated as Christina Grimmie Day in New Jersey to recognize Christinas lasting cultural impact, honor her legacy as a New Jersey artist, and promote awareness of efforts to support victims of unexpected tragedies. 2.    The Governor is respectfully requested to issue a proclamation recognizing June 10 1[, 2026] of each year1 as Christina Grimmie Day in New Jersey. 3.    This joint resolution shall take effect immediately.
