SYNOPSIS

     Prohibits contracts for student-athletes name, image, or likeness from remaining in effect beyond the date the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning student-athletes and supplementing P.L.2025, c.110 (C.18A:3B-89.2 et seq.).

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    a.  Notwithstanding any other law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, any contract into which a student-athlete enters providing compensation to the student-athlete for use of the student-athletes name, image, or likeness pursuant to section 2 of P.L.2025, c.110 (C.18A:3B-89.3) shall not:

     (1)   remain in effect beyond the date the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics; or

     (2)  require the student-athlete to provide as consideration either of the following:

     (a) any compensation the student-athlete earns after the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics for use of the student-athlete's name, image, or likeness; or

     (b)  rights associated with the use of the student-athletes name, image, or likeness after the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics.

     b.  Any provision of a contract in which a student-athlete enters into pursuant to section 2 of P.L.2025, c.110 (C.18A:3B-89.3) that is in violation of this section shall be void.

 

     2.    This act shall take effect immediately.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill prohibits any contract into which a student-athlete enters, providing compensation for use of the student-athletes name, image, or likeness from:

·        remaining in effect beyond the date the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics; or

·        requiring the student-athlete to provide as consideration either of the following: any compensation the student-athlete earns after the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics for use of the student-athletes name, image, or likeness; or rights associated with the use of the student-athletes name, image, or likeness after the student-athlete is no longer eligible to participate in intercollegiate athletics.

·        The bill stipulates that any provision of a contract in violation of the provisions of the bill would be void.
