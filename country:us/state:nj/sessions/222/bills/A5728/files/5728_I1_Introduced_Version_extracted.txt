STATEMENT This bill requires the establishment of an automatic voter registration process similar to that currently applicable at the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) to be available for persons applying at local police forces or the Division of State Police in the Department of Law and Public Safety for permits to carry and conceal handguns. Under current law, a person applying for a concealed carry permit must submit the application to the applicants local police force, or, in certain cases, to the New Jersey State Police.  Under the bill, the New Jersey State Police and certain police forces would be required to establish and implement, upon approval by the Secretary of State, a procedure for automatically and electronically transmitting voter registration information to the Secretary of State when a person submits an application for a concealed carry permit.  The Secretary of State would be required to verify that the law enforcement agency already collects documents that would provide proof of eligibility for voter registration, including age, citizenship, and residence address.  When submitting a concealed carry permit, a person would be given the option to register to vote, update their voter registration, or decline automatic voter registration.  The bill requires affected law enforcement agencies to comply with the automatic voter registration requirements established under current law for the MVC, including providing required notices, to the extent applicable, as determined by the Secretary of State.
