STATEMENT This bill amends P.L.2021, c.171 (C.40:55D-66.19) with the intent to streamline and clarify the approval process for installation of electric vehicle supply equipment (EVSE) and Make-Ready parking spaces at existing gasoline service stations, retail establishments, and other existing buildings, while preserving appropriate public health and safety protections and maintaining applicable inspection requirements. The bill provides that qualifying applications for the installation of EVSE are to be approved administratively through issuance of a non-discretionary permit in a ministerial permitting process, provided the proposed installation complies with the "State Uniform Construction Code Act," P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-119 et seq.), applicable safety standards adopted by State law, rule, or regulation, and any other law, rule, or regulation concerning EVSE.  By clarifying that these approvals are ministerial in nature, the bills intent is to promote uniform consistent, predictable, and timely permitting practices across the State. The bill removes certain approval requirements from existing law that have resulted in unnecessary delays, including requirements related to bulk zoning standards and prior site plan conditions that are unrelated to public health or safety. The bill expressly maintains all applicable local and Department of Community Affairs inspection requirements to ensure continued compliance with construction, fire, and safety standards. The bill provides that an application for the installation of EVSE or Make-Ready parking spaces that does not qualify for approval pursuant to subsection a. of the bill is not to be required to obtain variance relief, provided that the application is otherwise eligible for administrative approval, complies with the "State Uniform Construction Code Act," P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-119 et seq.), and applicable safety standards adopted by State law, rule, or regulation.  These applications are to be reviewed administratively and are not to be subject to site plan or other land use board review. The bill further provides that local review of development applications is limited to specific, identifiable public health or safety impacts supported by substantial evidence in the record, consistent with the limited, administrative nature of the approval process. The bill also provides that EVSE infrastructure that encroaches upon required setbacks established by a local government entity is not to constitute an adverse impact upon public health or safety.  The bill preserves essential safety oversight by clarifying that EVSE is not to obstruct required sight triangles, fire lanes, or emergency access, as determined in accordance with applicable safety standards adopted by State law, rule, or regulation. By reducing unnecessary permitting barriers while maintaining essential safety protections, this bill supports expanded access to electric vehicle charging for residents, facilitates private investment by businesses, promotes economic activity, and advances New Jerseys clean transportation and environmental goals in a manner that is efficient, predictable, and responsive to the needs of communities throughout the State.
