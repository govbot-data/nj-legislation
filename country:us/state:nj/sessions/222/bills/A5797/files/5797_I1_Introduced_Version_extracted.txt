A5797 ASSEMBLY, No. 5797 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  STERLEY S. STANLEY District 18 (Middlesex) SYNOPSIS Requires DEP to establish online reporting system for schools and child care centers to report test results concerning lead in drinking water. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning lead in drinking water and supplementing Title 58 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.  a.  No later than one year after the effective date of this section, the Department of Environmental Protection shall establish an online reporting system that allows a school or child care center to submit the results of any tests the school or child care center has conducted for lead in drinking water, and any other related information, to the department electronically. b.  No later than 90 days after the establishment of the online reporting system pursuant to subsection a. of this section, any school that is required by law, rule, or regulation, on or after July 1, 2016, to test its drinking water for lead shall submit or resubmit its test results and other required information to the department via the online reporting system.  The department shall compile the test results and other relevant information on its Internet website for access by the public in an easily-searchable format. c.  No later than one year after the effective date of this section, the Department of Children and Families shall submit the results of any tests it has required for lead in drinking water on or after January 1, 2017, to the Department of Environmental Protection, for updates to the drinking water data system established pursuant to subsection a. of this section. d.  No later than 90 days after the establishment of the online reporting system pursuant to subsection a. of this section, any child care center that is required by law, rule, or regulation, on or after January 1, 2017, to test its drinking water for lead shall submit or resubmit its test results and any other required information to the Department of Environmental Protection via the online reporting system.  The Department of Environmental Protection shall compile the test results and other relevant information on its Internet website for access by the public in an easily-searchable format. e.  No later than two years after the effective date of this section, the Department of Environmental Protection, in collaboration with the Department of Education and the Department of Children and Families, shall prepare and submit a report to the Governor and, pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), the Legislature.  The Department of Environmental Protection, the Department of Education, and the Department of Children and Families shall post the report on their respective Internet websites.  The report shall provide information about: (1) the extent of lead contamination in the drinking water of schools and child care centers; (2) the distribution of lead test results throughout the State, as depicted in a potential lead exposure mapping tool; (3) available assistance regarding remediation activities; and (4) recommendations for how the State could assist schools and child care centers with remediating lead in drinking water. 2.  This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill would require the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) to establish an online reporting system that allows schools and child care centers to submit test results concerning lead in drinking water to the DEP electronically. The bill would require the DEP to compile the information it receives on its website for access by the public in an easily-searchable format.  The bill would also require schools, child care centers, and the Department of Children and Families to forward future test results and certain existing test results to the DEP, in order to be added to the online database.  Finally, the bill would require the DEP to submit a report to the Governor and Legislature two years after the bills enactment, which contains certain specified information concerning lead in drinking water at schools and child care centers.
