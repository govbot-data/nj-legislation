A5779 ASSEMBLY, No. 5779 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  ALIXON COLLAZOS-GILL District 27 (Essex and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Requires county welfare agencies to direct clients to, and promote use of, Department of Treasurys personal finance web-based platform. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning county welfare agencies and supplementing Title 30 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.   The Commissioner of Human Services shall require each county welfare agency located in the State to: a.   direct clients to, and promote the use of, the Department of the Treasurys free personal finance web-based platform, or any subsequent platform provided by that department or the State; and b.   promote the availability of financial planning services provided at the agency, if offered, as well as the availability of the Department of the Treasurys free personal finance web-based platform or any subsequent platform provided by that department or the State, on the agencys website and in the agencys physical office. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill directs the Commissioner of Human Services to require each county welfare agency located in the State to: 1) direct clients to the Department of the Treasurys free personal finance web-based platform, or any subsequent platform provided by that department or the State; and 2) promote on the agencys website and in the agencys physical office the availability of financial planning services provided at the agency, if offered, as well as the availability of the Department of the Treasurys free personal finance web-based platform or any subsequent platform provided by that department or the State.
