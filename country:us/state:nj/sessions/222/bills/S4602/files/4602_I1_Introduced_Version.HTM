SYNOPSIS

     Authorizes long-term public contracts of up to 25 years for provision of electricity and related products for energy storage systems.

STATEMENT

 

     Under the "Local Public Contracts Law," P.L.1971, c.198 (C.40A:11-1 et seq.), all contracts for the provision or performance of goods or services are awarded for a period not to exceed 24 consecutive months.  However, the law specifies certain contracts which may be awarded for longer periods of time.  This bill amends the law to provide that the provision of electricity and electricity-related products and services related to an energy storage system, as specified in the bill, through an agreement is authorized for a term not to exceed 25 years, and provides that such agreements are not required to contain certain clauses specified for inclusion in other agreements.

     The bill also makes technical corrections to existing law.
