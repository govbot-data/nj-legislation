STATEMENT This bill repeals the recent enactment concerning patients access to, and medical providers provision of,  reproductive health care services, P.L.2026, c.74. The enactment, among other provisions: -- created the crime of interference with reproductive health care services and authorized a civil suit for interference with reproductive health care services; -- barred public entities from expending time or resources in further of interstate proceedings seeking to impose liability for the provision, receipt, or seeking of reproductive health care services that are legal in this State; and -- expanded the existing ban on extradition from this State of a person charged in another state with providing or receiving reproductive health care services that are legal in this State. P.L.2026, c.74 took effect August 20, 2026. Under this bill, the repeal of P.L.2026, c.74  would take effect immediately.
