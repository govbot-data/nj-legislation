A5652 ASSEMBLY, No. 5652 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED OCTOBER 19, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  LARRY WAINSTEIN District 33 (Hudson) SYNOPSIS Requires local construction official sit on municipal planning board, zoning board, and redevelopment agency board. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning membership of various municipal boards and amending P.L.1975, c.291 and P.L.1992, c.79. Class II--one of the officials of the municipality other than a member of the governing body, to be appointed by the mayor; provided that if there be an environmental commission, the member of the environmental commission who is also a member of the planning board as required by section 1 of P.L.1968, c.245 (C.40:56A-1), shall be deemed to be the Class II planning board member for purposes of [this act] the "Municipal Land Use Law," P.L.1975, c.291 (C.40:55D-1 et seq.) in the event that there be among the Class IV or alternate members of the planning board both a member of the zoning board of adjustment and a member of the board of education. STATEMENT This bill requires that the local construction official or designee sit on a municipalitys planning board, zoning board, and redevelopment agency board. Under current State law, a municipality is required to appoint a group of seven or nine members from four classes to sit on the planning board.  The bill adds a fifth class, which provides the local construction official or its designee is to be included on the planning board.  The bill does not require the local construction official be a municipal resident, unlike as is required of other classes of planning board members. A municipal zoning board is currently required to consist of seven members with not more than four alternate members very varying terms.  Each regular member and alternate member is required to be a municipal resident.  The bill requires that the local construction official be one of seven regular members of the zoning board.  The bill does not require the local construction official to be a municipal resident as is required of the remaining zoning board members. Current State law provides that a municipality is permitted, by ordinance, to create a municipal redevelopment agency that consists of seven commissioners to serve varying terms.  The bill requires the local construction official or its designee to be one of the seven commissioners. The bill provides that, regardless of the board or agency, the local construction official is to serve for the duration of their tenure as the construction official, or if the member is the local construction officials designee, to serve at the pleasure of the local construction official. The bill takes effect immediately except that the municipality may appoint the local construction official to the planning board, zoning board of adjustment, or redevelopment agency upon the expiration of a current term of a current member or commissioner.
