STATEMENT

 

     This bill would amend P.L.2020, c.92, commonly referred to as the Environmental Justice Law, to require the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) to evaluate the environmental and public health impacts of new data centers on local communities when reviewing permit applications.  Beginning one year after the bills effective date, the DEP would not consider complete for review any application for a permit for a new data center unless the permit applicant meets certain conditions enumerated in the Environmental Justice Law, including developing an environmental justice impact statement and holding a public hearing.  Additionally, the DEP would be authorized to deny a permit application for a new data center, regardless of whether or not the new data center will be located in an overburdened community, upon a finding that approval of the permit application would, result in a disproportionate impact to the community when compared to the impact and risk born by other communities in the State.
