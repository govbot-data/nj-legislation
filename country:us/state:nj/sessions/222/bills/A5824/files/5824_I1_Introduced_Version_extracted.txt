A5824 ASSEMBLY, No. 5824 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  TENNILLE R. MCCOY District 14 (Mercer and Middlesex) Assemblyman  BALVIR SINGH District 7 (Burlington) SYNOPSIS Requires school districts to provide at least 30 minute seated lunch period for students. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning lunch periods for students and supplementing chapter 33 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  Beginning with the first full school year next following the date of enactment of this act, a school district shall provide on each full school day a lunch period of at least 30 seated minutes for each student enrolled in the district. b.  The daily lunch period required pursuant to this section shall not be combined with the daily recess period required pursuant to section 1 of P.L.2018, c.73 (C.18A:35-4.31). c.  The provisions of this section shall not apply to students enrolled in a half-day program. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires school districts to provide on each full school day, a lunch period of at least 30 seated minutes for all students enrolled in the district, other than students enrolled in a half-day program. Under the bill, school districts would not be permitted to combine the daily lunch period requirement with the daily recess period of 20 minutes required under current law for students in grades kindergarten through five.
