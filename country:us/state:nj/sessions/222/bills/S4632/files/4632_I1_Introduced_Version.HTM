SYNOPSIS

     Provides certain TPAF members and certain retirees same benefits provided to members enrolled in retirement system within period starting after November 1, 2008, and ending on or before May 21, 2010.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning the benefits provided to members and certain retirees of the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund, amending various parts of the statutory law, and supplementing N.J.S.18A:66-1 et seq.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.  (New section)  a.  (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of P.L.2008, c.89; P.L.2010, c.1; P.L.2010, c.3; P.L.2011, c.78; or any other law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, all current, non-retired members of the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund enrolled in the system after May 21, 2010 shall be entitled to and shall receive the same benefits received by members of the retirement system who enrolled in the system within the period starting after November 1, 2008, and ending on or before May 21, 2010, including, but not limited to, service retirement, deferred retirement, early retirement, veterans retirement, ordinary disability retirement, and accidental disability retirement benefits. 

     (2) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, all new members of the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund who enroll in the system on or after the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), shall be entitled to and shall receive the same benefits received by members of the retirement system who enrolled in the system within the period starting after November 1, 2008, and ending on or before May 21, 2010, including, but not limited to, service retirement, deferred retirement, early retirement, veterans retirement, ordinary disability retirement, and accidental disability retirement benefits.

     (3) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, a member of the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund who enrolled in the system after May 21, 2010, whose application for retirement has been approved on or after the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), shall be entitled to and shall receive the same retirement benefits received by members who enrolled in the system within the period starting after November 1, 2008, and ending on or before May 21, 2010.

     b.  Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, any members of the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund receiving long term disability insurance pursuant to section 10 of P.L.2010, c.3 (C.18A:66-39.1), shall be considered eligible to apply for ordinary or accidental disability retirement benefits pursuant to N.J.S.18A:66-1 et seq., eligible to be received by members enrolled in the retirement system within the period starting after November 1, 2008, and ending on or before May 21, 2010.  If the member elects to apply for disability retirement benefits under the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund in accordance with this section, the member shall do so no later than two calendar years following the date of the members eligibility to file an application.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill provides for the restoration of certain benefits to certain members and retirees of the Teachers Pension and Annuity Fund (TPAF) to match benefits provided to members enrolled in the retirement system under Tier 3. 

     Under current law, TPAF members are enrolled into certain pension tiers based on when they became eligible to be enrolled in the pension system.  Tier 1 consists of members who were enrolled before July 1, 2007; Tier 2 consists of members who were enrolled on or after July 1, 2007, and on or before November 1, 2008; Tier 3 consists of members who were enrolled after November 1, 2008, and on or before May 21, 2010; Tier 4 consists of members who were enrolled after May 21, 2010, and before June 28, 2011; and Tier 5 consists of members who were enrolled on or after June 28, 2011.  TPAF member benefits differ depending on which tier the member is enrolled in, with Tier 1 providing the most generous benefits and each subsequent tier offering less generous benefits.

     Under this bill, current TPAF members who are enrolled in Tier 4 and Tier 5, and future TPAF members who would normally be enrolled in Tier 5, will be enrolled into Tier 3.  These members will be entitled to receive all of the benefits associated with that Tier.  These benefits include a decrease in the retirement age for members of Tier 5, a more favorable calculation of pension benefits, and the ability to receive ordinary and accidental disability retirement benefits instead of long term disability insurance.
