A5437 ASSEMBLY, No. 5437 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  LOUIS D. GREENWALD District 6 (Burlington and Camden) SYNOPSIS Amends FY2027 appropriations act to reassign appropriation for Virtua Health College of Medicine and Life Sciences to Virtua Health College for hospital support. STATEMENT The Fiscal Year 2027 appropriations act currently allocates $2 million to Rowan University for the Virtua Health College of Medicine and Life Sciences.  This bill reassigns that appropriation to the Department of Health for Virtua Health College for hospital support without altering the total amount appropriated for FY 2027.
