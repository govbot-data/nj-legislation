STATEMENT

 

     The Fiscal Year 2027 appropriations act currently allocates $2 million to Rowan University for the Virtua Health College of Medicine and Life Sciences.  This bill reassigns that appropriation to the Department of Health for Virtua Health College for hospital support without altering the total amount appropriated for FY 2027.
