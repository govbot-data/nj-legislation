A5752 ASSEMBLY, No. 5752 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  ANNETTE QUIJANO District 20 (Union) SYNOPSIS Requires developers to reserve at least 20 percent of residential units for occupancy by low- or moderate-income households for certain residential development projects. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning affordable housing in new residential development projects and supplementing P.L.1985, c.222 (C.52:27D-301 et al.) Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.         Notwithstanding the provisions of any law or court order to contrary, for a residential or mixed-use development project that includes five or more newly constructed residential units following the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), a developer shall reserve at least 20 percent of the total number of the residential units constructed for occupancy by low- and moderate-income households, in compliance with the Uniform Housing Affordability Controls, adopted pursuant to subsection b. of section 36 of P.L.2024, c.2 (C.52:27D-313.3).  A municipality shall receive credit towards the municipalitys fair share obligation for units constructed pursuant to this subsection. b.    In addition to any other recourse available, a municipality or third party may file a civil action in the Superior Court of New Jersey, Law Division alleging a violation, or violations, of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) and seeking enforcement.  A developer found in violation of P.L. , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) shall additionally be subject to a fine of not more than $30,000 per affected unit be paid to the municipality in which the violation occurred for deposit into the municipal affordable housing trust fund. 2.    The Commissioner of Community Affairs, pursuant to the "Administrative Procedure Act," P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.), shall adopt rules and regulations to effectuate the provisions of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill). 3.    This act shall take effect the first day of the sixth month next following enactment. STATEMENT This bill requires a developer, for a residential project consisting of five or more newly constructed residential units following the effective date of the bill, to reserve at least 20 percent of the total number of residential units constructed for occupancy by low- and moderate-income households in compliance with the Uniform Housing Affordability Controls. Currently, State law does not mandate that a residential project reserve a certain percentage of its housing stock for units for occupancy by low- or moderate-income households.  However, it is common practice among municipalities to adopt ordinances to enforce their affordable housing objectives by requiring a developer to reserve a certain percentage of housing units for low- or moderate-income households.  The bill codifies this practice. The bill additionally permits a municipality or third party to file a civil action alleging a violation of the bill, and seeking enforcement.  A developer found in violation of the bill is additionally liable to a fine of not more than $30,000 per affected unit be paid to the municipality in which the violation occurred for the deposit into the municipal affordable housing trust fund. The bill permits the Commissioner of Community Affairs to promulgate rules and regulations to effectuate the provisions of the bill. The bill is to take effect the first day of the sixth month next following enactment.
