MILLER District 4 (Atlantic, Camden and Gloucester) SYNOPSIS Revises laws concerning heating, ventilating, air conditioning and refrigeration contracting. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning HVACR contractor and subcontractor licensing and amending and supplementing P.L.2007, c.211 and amending P.L.1999, c.238. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    (New section) As used in sections 1 through 9 of P.L. , c. (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill): Board means the State Board of Examiners of Heating, Ventilating, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Contractors. Covered HVACR work means contracting work in heating, ventilating, air conditioning and refrigeration as defined in section 2 of P.L.2007, c.211 (C.45:16A-2), and shall include the assembly, furnishing, installation, alteration, maintenance, repair, replacement, or servicing of: (1) piping, refrigerant piping, hydronic piping, fittings, valves, supports, hangers, and other components used for the conveyance, distribution, circulation, supply, return, or control of refrigerants, water, steam, condensate, or other fluids used as part of a heating, ventilating, air conditioning, or refrigeration system; and (2) ductwork, plenums, dampers, louvers, air-intake components, exhaust components, air-distribution components, fittings, supports, hangers, curbs, and other sheet metal or mechanical components used for the movement, distribution, supply, return, intake, exhaust, ventilation, treatment, or control of air as part of a heating, ventilating, air conditioning, refrigeration, air movement, air distribution, exhaust, intake, or plenum system. Master HVACR contractors license means a current and valid license issued by the board pursuant to P.L.2007, c.211 (C.45:16A-1 et seq.), authorizing only the person, firm, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or other legal entity named on the license to engage in heating, ventilating, air conditioning and refrigeration contracting in this State. A Master HVACR contractors license shall not authorize any other person or separate legal entity to engage in HVACR contracting. HVACR system component means any component, material, or assembly used or intended to be used in covered HVACR work, including, but not limited to, piping, refrigerant piping, hydronic piping, fittings, valves, and related components used for the conveyance, distribution, circulation, or control of fluids, and ductwork, plenums, dampers, louvers, air-intake components, exhaust components, air-distribution components, fittings, supports, hangers, curbs, and related sheet metal or mechanical components used for the movement, distribution, intake, exhaust, ventilation, or control of air. Responsible contracting entity means any contractor, general contractor, construction manager, subcontractor, owner, developer, or other person or entity that hires, permits, directs, awards, administers, or contracts for covered HVACR work. Qualifying apprenticeship program means a registered apprenticeship program approved by the United States Department of Labor, Office of Apprenticeship, or by a federally recognized State Apprenticeship Agency or State Apprenticeship Council, provided that the program maintains records of enrollment and completion, issues proof of completion or enrollment, satisfies applicable federal apprenticeship standards, and has not been deregistered, suspended, or found by the approving agency to be out of compliance with applicable apprenticeship standards; STATEMENT This bill revises The State Heating, Ventilating, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Contracting License Law.  Specifically, the bill provides that a person is required to hold a Master HVACR contractors license prior to the registration of an HVACR contracting business in the State.  Additionally, the bill clarifies that a person is prohibited from engaging in or performing heating, ventilating, air conditioning and refrigeration contracting work, as a Master HVACR, without holding a Master HVACR contractors license. The bill prohibits a responsible contracting entity from hiring, permitting, directing, or contracting with a subcontractor to perform heating, ventilating, air conditioning and refrigeration work unless the subcontractor is independently licensed as a Master HVACR contractor. Under the bill, any person who works as a Master HVACR contractor but does not hold a Master HVACR contractors license will have the first day of the seventh month next following the effective date of the bill to obtain a Master HVACR contractors license or designate a bona fide representative before the failure to hold the license constitutes a violation of the law. The bill requires any person or entity engaged in the fabrication, manufacture, or assembly of ductwork, piping, or HVACR system components within the State for installation within the State to hold a Master HVACR contractors license. The bill provides for penalties of violations of its provisions as follows: (1)  for the first offense, a penalty of up to $10,000; (2)  for the second offense, a penalty of up to $25,000; (3)  for the third or any subsequent offense, a penalty of up to $50,000; and (4)  the board may suspend or revoke the persons license. Additionally, each day of noncompliance with the provisions of the bill and each project will constitute a separate violation. The bill provides for reciprocity standards for licensing issued by other states. Finally, the bill amends The Public Works Contractor Registration Act to require that any person bidding on a contract or work subject to that law if the work concerns heating, ventilating, air conditioning and refrigeration is to hold a Master HVACR contractors license.
