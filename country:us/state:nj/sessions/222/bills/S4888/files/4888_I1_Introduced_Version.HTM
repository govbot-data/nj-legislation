SYNOPSIS

     Establishes Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council in DOE.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act establishing an artificial intelligence youth advisory council and supplementing chapter 6 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    a.  There is established in the Department of Education an Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council for the purpose of providing a forum for the youth of the State to advise the Governor and the Legislature on the perspectives, opinions, and needs of students as it relates to the growing use of artificial intelligence. The advisory council shall research, analyze, discuss, and make specific recommendations on ways to use artificial intelligence in education.

     b.  The advisory council shall consist of 17 public members, each of whom shall serve a two-year term, appointed by the Governor through a competitive application process established by the Governor. The application process shall be designed to ensure diversity with respect to race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, disability, sexual orientation, and political affiliation, to the maximum extent practicable, and the representation of a cross section of public schools and public institutions of higher education located in urban, suburban, and rural areas of the State. The application process may request recommendations from each executive county superintendent and the president of each public institution of higher education.

     c.  To be eligible, a student seeking to be a public member shall be:

     (1) at least 14, but no more than 22, years of age;

     (2) a resident of the State for a period of at least two years immediately prior to applying for membership on the advisory council; and

     (3) a student enrolled in a public high school in the State or as an undergraduate student in good standing at a public institution of higher education in the State.

     d.  A public member may be reappointed to the advisory council until the member turns 22 years of age or ceases enrollment in a public high school or public institution of higher education in the State.

     e.  Any vacancies in the membership of the advisory council shall be filled in the same manner provided for the original appointments.

     f.  The public members of the advisory council shall serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for traveling and other miscellaneous expenses necessary to perform their duties within the limits of funds made available to the council for its purposes.

     g.  A public member who is less than 18 years of age shall be required to provide written permission from a parent or legal guardian to be a member of the advisory council and participate in its activities.

 

     2.    a.  The Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council shall organize as soon as practicable but no later than 60 days following the appointment of its members and shall select a chairperson and vice-chairperson from among the members.  The chairperson shall appoint a secretary who need not be a member of the council.

     b.  The advisory council may meet at the call of its chair and hold hearings at the times and in the places it deems appropriate and necessary to fulfill its charge.  The advisory council may meet using remote technology.

     c.  The advisory council may seek the advice of experts, such as persons specializing in the field of education, artificial intelligence, computer science, economic development, workforce development, or other related fields, as deemed appropriate by the membership of the council.

     d.  The Department of Education shall provide stenographic, clerical and other administrative assistants, and any professional staff as the advisory council requires to carry out its work. The council shall be entitled to call to its assistance and avail itself of the services of the employees of any State, county, or municipal department, board, bureau, commission, or agency as it may require and as may be available to it for its purposes.

 

     3.    The Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council shall:

     a.  examine issues related to the use of artificial intelligence in public schools and public institutions of higher education;

     b.  support existing, and develop new, Statewide initiatives relating to the safe, responsible, appropriate, ethical, and equitable use of artificial intelligence among school-aged children and students attending public institutions of higher education; and

     c.  make recommendations to the Governor and the Legislature concerning the regulation of artificial intelligence in public schools and public institutions of higher education.

 

     4.    The Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council shall submit an annual report to the Governor and to the Legislature, pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), by July 1 of each year, on the activities of the advisory council and its findings and recommendations regarding the use of artificial intelligence in public schools and public institutions of higher education.

     5.    This act shall take effect immediately.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill establishes the Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council in the Department of Education.

     The purpose of the council would be to provide a forum for the youth of the State to advise the Governor and the Legislature on the perspectives, opinions, and needs of students as it relates to the growing use of artificial intelligence. The advisory council will research, analyze, discuss, and make specific recommendations on ways to use artificial intelligence in education.

     The council would consist of 17 public members, each of whom is required to serve a two-year term, appointed by the Governor through a competitive application process established by the Governor. The Governor may consider recommendations from each county executive superintendent and the president of each public institution of higher education in the application process. To be eligible, a student seeking to be a public member is required to be: (1) at least 14, but no more than 22, years of age; (2) a resident of the State for a period of at least two years immediately prior to applying for membership on the advisory council; and (3) a student enrolled in a public high school in the State or as an undergraduate student in good standing at a public institution of higher education in the State.

     The Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council will: examine issues related to the use of artificial intelligence in public schools and public institutions of higher education; support existing, and develop new, Statewide initiatives relating to the safe, responsible, appropriate, ethical, and equitable use of artificial intelligence among school-aged children and students attending public institutions of higher education; and make recommendations to the Governor and the Legislature concerning the regulation of artificial intelligence in public schools and public institutions of higher education.

     Finally, the Artificial Intelligence in Education Youth Advisory Council is required to submit an annual report to the Governor and the Legislature.
