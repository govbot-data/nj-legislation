STATEMENT This bill allows certain debt adjustment companies to conduct business in the State.  A debt adjuster is a person or entity who: acts or offers to act for a consideration as an intermediary between a debtor and its creditors for the purpose of settling, compounding, or otherwise altering the terms of payment of any debts of the debtor; or receives money or other property from the debtor, or on behalf of the debtor, for payment to, or distribution among, the creditors of the debtor. Under New Jerseys current law on debt adjusters, which is administered and enforced by the Department of Banking and Insurance, only nonprofit social service agencies and nonprofit consumer credit counseling agencies may operate debt adjustment services in the State. The bill would create an exception to the current law to allow debt adjustment companies to conduct business in the State provided the company (1) does not receive or hold, actually or constructively, consumer funds; and (2) complies with the provisions of this bill. The bill generally subjects all licensed debt adjuster companies to a common set of rules and restrictions, but with certain exceptions.  Under the bill, a debt adjuster licensee other than a nonprofit social service agency or nonprofit consumer credit counseling agency is not required to provide a certification about salaries and expenses as part of its annual audit and is not subject to the bonding requirements that apply to nonprofit social service agencies and nonprofit consumer credit counseling agencies that perform debt adjuster services in the State.  The bill would also provide that the annual report that the commissioner may require a licensed nonprofit social service agency, nonprofit consumer credit counseling agency, or debt adjuster to file is to include the total number of enrolled consumers in the State, the total fees collected in the State for providing debt adjustment services, and the total amount of debt settled in the State.  It also provides that if a licensee fails to file an annual report, the licensee will be subject to penalties available under current law. The bill also requires debt adjuster licensees other than a nonprofit social service agency or nonprofit consumer credit counseling agency that enter into an agreement with a debtor to disclose certain information to the debtor. Lastly, the bill clarifies language about persons engaged in the practice of law that are not deemed to be debt adjusters.
