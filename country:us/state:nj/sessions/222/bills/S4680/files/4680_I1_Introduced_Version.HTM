SYNOPSIS

     Requires Director of the Division of Local Government Services in DCA to establish pilot program for acquiring abandoned properties in certain municipalities and develop land bank agreement.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning certain abandoned properties and supplementing and amending P.L.2016, c.4.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    (New section)  a.  The director, in accordance with the authority granted pursuant to P.L.2016, c.4 (C.52:27BBBB-1 et seq.), shall establish a pilot program, which shall expire on December 1, 2031, to acquire abandoned properties in a municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, as determined by the director pursuant to subsection b. of this section.  The pilot program shall permit the Department of Community Affairs to acquire an abandoned property through:

     (1)   purchase or gift; or

     (2)   the abandoned property acquisition processes established by the "Abandoned Properties Rehabilitation Act," P.L.2003, 210 (C.55:19-78 et al.).

     b.    (1)  The director shall establish and maintain a list of abandoned properties, as defined in section 4 of P.L.2003, c.210 (C.55:19-81), by compiling the tax rolls, ground surveys, and any abandoned property list maintained pursuant to the "Abandoned Properties Rehabilitation Act," P.L.2003, 210 (C.55:19-78 et al.) by the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery until the expiration of the pilot program.

     (2)   Upon the completion of the list of abandoned properties established pursuant to paragraph (1) of this subsection, as determined by the director, the director shall formally designate any property that meets the requirements of being abandoned, pursuant to section 4 of P.L.2003, c.210 (C.55:19-81), as abandoned if the property was not already designated as abandoned by the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, and shall use this list in any action to transfer title of the property to the Department of Community Affairs.

     c.     (1)  The director may conduct, or contract to conduct, pursuant to section 13 of P.L.2003, c.210 (C.55:19-90), rehabilitation or demolition services on any property on the abandoned property list where the Department of Community Affairs has been granted title and the director deems in need of rehabilitation or demolition.

     (2)   The redevelopment consultant appointed by the director, pursuant to subparagraph (o) of paragraph (3) of subsection a. of section 5 of P.L.2016, c.4 (C.52:27BBBB-5), shall expedite the permitting approval process for any permits concerning the rehabilitation or demolition of vacant properties.

     d.    (1)  An abandoned property the director deems habitable shall be subject to a tax sale, sheriff sale, or other property auction within one year of a propertys designation as abandoned, or within six months of the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) for properties that have been previously designated as abandoned by the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery.

     (2)   An abandoned property that the director determines is in need of rehabilitation or demolition shall be subject to a tax sale, sheriff sale, or other property auction after the required work is completed or three years from the date the Department of Community Affairs takes possession of the propertys title, whichever is earlier.

     e.     The director shall have the authority of a "public officer," as defined in section 3 of P.L.2003, c.210 (C.55:19-80), when acting pursuant to the "Abandoned Properties Rehabilitation Act," P.L.2003, 210 (C.55:19-78 et al.).

     2.    (New section)  a.  Within six months of the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), the director shall develop a land bank agreement pursuant to section 5 of P.L.2019, c.159 (C.40A:12A-78), incorporating the provisions of the "New Jersey Land Bank Law," sections 1 through 16 of P.L.2019, c.159 (C.40A:12A-74 et seq.), as appropriate.  The land bank agreement shall establish the responsibilities of a land bank entity in acquiring property on behalf of the director in a municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, in addition to any other responsibilities deemed necessary by the director.

     d.    The redevelopment consultant appointed by the director, pursuant to subparagraph (o) of paragraph (3) of subsection a. of section 5 of P.L.2016, c.4 (C.52:27BBBB-5), shall serve as the executive director of the land bank.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill adds additional powers to the authority granted to the Director of the Division of Local Government Services (director) in the Department of Community Affairs (DCA), pursuant to the "Municipal Stabilization and Recovery Act," P.L.2016, c.4 (C.52:27BBBB-1 et seq.).  Specifically, the bill requires the director to establish a pilot program to acquire abandoned properties, as determined by the director, in a municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, as determined by the director.  The pilot program is to expire on December 1, 2031  The pilot program is to permit the DCA to acquire an abandoned property through:

     (1)   purchase or gift; or

     (2)   the abandoned property acquisition processes established by the "Abandoned Properties Rehabilitation Act," (APRA) P.L.2003, 210 (C.55:19-78 et al.). 

     The bill requires the director to establish and maintain a list of abandoned properties, as defined in section 4 of P.L.2003, c.210 (C.55:19-81), by compiling the tax rolls, ground surveys, and any abandoned property list maintained pursuant to the APRA by the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery until the expiration of the pilot program.

     The bill provides that, once the director deems the list of abandoned properties complete, the director is to formally designate any property that meets the requirements of being abandoned, pursuant to section 4 of P.L.2003, c.210 (C.55:19-81), as abandoned if the property was not previously designated as abandoned by the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, and shall use this list in any action to transfer title of the property to the DCA.

     The director may conduct, or contract to conduct, rehabilitation or demolition services on any property on the abandoned property list where the DCA has been granted title and the director deems in need of rehabilitation or demolition.

     The redevelopment consultant appointed by the director is to expedite the permitting approval process for any permits concerning the rehabilitation or demolition of vacant properties.

     The bill provides that an abandoned property the director deems habitable is to be subject to a tax sale, sheriff sale, or other property auction within one year of a propertys designation as abandoned, or within six months of the effective date of the bill for properties that have been previously designated as abandoned by the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery.  An abandoned property the director determines is in need of rehabilitation or demolition is to be subject to a tax sale, sheriff sale, or other property auction after the required work is completed or three years from the date the DCA takes possession of the propertys title, whichever is earlier.

     The commissioner is to submit, for the duration of the pilot program, a biennial report to the Governor and to the Legislature, providing certain information enumerated in the bill.  The final report is to include recommendations from the redevelopment consultant appointed by the director concerning prospective changes to planning and zoning board development and redevelopment processes in the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery.

     Within six months of the effective date of the bill, the director is to develop a land bank agreement pursuant to the "New Jersey Land Bank Law," sections 1 through 16 of P.L.2019, c.159 (C.40A:12A-74 et seq.).  The land bank agreement is to establish the responsibilities of a land bank entity in acquiring property on behalf of the director in a municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, in addition to any other responsibilities deemed necessary by the director.  The director is to determine whether the land bank entity subject to the agreement is to be the DCA, a "nonprofit land bank entity," as defined in section 3 of P.L.2019, c.159 (C.40A:12A-76), or a municipality in need of stabilization and recovery.  The director, in collaboration with a municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, is to determine the date of establishment of the land bank in accordance with the land bank agreement developed pursuant to the bill.  The redevelopment consultant is to serve as the executive director of the land bank.

     Lastly, the bill amends the "Municipal Stabilization and Recovery Act" to require the director to appoint a redevelopment consultant, who is to be a resident of the municipality in need of stabilization and recovery, within 30 days of the enactment of this bill.
