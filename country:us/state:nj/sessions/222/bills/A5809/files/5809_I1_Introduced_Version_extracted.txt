A5809 ASSEMBLY, No. 5809 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  GREGORY E. MYHRE District 9 (Ocean) Assemblyman  BRIAN E. RUMPF District 9 (Ocean) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblymen Kanitra, Scharfenberger, Inganamort, Peterson, McGuckin, Assemblywomen Fantasia and Flynn SYNOPSIS Prohibits use of State funds by institutions of higher education for legal representation of undocumented immigrants. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning clinical legal programs at institutions of higher education and supplementing Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  Notwithstanding any law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, the State shall not appropriate funds to an institution of higher education for clinical legal programs that provide legal services to undocumented immigrants. b.  Notwithstanding any law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, an institution of higher education shall not utilize any funds appropriated by the State, including general institutional support funds, for clinical legal programs that provide legal services to undocumented immigrants. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill prohibits the use of State funds by institutions of higher education to support clinical legal programs that provide legal services to undocumented immigrants.  Specifically, the bill prohibits the State from appropriating funds to an institution of higher education for clinical legal programs that provide legal services to undocumented immigrants.  Additionally, the bill prohibits an institution of higher education from utilizing any funds appropriated by the State for such programs.
