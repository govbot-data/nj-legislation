A5795 ASSEMBLY, No. 5795 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  SHANIQUE SPEIGHT District 29 (Essex and Hudson) SYNOPSIS Requires DOE to designate non-profit entity to coordinate out-of-school time programs. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning out-of-school time programs and supplementing chapter 6 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    The Extended Learning Opportunities Unit within the Office of Student Support Services in the Department of Education, in consultation with the Division of Family Development in the Department of Human Services, shall designate a non-profit entity to be the States central coordinating entity for before-school, after school, summer, and out-of-school time programs. The designated non-profit entity shall coordinate, support, and strengthen the delivery of such programs by State and local entities and community-based organizations. The non-profit entity designated pursuant to this section shall be a member of the National Network of Statewide Afterschool Networks. 2.    a.  The non-profit entity designated pursuant to section 1 of this act shall coordinate with the following State officials: (1)  the Commissioner of Education, or a designee; (2)  the Commissioner of Children and Families, or a designee; (3)  the Commissioner of Human Services, or a designee; (4)  the Commissioner of Health, or a designee; and (5)  the Attorney General, or a designee. b.  Coordination pursuant to this section shall support the alignment of policies and funding streams, data sharing as permitted under federal or State law, and programmatic support. 3.    The non-profit entity designated pursuant to section 1 of this act shall: a.  assess and analyze State policies impacting out-of-school time programs in the State to: (1) identify and eliminate cross-agency barriers; (2) ensure consistency with State law; (3) review relevant research and best practices; (4) make recommendations to strengthen and stabilize the school-age child care sector; and (5) improve access to out-of-school time programs; b.  research and recommend a model for a voluntary school-age child care quality rating and improvement system; c.  identify and map existing funding streams that support children and youth and document current and potential sources of investment for a dedicated funding stream, including strategies to increase subsidy rates linked to tiered reimbursement within a school-age child care quality rating and improvement system. The identification and mapping pursuant to this section shall analyze public and private investments to better understand how child-serving programs are currently financed and where gaps or opportunities for alignment exist; d.  examine the development and operation of  school district and municipal out-of-school time programs that are not required to be licensed by the Office of Licensing within the Department of Children and Families; e.  make recommendations to increase collaboration between school district and municipal out-of-school time programs and licensed child care centers to improve program quality Statewide; f.  coordinate with child care advocacy organizations, Statewide youth-serving community-based organizations, and out-of-school time providers; g.  engage with community members in the development and implementation of policies, strategies, and programmatic recommendations; and h.  annually submit a report to the Governor, and the Legislature pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), concerning the recommendations of the non-profit entity to improve out-of-school time opportunities in the State. 4.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires the Department of Education to designate a non-profit agency to coordinate out-of-school time programs. Under the bill, the Department of Education, in consultation with the Department of Human Services, is required to designate a non-profit agency to be the States central coordinating entity for before-school, after school, summer, and out-of-school time programs. The designated non-profit entity will coordinate, support, and strengthen the delivery of such programs by State entities and community-based organizations. The non-profit entity designated pursuant to this section is required to be a member of the National Network of Statewide Afterschool Networks. The bill requires the non-profit entity designated under the bill to coordinate with various State agencies. Coordination will include the alignment of policies and funding streams, data sharing as permitted under federal or State law, and programmatic support. The bill requires the non-profit entity designated under the bill to: (1) assess and analyze State policies impacting out-of-school time programs in the State; (2) research and recommend a model for a voluntary school-age child care quality rating and improvement system; (3)  identify and map existing funding streams that support children and youth and document current and potential sources of investment for a dedicated funding stream including strategies to increase subsidy rates linked to tiered reimbursement within the quality rating and improvement system; (4) examine the development and operation of school district and municipal out-of-school time programs that are not required to be licensed; (5) make recommendations to increase collaboration between school district and municipal out-of-school time programs and licensed child care centers to improve program quality Statewide; (6) coordinate with child care advocacy organizations, Statewide youth-serving community-based organizations, and out-of-school time providers; (7) engage with community members in the development and implementation of policies, strategies, and programmatic recommendations; and (8) annually submit a report to the Governor and the Legislature concerning the recommendations of the non-profit entity to improve out-of-school time opportunities in the State.
