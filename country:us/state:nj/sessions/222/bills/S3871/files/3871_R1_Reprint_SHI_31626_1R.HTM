SYNOPSIS

     Directs Higher Education Student Assistance Authority to increase income protection allowance used in determining eligibility of independent adult student for Tuition Aid Grant Program.   

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As reported by the Senate Higher Education Committee on March 16, 2026, with amendments.

  


An Act concerning the Tuition Aid Grant Program and supplementing chapter 71B 1of Title 18A1 of the New Jersey Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1. The Higher Education Student Assistance Authority shall increase the income protection allowance used when calculating financial eligibility of an independent adult student for a tuition aid grant to equal the income protection allowance used by the United States Department of Education when calculating eligibility for an independent adult student for a federal Pell grant. 

 

     2. This act shall take effect immediately and shall first apply to tuition aid grants awarded in the first academic year beginning after the date of enactment.
