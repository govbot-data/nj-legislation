A5817 ASSEMBLY, No. 5817 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  SHANIQUE SPEIGHT District 29 (Essex and Hudson) Assemblyman  DAN HUTCHISON District 4 (Atlantic, Camden and Gloucester) SYNOPSIS Requires local units to submit inspection and testing results of all elevator devices for inclusion on DCA Service Portal. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning the testing and inspection of elevator devices and supplementing P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-119 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    The governing body of a local unit shall ensure that the inspection and testing results of all elevator devices, as defined in section 1 of P.L.1997, c.336 (C.52:27D-126f), within its jurisdiction which are subject to routine, periodic, and acceptance inspections and tests by the local enforcing agency or the Department of Community Affairs under the provisions of the "State Uniform Construction Code Act," P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-119 et seq.), or which are subjected to acceptance testing and are routinely and periodically inspected and tested by a qualified elevator device inspection firm pursuant to subsection b. of section 1 of P.L.1997, c.336 (C.52:27D-126f), are submitted to the Elevator Safety Unit in the Department of Community Affairs for inclusion in the departments Service Portal. The administrative costs of the information submission required by this section shall be addressed through the associated inspection fees. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires the governing body of a local unit to ensure that the inspection and testing results of all elevator devices within its jurisdiction which are subject to routine, periodic, and acceptance inspections and tests by the local enforcing agency or the Department of Community Affairs under the provisions of the "State Uniform Construction Code Act," P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-119 et seq.), or which are subjected to acceptance testing and are routinely and periodically inspected and tested by a qualified elevator device inspection firm, are submitted to the Elevator Safety Unit in the Department of Community Affairs for inclusion in the departments Service Portal.
