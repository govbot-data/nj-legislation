STATEMENT This bill amends the Fiscal Year 2027 appropriations act to reassign a portion of appropriation for Eid Committee of New Jersey to the Cultural Society of Berger County to support the Moms Food Pantry. The Fiscal Year 2027 appropriations act currently allocates $350,000 to the Eid Committee of New Jersey for a community engagement initiative. This bill reassigns $300,000 from this appropriation to the Cultural Society of Bergen, which is a non-profit organization that supports families in need by providing essential groceries and basic household necessities.  These funds are provided to support the organizations Moms Food Pantry.  This bill does not alter the total amount appropriated for FY2027.
