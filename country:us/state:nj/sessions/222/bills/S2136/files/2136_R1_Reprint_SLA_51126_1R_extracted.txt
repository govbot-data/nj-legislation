S2136 1R [First Reprint] SENATE, No. 2136 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE PRE-FILED FOR INTRODUCTION IN THE 2026 SESSION Sponsored by: Senator PAUL D. MORIARTY District 4 (Atlantic, Camden and Gloucester) SYNOPSIS Requires employers to include in job posting whether posting is for existing position and sets additional job posting requirements. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Senate Labor Committee on May 11, 2026, with amendments. An Act concerning job postings and supplementing Title 34 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 11.   The Legislature finds and declares that : a.     Transparent and accurate job postings and advertisements promote fairness for job applicants and efficiency in the labor market; b.    Advertisements for job postings that do not correspond to hiring need and are never filled, commonly referred to as ghost jobs, create an undue burden on job seekers leading many to distrust employers and make the job finding process more difficult; c.     According to internal data from the hiring platform Greenhouse, between 18 percent and 22 percent of jobs advertised in 2024 were postings for positions that were never filled, with nearly 70 percent of companies on the platform posting at least one ghost job in the latter half of 2024; d.    Ongoing recruitment efforts for anticipated staffing needs or recurring positions are necessary to maintain workforce stability; and e.     This act is not intended to prohibit recruitment practices that include appropriate disclosures or to hold employers liable for third-party job posting companies acting outside of the control of the employer.1 1[1.] 2.1     a.
