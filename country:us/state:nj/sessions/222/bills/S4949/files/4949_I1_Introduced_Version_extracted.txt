S4949 SENATE, No. 4949 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  JOHN F. MCKEON District 27 (Essex and Passaic) Senator  JOSEPH PENNACCHIO District 26 (Morris and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Concerns activation of parental controls on internet-enabled devices. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning Internet-enabled devices and supplementing P.L.1960, c.39 (C.56:8-1 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  As used in this section: At retail means any retail transaction conducted in person and excludes any telephone, mail, or Internet-based transaction. Internet-enabled device means a smartphone, tablet, or smartwatch which is capable of connecting to the Internet and enabling the user to access content on the Internet, including social media applications. b.    Any person selling an Internet-enabled device at retail shall, at the point of sale: (1)   inquire if the Internet-enabled device is being purchased for a minor age 14 or under; (2)   inquire if the purchaser would like any available parental controls activated on the Internet-enabled device if the device is being purchased for a minor age 14 or under; and (3)   activate the parental controls on the Internet-enabled device if the purchaser requests that the parental controls be activated. c.     Any person selling an Internet-enabled device at retail shall post a sign at the point where the sales transaction is completed with a notice that parental controls may be activated on an Internet-enabled device that is being purchased for a minor age 14 or under. d.    A person who violates the provisions of subsections b. or c. of this section shall be liable to a civil penalty of $1,000, which shall be collected by the Director of the Division of Consumer Affairs in the Department of Law and Public Safety in a summary proceeding before a court of competent jurisdiction pursuant to the provisions of the "Penalty Enforcement Law of 1999," P.L.1999, c.274 (C.2A:58-10 et seq.). e.     A violation of this section shall not be considered an unlawful practice in violation of P.L.1960, c.39 (C.56:8-1 et seq.). 2.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the third month next following the date of enactment. STATEMENT This bill concerns the activation of parental controls on Internet-enabled devices. Under the provisions of this bill, any person selling an Internet-enabled device at retail shall, at the point of sale: 1) inquire if the Internet-enabled device is being purchased for a minor age 14 or under; 2) inquire if the purchaser would like any available parental controls activated on the Internet-enabled device if the device is being purchased for a minor age 14 or under; and 3) activate the parental controls on the Internet-enabled device if the purchaser requests that the parental controls be activated. In addition, the bill requires any person selling an Internet-enabled device at retail to post a sign at the point where the sales transaction is completed with a notice that parental controls may be activated on an Internet-enabled device that is being purchased for a minor age 14 or under. Under the bill, at retail means any retail transaction conducted in person and excludes any telephone, mail, or Internet-based transaction.  In addition, the bill defines an Internet-enabled device as a smartphone, tablet, or smartwatch which is capable of connecting to the Internet and enabling the user to access content on the Internet, including social media applications. A person who violates the provisions of this bill is liable to a civil penalty of $1,000, which is to be collected by the Director of the Division of Consumer Affairs in the Department of Law and Public Safety in a summary proceeding before a court of competent jurisdiction pursuant to the "Penalty Enforcement Law of 1999. A violation is not considered an unlawful practice under the consumer fraud act.
