District 18 (Middlesex) Co-Sponsored by: Senators Corrado, Singleton, McKnight, Moriarty, Holzapfel, Burgess, Henry and Beach SYNOPSIS Requires school bus drivers and school bus aides to call 911 emergency line in potential life-threatening emergencies; requires certain school buses transporting students with disabilities to be equipped with certain safety features. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As reported by the Senate Budget and Appropriations Committee on June 24, 2026, with amendments.
