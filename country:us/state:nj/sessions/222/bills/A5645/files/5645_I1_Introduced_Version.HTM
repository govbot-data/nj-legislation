STATEMENT

 

     This bill requires the Commissioner of Education to provide school districts with a preliminary State school aid notice no later than the end of the first week of November.

     Under current law, the first notice of State school aid is required to be sent to school districts no later than two days following the delivery of the Governors budget address, which is required to be delivered to the Legislature on or before the fourth Tuesday in February.  The notice provides each district with the maximum amount of aid payable to the district in the succeeding school year.  This bill would additionally require the commissioner to produce a preliminary State aid notice in the November prior to the Governors budget address.  The preliminary notices would first be produced for State school aid being distributed in the second full school year following the date of enactment.
