SYNOPSIS

     Establishes minimum NJ FamilyCare reimbursement rate for certain residential substance use disorder treatment services.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning substance use disorder treatment services and supplementing Title 30 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    As used in this act:

     ASAM level of care means the ASAM Patient Placement Criteria developed by the American Society of Addiction Medicine.

      NJ FamilyCare means the State-branded name given to the Medicaid program established pursuant to P.L.1968, c.413 (C.30:4D-1 et seq.) and the program established pursuant to P.L.2005, c.156 (C.30:4J-8 et al.) which includes the Childrens Health Insurance Program.

 

     2.    a.  The NJ FamilyCare reimbursement rate paid for Clinically Managed High-Intensity Residential treatment services designated as ASAM level of care 3.5 for enrollees with a diagnosed substance use disorder, whether provided through the NJ FamilyCare fee-for-service or managed care delivery system, shall be no less than $322.09 per diem. 

     b.    The reimbursement rate established pursuant to subsection a. of this section shall be annually increased by the annual percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for Medical Care.

 

     3.    a.  The NJ FamilyCare reimbursement rate paid for Medically Monitored Intensive Inpatient treatment services designated as ASAM level of care of 3.7 for enrollees with a diagnosed substance use disorder, whether provided through the NJ FamilyCare fee-for-service or managed care delivery system, shall be no less than $462.02 per diem.

     b.    The reimbursement rate established pursuant to subsection a. of this section shall be annually increased by the annual percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for Medical Care.

 

     4.    The Commissioner of Human Services shall apply for such state plan amendments or waivers as may be necessary to implement the provisions of this act and to secure federal reimbursements for State Medicaid expenditures under the federal Medicaid program. 

 

     5.    The Commissioner of Human Services, pursuant to the Administrative Procedure Act, P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.) shall adopt rules and regulations necessary to implement the provisions of this act.

 

     6. This act shall take effect immediately.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill would increase the NJ FamilyCare per diem reimbursement rate for Clinically Managed High-Intensity Residential services, designated as level of care 3.5 by the American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM), for individuals with a diagnosed substance use disorder (SUD) who require 24 hour residential care supported by trained counselors but who do not require nursing care, to a minimum of $322.09.  This reimbursement rate would increase annually by the annual percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for Medical Care (CPI-M). 

     The bill also would increase the NJ FamilyCare rate paid for Medically Monitored Intensive Inpatient care, designated as level of care 3.7 by the American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM), for enrollees who require 24-hour residential care with nursing services and a physician available for significant medical or psychiatric problems, at a per diem rate of no less than $462.02.  This per diem rate would be annually increased by the annual percentage change in the CPI-M.

     Currently, the NJ FamilyCare program pays $161.13 per diem for ASAM 3.5 residential treatment services, inclusive of room and board, for patients accessing care through the NJ FamilyCare fee-for-service delivery system.  This per diem rate is significantly lower than the Medicaid rates paid in Delaware ($259.95), Pennsylvania ($260.65), and New York ($288.71).  The NJ FamilyCare per diem rate for ASAM 3.7 residential care, inclusive of room and board, for enrollees accessing services via the NJ FamilyCare fee-for-service delivery system is $245.15.  By comparison, Delaware pays $377.60 per diem for the same services, while Pennsylvania reimburses $321.78, and New York pays $500.00.
