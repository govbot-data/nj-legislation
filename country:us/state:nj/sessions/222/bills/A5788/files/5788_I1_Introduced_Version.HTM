SYNOPSIS

     Requires Department of Health to develop educational materials and outreach campaign concerning sleep disorders; makes appropriation.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning sleep disorders, supplementing Title 26 of the Revised Statutes, and making an appropriation.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1. a. As used in this section:

     Department means the Department of Health.

     b. The department shall develop educational materials and conduct public education outreach activities related to sleep health and sleep disorders, including, but not limited to:

     (1) narcolepsy type 1 and type 2;

     (2) idiopathic hypersomnia;

     (3) obstructive sleep apnea;

     (4) central sleep apnea; and

     (5) circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders.

     c. The department shall design the educational materials and public education outreach activities under this section to:

     (1) increase public awareness of sleep disorders and the impact of sleep disorders on physical, mental, and public health;

     (2) promote early recognition, diagnosis, and evidence-based treatment of sleep disorders;

     (3) reduce stigma associated with sleep disorders, particularly sleep disorders that affect neurological and mental health;

     (4) educate health care providers, educators, and the general public on best practices for the screening, diagnosis, and management of sleep disorders; and

     (5) address the public safety implications of untreated sleep disorders, including occupational hazards and risks associated with driving.

     d. The department shall ensure that the educational materials and public education outreach activities under this section are updated from time to time to reflect the most recent scientific and medical research concerning sleep disorders.

     e. The department may partner with other governmental entities and private organizations, including insurers, health care provider professional organizations, local school systems, and community health programs, to disseminate the educational materials and conduct the public education outreach activities required under this section.

     f. There is appropriated from the General Fund to the Department of Health the sum of $125,000 to develop the educational materials and implement the public education outreach activities required under this section.

 

     2. This act shall take effect on the first day of the second month next following enactment, except that the Commissioner of Health may take such anticipatory action as may be necessary for the timely implementation of this act.

 

 

STATEMENT

 

     This bill requires the Department of Health to develop educational materials and conduct public education outreach activities concerning sleep health and sleep disorders.  The bill specifically calls for greater public education and awareness of narcolepsy, idiopathic hypersomnia, obstructive sleep apnea, central sleep apnea, and circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders.  Under the bill, the departments educational and outreach goals include: increasing public awareness of sleep disorders and their impact on physical, mental, and public health; promoting early recognition, diagnosis, and treatment of sleep disorders; reducing stigma associated with sleep disorders; educating health care professionals concerning sleep disorder screening, diagnosis, and treatment; and addressing public safety implications of untreated sleep disorders.  The bill requires the department to ensure that the educational materials and outreach activities are updated to reflect the most recent scientific and medical research.  The bill allows the department to partner with public and private organizations to carry out these activities.  Finally, the bill appropriates $125,000 to implement these activities.
