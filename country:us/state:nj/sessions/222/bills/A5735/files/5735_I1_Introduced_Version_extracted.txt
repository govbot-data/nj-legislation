STATEMENT This bill amends P.L.1972, c.134 (C.40:56-65 et seq.) to authorize the establishment of seasonal pedestrian malls.  Specifically, the bill amends the laws findings and declarations to state that a district management corporation may determine whether a seasonal pedestrian mall would be in the best interest of the community to foster economic activity along avenues of commerce during advantageous times of year.  The bill also amends the definition of "pedestrian mall" to include "seasonal pedestrian mall." Further the bill provides that a governing body of a municipality is only to conduct one study concerning the establishment of a seasonal pedestrian mall, pursuant to law, and that the governing body is to notify property owners that abut the seasonal pedestrian mall of its proposed establishment. Lastly, the bill creates an exception for pedestrian malls that may be entirely funded from outside sources from submitting an estimate of the cost of operating and maintaining and annually improving the pedestrian mall to the governing body.  The bill makes additional technical changes.
