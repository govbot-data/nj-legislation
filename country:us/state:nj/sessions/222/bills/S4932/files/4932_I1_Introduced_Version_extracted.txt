STATEMENT This bill expands the Cultural Arts Incentives Program to include certain convention centers as a type of cultural arts institution facility for which a tax credit may be awarded. Under the Cultural Arts Incentives Program, also known as the Cultural Arts Facilities Expansion Program, the New Jersey Economic Development Authority (EDA) may award tax credits to cultural arts institutions to incentivize the establishment or redevelopment of a cultural arts institution facility. The bill amends the definition of a cultural arts institution facility to include convention centers. Specifically, the bill defines convention center as a facility that contains at least 400,000 square feet of space utilized to house conventions, conferences, trade shows, expositions, or similar events, and that is located within a municipality subject to the Municipal Stabilization and Recovery Act.  As defined, this specifically includes the Atlantic City Convention Center.  The bill also amends the definition of cultural arts institution to specify that the mission of such institution may include the hosting of conventions, conferences, trade shows, and exhibitions. Under current law, applications for the program are to be reviewed on a rolling basis, unless the EDA determines that demand is likely to exceed available tax credits, at which time the EDA may implement a competitive application process.  The bill amends existing law to require all applications to be reviewed on a rolling basis, regardless of the anticipated demand for the program.
