A5551 ASSEMBLY, No. 5551 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  KENYATTA STEWART District 35 (Bergen and Passaic) Assemblyman  BALVIR SINGH District 7 (Burlington) SYNOPSIS Provides corporation business tax and gross income tax credits to small businesses for portion of costs paid for advertisements with local news organizations. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning tax credits for small businesses that advertise with local news organizations, and supplementing P.L.1945, c.162 (C.54:10A-1 et seq.) and Title 54A of the New Jersey Statutes. STATEMENT This bill provides corporation business tax credits and gross income tax credits to small businesses that advertise with certain local news organizations. Under the bill, a small business is allowed a tax credit based on a percentage of its qualifying local media advertising expenses paid during a tax period.  A small business is defined under the bill to mean any business which has its principal place of business in this State, is independently owned and operated, and employs the equivalent of fewer than 50 full-time employees.  The bill also defines qualifying local media advertising expense means any trade or business expense deductible under federal law for advertising in a local newspaper or advertising on any broadcast radio or television station licensed by the Federal Communications Commission to serve a local community.  These expenses may also include, but are not limited to, sponsorships with a local newspaper, broadcast radio, or television station. For tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027, but before January 1, 2028, a small business would be eligible for a tax credit equal to 80 percent of the qualifying local media advertising expenses paid by the business during the tax period, up to $5,000. For tax periods beginning on or after the January 1, 2028, a small business would be eligible for a credit equal to 50 percent of the qualifying local media advertising expenses paid by the business during the tax period, up to $2,500 per tax period.  The taxpayer is required to also submit documentation verifying each expense for which the tax credit is claimed when the taxpayer submits their tax returns.
