S4934 SENATE, No. 4934 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  JOSEPH P. CRYAN District 20 (Union) SYNOPSIS Requires Chief Innovation Officer to conduct study and issue report on certain artificial intelligence model safety. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act directing the Chief Innovation Officer to conduct a study and issue a report on artificial intelligence model safety. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  As used in this act: Artificial intelligence technology means any technology involving the development or implementation of machine-based systems that, for explicit or implicit objectives, infer, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions that may influence physical or virtual environments. Foundation model means an artificial intelligence technology that is trained on a broad data set, designed for generality of output, and adaptable to a wide range of distinctive tasks. Frontier model means a foundation model that was trained using a quantity of computing power to be determined by the New Jersey Office of Information Technology. Kill switch means any hardware, software, firmware, or embedded feature that allows for a human operator to promptly shut down or disable a frontier model. b.    The Chief Innovation Officer of the New Jersey Innovation Authority shall consult with national experts and conduct a study on the technical feasibility of requiring the creation and implementation of a kill switch for a frontier model deployed or used in the State. c.     Within three months of the effective date of this act, the Chief Innovation Officer of the New Jersey Innovation Authority shall prepare and submit a detailed written report of the studys findings to the Governor, and to the Legislature pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1). 2.    This act shall take effect immediately and shall expire upon the submission of the written report required pursuant to subsection c. of section 1 of this act. STATEMENT This bill requires the Chief Innovation Officer of the New Jersey Innovation Authority (officer), in consultation with national experts, to conduct a study on the technical feasibility of requiring the creation and implementation of a kill switch for a frontier model, as those terms are defined in the bill, deployed or used in the State.  Within three months of the effective date of the bill, the officer is to prepare and submit a written report concerning the study to the Governor and the Legislature.
