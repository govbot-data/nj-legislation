STATEMENT

 

     This bill gradually reduces the corporation business tax rate and eliminates the corporate transit fee.

     Under current law, the corporation business tax rate is nine percent for a taxpayer that is not a New Jersey S corporation, except that a the tax rate for a taxpayer that is not a partnership and has income less than $100,000 is taxed at a reduced rate of 7.5 or 6.5 percent, depending upon income.  Currently, the corporate transit fee will expire after December 31, 2028.

     Under the bill, the corporation business tax will be reduced from nine percent to 2.5 percent over several years.  The corporate transit fee will be eliminated for privilege periods beginning on or after December 31, 2026.

     For privilege periods ending between January 1, 2027 and December 31, 2027, the corporation business tax rate will be seven percent.  For privilege periods ending between January 1, 2028 and December 31, 2028, the rate will be five percent.  For privilege periods ending between January 1, 2029 and December 31, 2029, the rate will be three percent.  For privilege periods ending after December 31, 2029, the rate will 2.5 percent.

     For a taxpayer other than a partnership with less than $100,000 of income, the corporation business tax rate will be reduced at a quicker rate.  Currently, the rate is 7.5 percent for taxpayers with income greater than $50,000 but less than $100,000, and 6.5 percent for taxpayers with income less than $50,000.  Under the bill, the rate will be 3.5 percent for taxpayers with less than $100,000 of income for privilege periods ending between January 1, 2027 and December 31, 2027, and 2.5 percent for privilege periods ending after December 31, 2027.

     A privilege period is the 12-month period for which corporation business tax is due.
