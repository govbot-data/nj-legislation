SYNOPSIS

     Efficient Income Verification Act.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning income verification and amending and supplementing chapter 21 of Title 43 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    (New section) This act shall be known and may be cited as the Efficient Income Verification Act.

 

     2.    (New section) The Legislature finds and declares that:

     a.     The federal government has enacted significant changes to the administration of public benefit programs through the federal law commonly known as H.R. 1, including new requirements concerning eligibility verification and work requirements for certain programs.

     b.    These federal changes will require New Jersey to modernize the manner in which it verifies income and determines eligibility for public benefits.  Timely and accurate income information is increasingly important to the State's ability to implement federal requirements and administer public benefit programs effectively.

     c.     Under existing systems, income information available to the State may not reflect an individual's current circumstances.  This can create unnecessary administrative burdens and contribute to both erroneous payments and delays or interruptions in benefits for individuals who remain eligible.

     d.    New Jersey should take advantage of information already available through existing State systems to modernize income verification and improve the administration of public benefits.  More timely information will allow the State to make more accurate and efficient eligibility determinations.

     e.     Automating income verification can reduce the need for individuals to repeatedly provide documentation concerning income that is otherwise available to the State, thereby reducing administrative burdens and helping to ensure that eligible individuals and families receive the benefits for which they qualify.

     f.     At the same time, improved income verification will strengthen program integrity by allowing the State to more promptly identify changes in eligibility and reduce erroneous or improper payments.

     g.    The Legislature finds that modernizing income verification is necessary to assist New Jersey in meeting the requirements and objectives established by H.R. 1, while improving the efficiency and effectiveness of the State's public benefit programs.

     h.    The Legislature finds that modernizing income verification can further assist New Jersey in reducing administrative burdens across a wide range of public benefits and services.

     i.     It is therefore the intent of the Legislature to establish a more timely and automated income-verification system that reduces payment errors and protects taxpayer resources while maximizing, to the maximum extent practicable, access to benefits and services for individuals and families who are eligible to receive them.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill enacts the Efficient Income Verification Act.  The bill requires employers to report wage information to the Department of Labor and Workforce Development in each pay period.  Current law requires employers to report that information quarterly.  The bill also requires employers to report the start and end dates of relevant pay periods, pay dates, pay frequency, and hours worked.

     The bill requires wage information to be shared with the Department of Human Services for the purpose of accurately verifying eligibility for public assistance.  The Department of Labor and Workforce Development is required to continually furnish or otherwise provide timely access to wage information, including, but not limited to, wages and hours worked, to the Department of Human Services to facilitate up-to-date and accurate income verification and eligibility determinations.

     The bill authorizes the Department of Labor and Workforce Development to share information reported by employers with any agency of this State for the exclusive purposes of determining eligibility for, and the accurate issuance of, public assistance or benefits, or for the automating or streamlining delivery of those benefits or services.  The bill also authorizes the Department of Labor and Workforce Development to enter into agreements with other agencies of this State to develop and implement systems which automate and streamline the delivery of public benefits or services.

     The bill requires the Department of Labor and Workforce Development, in consultation with the New Jersey Innovation Authority and the other departments or agencies that administer government benefits or services, to provide a report to the Governor and to the Legislature regarding options to expand income reporting to support more comprehensive, automated income verification for secure and accurate administration of government benefits or services.
