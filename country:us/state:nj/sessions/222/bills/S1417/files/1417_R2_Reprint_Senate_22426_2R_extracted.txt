CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As amended by the Senate on February 24, 2026. An Act concerning arbitration 2[proceedings] hearings2 and supplementing P.L.1983, c.358 (C.39:6A-24 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  A party to an arbitration 2[proceeding] hearing2 concerning a claim for personal injury protection 2[coverage] benefits as established by section 24 of P.L.1998, c.21 (C.39:6A-5.1)2 shall be represented by an attorney licensed to practice law in the State1, retained at the partys own expense1. b.    2[As used in this section, personal injury protection coverage means the same as section 4 of P.L.1972, c.70 (C.39:6A-4).] This section shall not apply to a pro se litigant, who is a litigant appearing on the litigants own behalf.2 2.    This act shall take effect on the 90th day next following 1the date of1 enactment and shall apply to arbitration 2[proceedings] hearings2 that are initiated on or after that date.
