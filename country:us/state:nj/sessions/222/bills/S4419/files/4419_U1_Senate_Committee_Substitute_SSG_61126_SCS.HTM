SYNOPSIS

     Everyone Counts in New Jersey Act; establishes New Jersey Census Complete Count Commission, New Jersey Office of Census, and New Jersey Census Complete Count Trust Fund.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     Substitute as adopted by the Senate State Government, Wagering, Tourism & Historic Preservation Committee.

  

 

 

An Act establishing the Everyone Counts in New Jersey Act for the purpose of ensuring a complete and accurate count of the States population each federal decennial census and supplementing chapter 4 of Title 52 of the Revised Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    This act shall be known and may be cited as the Everyone Counts in New Jersey Act.

 

     2.    a.  Within 60 days after the effective date of P.L.   , c.   (C.    ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), and not later than April 1 of the fourth year preceding the conduct of each federal decennial census thereafter, there shall be created in, but not of, the Department of State, the New Jersey Complete Count Commission.

     b.    The commission shall consist of 29 members as follows:

     (1)  the President of the Senate, or a designee;

     (2)  the Speaker of the General Assembly, or a designee;

     (3)  the Minority Leader of the Senate, or a designee;

     (4)  the Minority Leader of the General Assembly, or a designee;

     (5)  two members of the New Jersey Legislative Latino Caucus, one of whom shall be a member of the Senate and one of whom shall be a member of the General Assembly, to be appointed by the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the General Assembly, respectively, at the recommendation of the caucus chairperson;

     (6)  two members of the New Jersey Legislative Black Caucus, one of whom shall be a member of the Senate and one of whom shall be a member of the General Assembly, to be appointed by the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the General Assembly, respectively, at the recommendation of the caucus chairperson;

     (7)  two members of the New Jersey Legislative Asian-American and Pacific Islander Caucus, one of whom shall be a member of the Senate and one of whom shall be a member of the General Assembly, to be appointed by the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the General Assembly, respectively, at the recommendation of the caucus chairperson;

     (8)  the Governor, or a designee;

     (9)  the Secretary of State, or a designee;

     (10) the Commissioner of the Department of Labor and Workforce Development, or a designee;

     (11) three individuals representing municipalities in this State, reflecting the geographic diversity of the State, appointed by the Secretary of State;

     (12) four individuals representing counties in this State, reflecting the geographic diversity of the State, appointed by the Governor;

     (13) four representatives from different organizations representing the interests of communities of color in the State, appointed by the Secretary of State;

     (14) three representatives from different organizations representing the interests of businesses in the State, including one organization representing minority business interests, appointed by the Governor; and

     (15) two individuals representing community-based, philanthropic, non-profit, educational, civic, and faith-based organizations, appointed by the Secretary of State.

     c.    No more than 16 of the members appointed to the commission shall be of the same political party, as determined by the members political party affiliation during the one year preceding the members appointment to the commission.

     d.    All appointments to the commission shall be made within 90 days after the day on which the commission is established for the next federal decennial census.  Vacancies in the membership of the commission shall be filled in the same manner as the original appointments were made.  Members of the commission shall serve without compensation but shall be entitled to reimbursement for any actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties, within the limits of funds appropriated or otherwise made available to the commission for its purposes.

 

     3.    a.  The commission shall organize within 30 days after the appointment of its members.  The Secretary of State shall serve as chairperson of the commission.  The commission shall select a vice chairperson from among its members.  The commission may also appoint a secretary, a director, and an assistant director, who need not be a member of the commission.  The director and assistant director may be compensated from funds made available to the commission for this purpose.  

     b.    The commission shall meet at the call of the chairperson, or upon the request of any 15 members of the commission, who shall constitute a quorum.

     c.    To assist in carrying out its duties, the commission may create and appoint subcommittees as it deems appropriate and shall solicit participation from relevant experts and practitioners involved in federal census issues.

     d.    Each commission shall expire on December 31 of the year in which the federal decennial census occurs.

 

     4.    The commission shall coordinate with the New Jersey Office of the Census, established pursuant to section 7 of P.L.    , c.    (C.    ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), to accomplish its obligations and objectives.  The Department of State shall provide the staff services necessary to support the functions of the commission.  The commission shall be entitled to call to its assistance and avail itself of the services of the employees of any other State department, board, bureau, commission, or agency as it may require and as may be available to it for its purposes.

 

     5.    a.  The commission shall develop, recommend, and assist in the administration of a census outreach strategy to encourage full participation in the federal decennial census of population required by section 141 of Title 13 of the United States Code.  The census outreach strategy shall be designed to ensure an accurate and complete count of all persons in New Jersey.

     b.  The census outreach strategy shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

     (1) State agency initiatives to encourage participation in the census of population;

     (2) the establishment and support of school-based outreach programs;

     (3) partnerships with non-profit, community-based organizations;

     (4) a multilingual and multicultural multi-media campaign, in accordance with the provisions of P.L.2023, c.263 (C.52:14-40 et seq.), which shall include information on the importance of completing the next census survey and may be widely disseminated throughout the State through print, audio, television, the Internet, and social media;

     (5) at least three public hearings, one in each of the northern, central, and southern regions of the State, to receive testimony from the public, elected officials, experts, and others on ways to ensure a full census count of the population in this State for the next federal decennial census; and

     (6) a plan to ensure that hard-to-count and historically undercounted populations, such as African Americans, Hispanics or Latinos, Asian Americans, Middle Eastern and North African persons, non-native English speakers, persons who are indigent, persons who are homeless, college students, children under five years of age, men between the ages of 18 and 49, the elderly, persons displaced by natural disasters, persons who are incarcerated, and members of the LGBTQ+ community are counted, and that immigrant populations in this State understand the confidentiality of the census survey and the importance of completing the federal decennial census regardless of citizenship status.

     c.    The outreach strategy shall be coordinated through the Office of the Secretary of State, which shall provide administrative support to the commission and coordinate with all State agencies and constitutional officers, as well as units of local government, to identify effective methods of outreach to New Jerseys population and to provide resources to ensure that the outreach strategy is successful and that all New Jersey residents are counted.  All State agencies shall provide the Office of the Secretary of State with the information for that agencys designated census coordinator, cooperate with the commission, and provide support to the commission.  For the purposes of this subsection, State agencies means any executive agency or department directly responsible to the Governor.

     Other entities of government, including other constitutional officers, the offices of the legislative and judicial branches, and units of local government, shall cooperate and provide all reasonable assistance to the commission.

 

     6.    The commission shall submit an interim report to the Governor, and to the Legislature pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), within eight months of its organizational meeting containing its recommended outreach strategy developed pursuant to section 5 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).  Thereafter, the commission shall submit its final report to the Governor, and to the Legislature pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), no later than June 30 of the second year before the upcoming federal decennial census containing its recommended outreach strategy developed pursuant to section 5 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).

 

     7.    a. There is established in the Department of State the New Jersey Office of the Census. The Secretary of State shall appoint a director of the office to accomplish the responsibilities set forth in this section.  The director shall receive an annual salary determined by the Secretary of State within the limit of funds appropriated to the office for such purposes and shall serve at the pleasure of the Secretary of State.

     The Secretary of State shall also appoint an official State demographer within the New Jersey Office of the Census to coordinate census preparation efforts, including those listed under paragraph (8) of subsection c. of this section, and publish any follow-up reports for the federal decennial census, the American Community Survey, and any other relevant federal or State survey that may be necessary for demographic and census data collection.

     b.    The Secretary of State shall have the power to assign functions to the office and may appoint staff, agents, and consultants, prescribe the duties of the office, and fix their compensation within the limit of funds appropriated for such purposes.  The Secretary of State shall endeavor to obtain the services of experts in the State with expertise and experience in census enumeration, process, education, and promotion.

     c.    The New Jersey Office of the Census shall have the power and responsibility to:

     (1) promote a government-wide effort to achieve a complete count of New Jersey residents for each federal decennial census;

     (2) coordinate with the New Jersey Complete Count Commission, as established by section 2 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), to complete the obligations of the commission;

     (3) assist in the administration of census outreach efforts to encourage a complete count, including providing staff and consultant assistance to the New Jersey Complete Count Commission, as established by section 2 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), and the administration of State-funded grant programs to support local governments and non-profit organizations that conduct census outreach efforts;

     (4) support United States Census Bureau outreach efforts to facilitate quality public data gathering;

     (5) provide information in languages accessible to accommodate New Jerseys diverse population in accordance with the provisions of P.L.2023, c.263 (C.52:14-40 et seq.);

     (6) liaise with local governments through the Division of Local Government Services in the Department of Community Affairs regarding census efforts and local complete count efforts;

     (7) coordinate Census Day events throughout the State;

     (8) coordinate with the New Jersey State Data Center and the Office of Research and Information in the Department of Labor and Workforce Development and the Office of Information Technology, when applicable, to:

     (a) participate in the federal Local Update of Census Addresses;

     (b) develop and maintain a street address maintenance program;

     (c) support federal census operations;

     (d) complete demographic analyses; and

     (e) conduct research for the promotion of a fair and full count before the next federal decennial census;

     (9) develop partnerships with community organizations focused on a complete count, including, but not limited to, philanthropic organizations and foundations dedicated to a full and complete federal census count;

     (10) prepare annual population estimates for the State, counties, and local governments;

     (11) coordinate and oversee Statewide submissions for census programs, including the Local Update of Census Addresses, Census Question Resolution, annual Boundary and Annexation Survey, Participant Statistical Areas Program, and Building Permit Survey;

     (12) convene and maintain a working group of State agency statisticians and university researchers to conduct ongoing research on State demographic data trends and sources;

     (13) evaluate the demographic impact of policy proposals;

     (14) implement evidence-based policymaking;

     (15) for each federal decennial census, develop a timeline, to be published by January 15 of the fifth year before the upcoming census, except that the timeline for the 2030 federal decennial census shall be published by February 15, 2027, to guide the work of the office and of the New Jersey Complete Count Commission, established pursuant to section 2 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), that includes, but is not limited to:

     (a)   a review of the previous decades census numbers and the accuracy of such numbers;

     (b)  working with localities to correct census numbers as appropriate and consistent with applicable law;

     (c) releasing demographic profiles for local governments;

     (d) promoting participation by New Jerseyans in the American Community Survey;

     (e) identifying the best practices and shortcomings from the previous decades census efforts;

     (f) educating local governments and stakeholders about the United States Census Bureaus Local Update of Census Addresses, and assisting with participation in the program;

     (g) review census communications for the upcoming federal decennial census;

     (h) developing test programs to gauge residents awareness of the federal decennial census and conducting outreach to hard-to-count communities regarding the upcoming federal decennial census;

     (i) participating in end-to-end census test;

     (j) reviewing the United States Census Bureaus Local Update of Census Addresses results for accuracy;

     (k) developing census messaging and grant programs to support efforts of local governments and public interest stakeholders to engage in census outreach efforts;

     (l) developing and launching state education and communication campaigns;

     (m) providing census outreach materials in multiple languages in accordance with the provisions of P.L.2023, c.263 (C.52:14-40 et seq.); and

     (n) implementing an overall comprehensive census plan to ensure a complete count of all New Jersey households;

     (16) coordinate with the New Jersey Complete Count Commission to develop and promulgate a New Jersey Census Bill of Rights and Responsibilities no later than one year after the effective date of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), which shall affirm the rights of all New Jerseyans to:

     (a) participate in the federal decennial census and other important censuses free from threat or intimidation;

     (b) maintain confidentiality of the information provided in a census form or by verbal response;

     (c) respond to a census by means of either electronic or written responses, or by telephone;

     (d) request language assistance; and

     (e) verify the identity of a census worker;

     (17) translate the New Jersey Census Bill of Rights and Responsibilities in languages other than English, consistent with the provisions of P.L.2023, c.263 (C.52:14-40 et seq.);

     (18) make the New Jersey Census Bill of Rights and Responsibilities available on its Internet website and available to the public for uses such as inclusion on city and county census Internet websites and census questionnaire assistance center Internet websites; and

     (19) any other actions as determined by the Secretary of the State to be necessary to carry out the responsibilities of the office.

 

     8.    The New Jersey Office of the Census shall provide any materials and data requested, including but not limited to demographic analyses and population estimates completed in accordance with the offices duties, to be included in a publicly accessible database maintained and administered by the Department of State.

 

     9.    There is established in the Department of State a New Jersey Census Complete Count Trust Fund into which shall be appropriated from the State General Fund the sum of $1 million annually. Moneys in the fund shall be invested in permitted investments or shall be held in interest-bearing accounts in those depositories as the State Treasurer may select, and may be invested and reinvested in permitted investments or as other trust funds in the custody of the State Treasurer in the manner provided by law.  All interest or other income or earnings derived from the investment or reinvestment of moneys in the fund shall be credited to the fund.  Moneys in the fund shall be used by the Department of Sate to fund State, local, and community efforts to achieve the full count of the States population in each federal decennial census.

 

     10.  This act shall take effect immediately.
