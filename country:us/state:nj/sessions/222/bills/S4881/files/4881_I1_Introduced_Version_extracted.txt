S4881 SENATE, No. 4881 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  LINDA R. GREENSTEIN District 14 (Mercer and Middlesex) SYNOPSIS Establishes Data Center Community Intervenor Compensation Grant Program and fund in DCA to provide financial assistance for certain data center-related services; appropriates $10 million. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning financial compensation for the provision of certain services related to data centers, supplementing Title 52 of the Revised Statutes, and making an appropriation. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  There is established in the Department of Community Affairs a Data Center Community Intervenor Compensation Grant Program and a non-lapsing fund that shall be known as the Data Center Community Intervenor Compensation Fund.  The grant program and the fund shall be administered by the Division of Housing and Community Resources in the Department of Community Affairs. b.    The fund shall consist of: the monies appropriated by the Legislature for inclusion in the fund; investment earnings of the fund; and monies contributed to the fund by private sources, to be used for the purposes of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).  The monies in the fund shall be invested and reinvested by the director of the Division of Investment in the Department of the Treasury. c.     The purpose of the fund shall be to provide financial assistance, in the form of grant awards, to community interest representatives, including but not limited to, local units and their departments and offices, nonprofit organizations, and qualified community associations, as determined by the Department of Community Affairs, which intervene in the legal proceedings related to any economic, environmental, social welfare, or other community-related issues concerning data centers and their development, including, but not limited to, zoning, siting, permitting, impact assessments, public noticing, community benefits agreements, and construction. 2.    The Division of Housing and Community Resources in the Department of Community Affairs shall: a.     establish criteria for the determination of eligibility for financial assistance, in the form of grant awards, from the fund; b.    establish procedures for determining the amount of each grant award, based on the services provided and financial expenditures of the applicant and the resources available in the fund, to meet the applicants financial needs; c.     solicit and raise private monies to finance the fund; and d.    receive and disburse contributions to the fund as may be forthcoming from private and public sources. 3.    a.         To participate in the grant program, an applicant shall submit an application to the Division of Housing and Community Resources in the Department of Community Affairs in a manner and on such forms as determined by the director of the division.  An application shall include information detailing the applicants proposed uses for the grant funding, including, but not limited to: (1)   the amount of the grant award being sought; and (2)   a specific description of the types of services provided, including any expenditures, related to the zoning, siting, permitting, impact assessments, public noticing, community benefits agreements, and construction of data centers. b.    The Division of Housing and Community Resources shall review the applications submitted pursuant to subsection a. of this section and shall award grant funding to eligible applicants.  The division shall establish an annual maximum grant award amount to ensure that the maximum number of program applicants receive a grant award, given available funding. 4.    The Division of Housing and Community Resources in the Department of Community Affairs shall submit an annual report to the Governor and, pursuant to section 2 of P.L.1991, c.164 (C.52:14-19.1), to the Legislature that shall include a description for the preceding year of: the total amount of financial assistance that was provided as grant awards to eligible applicants; the total number of applicants who received a grant award under the fund; the types of services, and specific amounts, for which the grants were awarded; and recommendations that the division deems appropriate and necessary concerning the operations of the grant program and oversight of the fund. 5.    There is appropriated from the General Fund to the Data Center Community Intervenor Compensation Fund, established pursuant to section 1 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill), the sum of $10,000,000 for the purpose of providing financial assistance, in the form of grant awards, to community interest representatives, including but not limited to, local units and their departments and offices, nonprofit organizations, and qualified community associations, which intervene in the legal proceedings related to any economic, environmental, social welfare, or other community-related issues concerning data centers and their development, including, but not limited to, zoning, siting, permitting, impact assessments, public noticing, community benefits agreements, and construction. 6.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the fourth month next following the date of enactment, but the Director of the Division of Housing and Community Resources in the Department of Community Affairs, in consultation with the Commissioner of Community Affairs, may take any anticipatory action in advance of that date as may be necessary to implement the provisions of this act. STATEMENT This bill establishes in the Department of Community Affairs (DCA) a Data Center Community Intervenor Compensation Grant Program (program) and a non-lapsing fund known as the Data Center Community Intervenor Compensation Fund (fund).  The grant program and fund will be administered by the Division of Housing and Community Resources (division).  The purpose of the fund is to provide financial assistance, in the form of grant awards, to community interest representatives, including but not limited to, local units and their departments and offices, nonprofit organizations, and qualified community associations, as determined by the department, which intervene in the legal proceedings related to any economic, environmental, social welfare, or other community-related issues concerning data centers and their development, including, but not limited to, zoning, siting, permitting, impact assessments, public noticing, community benefits agreements, and construction. To participate in the grant program, an applicant is required to submit an application to the division in a form and manner as determined by the director of the division.  An application is required to include information detailing the applicants proposed uses for the grant funding, including, but not limited to: the amount of the grant award being sought; and a specific description of the types of services provided, including any expenditures, related to the zoning, siting, permitting, impact assessments, public noticing, community benefits agreements, and construction of data centers. The bill also provides that the division is required to submit an annual report to the Governor and the Legislature that contains information on the uses of the fund over the preceding year, including: the total amount of financial assistance that was provided as grant awards to eligible applicants; the total number of applicants who received a grant award under the fund; the types of services, and specific amounts, for which the grants were awarded; and recommendations that the division deems appropriate and necessary concerning the operations of the grant program and oversight of the fund. The bill appropriates $10 million to the fund from the General Fund for the purposes specified in the bill.
