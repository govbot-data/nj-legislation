AJR194 ASSEMBLY JOINT RESOLUTION No. 194 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  DAN HUTCHISON District 4 (Atlantic, Camden and Gloucester) SYNOPSIS Designates third week of January of each year as Security Technician Week in New Jersey. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. A Joint Resolution designating the third week of January of each year as Security Technician Week in New Jersey. Whereas, Security technicians play a vital role in protecting homes, businesses, schools, and other critical infrastructure in the State; and Whereas, Security technicians design, install, maintain, and service physical and electronic security systems, including fire alarms, intrusion detection systems, access control systems, and video surveillance technologies, that support public safety and emergency response efforts; and Whereas, The rapid evolution of technology requires security technicians to anticipate challenges, adapt to new threats, and innovate solutions; and Whereas, In addition to providing specialized technical support, security technicians also conduct risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities in existing security frameworks and implement strategies to mitigate them; and Whereas, Security technicians work closely with public officials to develop and implement emergency response plans to effectively handle security incidents; and Whereas, Security technicians also maintain familiarity with State and federal regulations to ensure security systems comply with these regulations; and Whereas, Security technicians often work behind the scenes, but their work is instrumental in ensuring the functionality, reliability, and effectiveness of security systems that safeguard communities throughout the State; now, therefore, Be It Resolved by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    The third week of January of each year is designated as Security Technician Week in New Jersey to recognize the critical role security technicians play in protecting the people and infrastructure of this State. 2.    The Governor is respectfully requested to issue a proclamation calling upon public officials and citizens of this State to observe Security Technician Week with appropriate activities and programs. 3.    This joint resolution shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This joint resolution designates the third week of January of each year as Security Technician Week in New Jersey to recognize the critical role security technicians play in protecting the people and infrastructure of this State.  Security technicians play a vital role in protecting homes, businesses, schools, and other critical infrastructure in the State.  Security technicians design, install, maintain, and service physical and electronic security systems, including fire alarms, intrusion detection systems, access control systems, and video surveillance technologies, that support public safety and emergency response efforts. The rapid evolution of technology requires security technicians to anticipate challenges, adapt to new threats, and innovate solutions.  In addition to providing specialized technical support, security technicians also conduct risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities in existing security frameworks and implement strategies to mitigate them.  Security technicians work closely with public officials to develop and implement emergency response plans to effectively handle security incidents.  Security technicians also maintain familiarity with State and federal regulations to ensure security systems comply with these regulations.  Security technicians often work behind the scenes, but their work is instrumental in ensuring the functionality, reliability, and effectiveness of security systems that safeguard communities throughout the State.  This joint resolution recognizes the dedication of security technicians to protecting the people and infrastructure of this State.
