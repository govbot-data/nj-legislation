SYNOPSIS

     Establishes first-time homebuyer savings program and provides gross income tax benefits for accounts established under the program.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act providing assistance for first-time homebuyers and supplementing Title 55 of the Revised Statutes and Title 54A of the New Jersey Statutes.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    As used in sections 1 through 6 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill):

     Certified first-time homebuyer means an individual or individuals certified as a first-time homebuyer by the New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency in accordance with section 3 of P.L.    , c.    (C.       ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).

     Eligible expense means a payment made to a mortgagor, construction contractor, or other vendor hired by a certified first-time homebuyer to perform work at a qualified residential property.

     Executive director means the Executive Director of the New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency.

     Financial institution means a State or federally chartered bank, savings bank, savings and loan association, credit union, or other entity held out to the public as a place of deposit of funds or medium of savings or investment that has been approved by the New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency.

     First-time homebuyer savings account means a savings, deposit, investment, or other brokerage account insured under federal or State law that is opened by a certified first-time homebuyer with a financial institution that has entered into an agreement with the New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency in accordance with section 2 of P.L.     , c.    (C.       ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill).

     Principal residence means a qualified residential property actually and continually occupied by the certified first-time homebuyer as the certified first-time homebuyers permanent residence, as distinguished from a vacation home, property owned and rented or offered for rent by the certified first-time homebuyer, and other secondary real property holdings.

     Qualified home purchase means the acquisition, by sale, of a qualified residential property located in this State which is occupied as a certified first-time homebuyers principal residence; or the construction of a new qualified residential property located in this State paid for by the certified first-time homebuyer and occupied as the certified first-time homebuyers principal residence.

     Qualified residential property means a dwelling house; a condominium unit under the form of real property ownership provided for under the Condominium Act, P.L.1969, c.257 (C.46:8B-1 et seq.); a unit in a cooperative or mutual housing corporation; a unit in a horizontal property regime under the form of real property ownership provided under the Horizontal Property Act, P.L.1963, c.168 (C.46:8A-1 et seq.); a unit in a continuing care retirement community; or a manufactured home that is taxable as real property or that is installed in a mobile home park.

 

     2.    a.  The executive director shall establish a program to encourage first-time homeownership by providing certified first-time homebuyers with access to savings accounts for costs related to the purchase of a qualified residential property. 

     b.    The executive director shall enter into an agreement with each financial institution that expresses to the agency in writing an interest in establishing and maintaining first-time homebuyer savings accounts under the program.  Each agreement shall, at minimum, contain provisions to:

     (1)   allow a certified first-time homebuyer to open a savings, deposit, investment, or other brokerage account with the institution where cash or other marketable securities may be contributed;

     (2)   require the transfer of any assets that may be held in another similar first-time homebuyer savings account maintained by a certified first-time homebuyer through a comparable program in this State or any other state into an account established with the financial institution under the program created pursuant to P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill);

     (3)   limit the amount of cash or other marketable securities that may be contributed to an account during each taxable year to no more than $20,000 for an individual accountholder, and $40,000 for a joint account owned by two certified first-time homebuyers who are married;

     (4)   limit the aggregate amount of cash or other marketable securities that may be contributed to an account during all taxable years to no more than $200,000;

     (5)   allow a certified first-time homebuyer to make withdrawals or distributions of assets in a first-time homebuyer savings account, without penalty, to pay for an eligible expense incurred by the certified first-time homebuyer for a qualified home purchase;

     (6)   notify a certified first-time homebuyer that it is their responsibility to report and pay any taxes or penalties that may become due as a result of a withdrawal or distribution that is used for any purpose other than an eligible expense;

     (7)   provide a schedule of account fees to be paid by certified first-time homebuyers;

     (8)   allow the executive director to amend the agreement as needed; and

     (9)   specify the conditions under which the agreement may be terminated.

 

     3.    a.  The executive director shall establish an application process to certify individuals who seek to open a first-time homebuyer savings account as certified first-time homebuyers.  The form and manner of each application submitted pursuant to this section shall be as prescribed by the executive director.  At minimum, an individual applicant or applicants, as the case may be, shall be required to provide information concerning:

     (1)   the state or jurisdiction where each applicant currently resides;

     (2)   the home ownership history of each applicant;

     (3)   the purposes for which assets maintained in a first-time homebuyer savings account will be used;

     (4)   any homebuyer education courses completed by each applicant; and

     (5)   any other first-time homebuyer savings accounts maintained through the program established pursuant to P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill) or through any comparable program in this State, or in any other state or jurisdiction.

     b.    The executive director shall review each completed application that is submitted pursuant to subsection a. of this section and shall issue written certification that an applicant or applicants are a certified first-time homebuyer if the executive director determines that the applicant or applicants:

     (1)   reside in the State or will reside in the State after making a qualified home purchase;

     (2)   have not made a qualified home purchase, or owned residential property in this State, or any other state or jurisdiction;

     (3)   will use assets maintained in a first-time homebuyer savings account to pay eligible expenses incurred by the certified first-time homebuyer for a qualified home purchase;

     (4)   have completed a homebuyer education course approved by the executive director during the taxable year in which the application was filed, or during any of the three taxable years immediately preceding such year; and

     (5)   do not maintain any other first-time homebuyer savings accounts through any comparable program in this State, or any other state or jurisdiction, or will terminate an account maintained through a comparable program within 90 days of the date written certification is issued.

     c.     A certified first-time homebuyer shall provide a copy of the written certification issued by the executive director pursuant to subsection b. of this section to the financial institution administering a first-time homebuyer savings account before the financial institution opens the account for the certified first-time homebuyer. 

 

     4.    a.  The executive director shall establish a process for a financial institution to provide monthly reports, in a form and manner prescribed by the executive director, the following information regarding each first-time homebuyer savings accounts maintained by the financial institution for certified first-time homebuyers:

     (1)   the date the account was opened;

     (2)   the aggregate amount of any assets transferred to the account from any other financial institution during the prior month;

     (3)   the date and the amount of each withdrawal or distribution of assets made during the prior month; and

     (4)   the fair market value of all assets maintained in the account as of the close of business on the last day of the prior month.

     b.    A report submitted pursuant to subsection a. of this section shall be filed with the executive director no later than 30 days following the conclusion of the prior month.  The executive director shall transmit a copy of each report filed by a financial institution to the Director of the Division of Taxation in the Department of the Treasury within 30 days of the date the report is filed with the executive director.

     (c)   pay fees charged to the certified first-time homebuyer by the financial institution for the establishment or maintenance of the account; or

     (d)   pay expenses attributable to the death or disability of a certified first-time homebuyer or their spouse.

     (2)   In the event that a certified first-time homebuyer fails to submit a report pursuant to subsection a. of this section, a penalty shall be imposed on the first-time homebuyer in accordance with paragraph (1) of subsection b. of this section regardless of the purpose for which a withdrawal or distribution from a first-time homebuyer savings account was made.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill establishes a first-time homebuyer savings program and provides certain gross income tax benefits for account contributions and distributions.

 

First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account Program

 

     Under the bill, the first-time homebuyer savings program would be established by the Executive Director of the New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency to encourage first-time homeownership by providing certified first-time homebuyers with access to savings accounts for costs related to the purchase of qualified residential property.  Specifically, the executive director would be required to enter into agreements with each financial institution that expresses an interest in establishing and maintaining first-time homebuyer savings accounts created under the program.  Among other requirements set forth in the bill, each agreement entered into by the executive director with a financial institution would be required to:

     (1)   limit the amount of cash or other marketable securities that may be contributed to an account during each taxable year to no more than $20,000 for an individual accountholder, and $40,000 for a joint account owned by two certified first-time homebuyers who are married;

     (2)   limit the aggregate amount of cash or other marketable securities that may be contributed to an account during all taxable years to no more than $200,000; and

     (3)   allow a certified first-time homebuyer to make withdrawals or distributions of assets in a first-time homebuyer savings account, without penalties, to pay for an eligible expense incurred by the certified first-time homebuyer for a qualified home purchase.

     The bill defines an eligible expense as a payment made to a mortgagor, a construction contractor, or other vendor hired by a certified first-time homebuyer to perform work at a qualified residential property.  The bill defines qualified residential property to include various types of dwellings commonly used as residences.  The bill defines a qualified home purchase as the acquisition, by sale, of a qualified residential property located in this State which is occupied as a certified first-time homebuyers principal residence; or the construction of a new qualified residential property located in this State paid for by the certified first-time homebuyer and occupied as the certified first-time homebuyers principal residence.

     In addition, the executive director would also establish an application process to certify individuals seeking to open first-time homebuyer savings accounts as certified first-time homebuyers.  An individual or individuals would be certified as a first-time homebuyer if they:

     (1)   reside in the State or will reside in the State after making a qualified home purchase;

     (2)   have not made a qualified home purchase, or owned residential property in this State, or any other state or jurisdiction;

     (3)   will use assets maintained in a first-time homebuyer savings account to pay eligible expenses incurred by the certified first-time homebuyer for a qualified home purchase;

     (4)   have completed a homebuyer education course approved by the executive director during the taxable year in which the application was filed, or any of the three taxable years immediately preceding such year; and

     (5)   do not maintain any other first-time homebuyer savings accounts through any comparable program in this State, or any other state or jurisdiction, or will terminate an account maintained through a comparable program within 90 days of the date written certification is issued.

     A certified first-time homebuyer would be required to provide a copy of the written certification to the financial institution administering a first-time homebuyer savings account before the financial institution would be able to open an account.

     The bill also requires the executive director to establish a process by which a financial institution participating in the program would provide monthly reports on certain information concerning each first-time homebuyer savings account maintained by the institution for certified first-time homebuyers.  The report would include, for each account, the date the account was opened, the amount of assets transferred to the account, the date and amount of each withdrawal or distribution, and the fair market value of the assets in the account.  The report would be filed with the executive director and a copy transmitted to the Director of the Division of Taxation. 

     The executive director would further be required to establish a process for certified first-time homebuyers to submit reports within one month following each withdrawal or distribution made from their account.  A 10 percent penalty would be imposed by the executive director if, upon review of a report, it is determined that a withdrawal or distribution of assets was for any purpose other than to pay eligible expenses incurred for a qualified home purchase, transfer assets to another similar first-time homebuyer savings account, pay fees charged by a financial institution that maintains the account, or pay expenses attributable to the death or disability of a certified first-time homebuyer or their spouse.  The penalty would also be imposed if this report is not filed, regardless of the purpose of a withdrawal or distribution.

 

Gross Income Tax Benefits for Certified First-Time Homebuyers with Program Accounts

 

     The bill also provides certain gross income tax benefits for certified first-time homebuyers with accounts created under the program.  The bill provides a gross income tax deduction for certain contributions made to a first-time homebuyer savings account.  Specifically, an individual taxpayer would be allowed to deduct up to $20,000 in contributions made to an account during a taxable year while taxpayers who are married and filing jointly would be allowed to deduct up to $40,000 during a taxable year.

     The bill also excludes qualified distributions from the gross income of the taxpayer making the distribution.  The bill further specifies that the net profits from the sale of any property held in a first-time homebuyer savings account would not be considered taxable income until the year in which a distribution is made, except as otherwise excluded if the monies are withdrawn for a qualified distribution.  For purposes of the exclusion, a qualified distribution is defined as a withdrawal or distribution from a first-time homebuyer savings account to:

     (1)   pay eligible expenses incurred by the certified first-time homebuyer for a qualified home purchase;

     (2)   transfer assets to another first-time homebuyer savings account or to another similar account maintained through a comparable program in this State or another state or jurisdiction;

     (3)   pay fees charged to the certified first-time homebuyer by the financial institution for the establishment or maintenance of the account; or

     (4)   make a withdrawal or distribution attributable to the death or disability of a certified first-time homebuyer or their spouse.

     When filing a return for any taxable year in which a withdrawal or distribution is made from a first-time homebuyer savings account, a taxpayer would be required to attach to their return copies of each report submitted to the New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency for a withdrawal or distribution that occurred during the taxable year.
