S3973 SENATE, No. 3973 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED MARCH 19, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  PARKER SPACE District 24 (Morris, Sussex and Warren) SYNOPSIS Requires continuing education for certain licensees relating to edition of National Electrical Code most recently adopted by Commissioner of Community Affairs. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning continuing education for certain licensees of the Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors and amending P.L.1999, c.10 and P.L.2001, c.21. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    Section 3 of P.L.1999, c.10 (C.45:5A-13.3) is amended to read as follows: 3.    The educational course of study required of licensed electrical contractors for each triennial registration period shall include 34 hours of continuing education as follows: a.     A 10-hour course of study relating to the most recent edition of the National Electrical Code that has been adopted on the date of licensure renewal by the Commissioner of Community Affairs pursuant to section 5 of P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-123), nine hours of which shall pertain to the code and one hour of which shall pertain to applicable State statutes and regulations; and b.    Twenty-four hours of instruction approved by the board covering one or more of the following subjects: (1)   Installation, erection, repair or alteration of electrical equipment for the generation, transmission or utilization of electrical energy; (2)   Transmission or utilization of electrical energy; (3)   Job estimating, management and business practices; (4)   Supervisory responsibilities required of licensees pursuant to the laws of this State; and (5)   Any other subject relevant to electrical contracting and construction as determined by the board. (cf: P.L.1999, c.10, s.3) 2.    Section 6 of P.L.2001, c.21 (C.45:5A-11.4) is amended to read as follows: 6. a. The board shall require each Class A journeyman electrician, other than a Class A journeyman electrician licensed to practice electrical contracting issued by the board, and each Class B wireman, as a condition for triennial license renewal pursuant to section 1 of P.L.1972, c.108 (C.45:1-7), to complete a 15-hour course of study relating to the most recent edition of the National Electrical Code adopted by the Commissioner of Community Affairs pursuant to section 5 of P.L.1975, c.217 (C.52:27D-123). b.    The board shall approve all programs of education for the 15-hour course of study established pursuant to subsection a. of this section and the instructors for those courses. (cf: P.L.2021, c.479, s.8) 3.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill requires certain licensees of the Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors to complete continuing education courses relating to the edition of the National Electrical Code adopted on the date of licensure renewal by the Commissioner of Community Affairs as the electrical subcode for New Jersey.  Specifically, the bill updates the continuing education requirements for electrical contractors, Class A journeyman electricians, and Class B wiremen licensed by the board.
