SYNOPSIS

     Provides for practice of dental hygiene without supervision pursuant to collaborative practice agreement with licensed dentist.

 

CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT

     As introduced.

  


An Act concerning dental hygienists and amending and supplementing various parts of the statutory law.

 

     Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey:

 

     1.    (New section)   The Legislature finds and declares that:

     a.     Oral health and overall physical health are inextricably linked, as oral health is a critical component of overall physical health and systemic health can in turn impact oral health.

     b.    Poor oral health can not only cause conditions such as cavities and tooth loss, but can also increase the risk of heart attacks, strokes, and Alzheimers disease and cause significant psychological and financial strain.

     c.     Access to oral health care services, including routine dental cleanings, is key to maintaining optimal oral health, encouraging overall physical health, and preventing avoidable financial and psychological costs.

     d.    Despite the importance of oral health care, over 200,000 New Jerseyans do not have adequate access to dental care and only three in ten adults in the State reported visiting a dentist or dental clinic in 2024.  Certain areas of the State face workforce shortages, and providers may additionally reserve a significant amount of their availability for specialized or emergent services.

     e.     Dental hygienists are highly educated and experienced in providing preventative oral health care through dental hygiene services but are currently required to work under the supervision and direction of a dentist.

     f.     Several states that previously had populations facing inadequate access to oral health care services similar to New Jersey have allowed dental hygienists to independently provide dental hygiene services to help close the gap in access to oral health care.

     g.    The Legislature therefore determines that it is in the public interest to broaden access to preventative oral health care and subsequently improve the oral and physical health of individuals across the State by providing for the practice of dental hygiene without dentist supervision.

     8.    (New section)  a.  To be eligible for certification as a collaborative practice dental hygienist, an applicant shall:

     (1)   be licensed to practice dental hygiene in the State and hold a certificate of registration issued by the board;

     (2)   complete at least 2,000 hours of dental hygiene services supervised by a licensed dentist; and

     (3)   hold a current certification in Basic or Advanced Cardiac Life Support by an association approved by the board.

     b.    Upon the submission of a written application, which shall include a copy of a signed collaborative practice agreement between the applicant and a licensed dentist and payment of a fee in an amount determined by the board, the board shall certify for collaborative practice any applicant who, in the opinion of the board, has satisfied the requirements for certification.

     c.     A collaborative practice agreement shall include:

     (1)   the name of the dental hygienist and the collaborating dentist;

     (2)   the address of the independent office, school, dental clinic, or institution where the dental hygienist is authorized to practice dental hygiene;

     (3)   provisions for consultation with the collaborating dentist, including a schedule for the periodic review of patient records, which shall occur at least quarterly;

     (4) provisions for the referral of each patient to the collaborating dentist, which shall occur at least annually;

     (5)   provisions for alternative coverage during the absence or incapacity of the collaborating dentist; and

     (6)   any other information as required by the board.

     d.    (1)  Certification as a collaborative practice dental hygienist shall be issued for a two-year period and may be renewed upon submission of a renewal application and payment of a renewal fee as determined by the board.

     (2)  The board may establish continuing education requirements for the renewal of a certification as a collaborative practice dental hygienist pursuant to this subsection.

 

     9.    (New section)  a.  A certified collaborative dental hygienist dental hygiene pursuant to a collaborative practice agreement shall:

     (1)   practice dental hygiene without the supervision of a licensed dentist only at the independent office, dental clinic, or institution named in the collaborative practice agreement;

     (2)   obtain signed consent from each patient advising the patient of the collaborative practice agreement and the dental hygienists scope of practice;

     (3)   consult with, and refer each patient to, the collaborating dentist according to the schedule established by the collaborative practice agreement pursuant to section 8 of P.L.    , c.    (C.        ) (pending before the Legislature as this bill);

     (4)   annually review the collaborative practice agreement with the collaborating dentist; and

     (5)   make the collaborative practice agreement available for inspection upon request.

     b.    A collaborating dentist shall:

     (1)   consult with the certified collaborative practice dental hygienist according to the schedule established by the collaborative practice agreement;

     (2)   annually review the collaborative practice agreement with the certified collaborative practice dental hygienist; and

     (3)   make the collaborative practice agreement available for inspection upon request.

     c.     A certified collaborative practice dental hygienist shall not enter into more than three collaborative practice agreements at one time.

STATEMENT

 

     This bill provides for the practice of dental hygiene without supervision by a licensed dentist pursuant to a collaborative practice agreement.

     Specifically, the bill establishes a certification for collaborative practice, which the New Jersey State Board of Dentistry is to issue to a licensed dental hygienist who submits to the board a signed collaborative practice agreement between the dental hygienist and a licensed dentist and meets certain other requirements.  Under a collaborative practice agreement, a dental hygienist certified for collaborative practice is to:

     (1)   practice dental hygiene without the supervision of a licensed dentist only at the independent office, dental clinic, or institution named in the collaborative practice agreement;

     (2)   obtain a signed consent from each patient advising the patient of the collaborative practice agreement and the dental hygienists scope of practice;

     (3)   consult with, and refer each patient to, the collaborating dentist according to the schedule established by the collaborative practice agreement;

     (4)   annually review the collaborative practice agreement with the collaborating dentist; and

     (5)   make the collaborative practice agreement available for inspection upon request.

     A collaborative practice agreement is to include:

     (1)   the name of the dental hygienist and the collaborating dentist;

     (2)   the address of the independent office, school, dental clinic, or institution where the dental hygienist is authorized to practice dental hygiene;

     (3)   provisions for consultation with the collaborating dentist, including a schedule for the periodic review of patient records, which shall occur at least quarterly;

     (4) provisions for the referral of each patient to the collaborating dentist, which shall occur at least annually;

     (5)   provisions for alternative coverage during the absence or incapacity of the collaborating dentist; and

     (6)   any other information as required by the board.

     Under the bill, certified collaborative practice dental hygienists and licensed dentists may enter into no more than three collaborative practice agreements at one time.
