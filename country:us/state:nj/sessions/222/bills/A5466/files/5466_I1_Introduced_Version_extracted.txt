STATEMENT This bill authorizes podiatric physicians to supervise physician assistants in the performance of podiatric medical services.  Under the bill, podiatric physicians are incorporated into various provisions of current law regulating the oversight provided now by State-licensed physicians when supervising a physician assistant.  The State Board of Medical Examiners is to determine, among other items, the podiatric medical and surgical procedures and services a physician assistant is to provide and perform in a podiatric medicine office, a health care facility, an institution, a veterans' home, or a private home.  The bill further stipulates that (1) it is misconduct for a podiatric physician who permits a physician assistant under the podiatric physicians supervision to practice contrary to the provisions of the current law governing physician assistants; and (2) in the performance of all practice-related activities, a physician assistant is to be conclusively presumed to be the agent of the podiatric physician under whose supervision the physician assistant is practicing. The bill also provides that a podiatric physician who supervises a physician assistant is given the option to maintain a written delegation agreement with the physician assistant but a physician assistant is required to sign a separate written agreement with each podiatric physician who delegates podiatric medical services pursuant to regulations as determined by the board.  The bill delineates what is to be included in the written agreement.
