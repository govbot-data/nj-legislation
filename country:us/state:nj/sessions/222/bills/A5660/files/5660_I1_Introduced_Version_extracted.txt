STATEMENT This bill would eliminate the statute of limitations for certain crimes of forgery and other fraudulent practices for which the parties involved have a history of domestic violence. Under current law, crimes of forgery and other fraudulent practices are set forth in chapter 21 of Title 2C of the New Jersey Statutes.  These crimes include, but are not limited to: forgery, frauds related to public records and recordable instruments, falsifying or tampering with records, destruction or falsification of records, and identity theft.  Generally, a prosecution for these crimes is required to be commenced within five years after the crime is committed. Under the bill, there is no time limit for initiating a prosecution for these crimes if the actor is the subject of or has had a temporary or final domestic violence restraining order entered against the actor, and the victim of the crime is the victim of domestic violence for which the order was entered; or at the time of the offense, the actor is or previously was the subject of a judicial order or protective order entered pursuant to N.J.S.A.2C:28-5.1 prohibiting contact with the victim of the offense.
