S4916 SENATE, No. 4916 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  ANGELA V. MCKNIGHT District 31 (Hudson) SYNOPSIS Establishes Non-Certificated School Employee Summer Assistance Program in DOE. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning payment of certain school employees and supplementing chapter 6 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    As used in this act: Department means the Department of Education. Program means the Non-Certificated School Employee Summer Assistance Program. Regular assignment period means the period during which regular class sessions are being held in a participating school district and excludes the summer recess period. Summer recess period means the period during which regular class sessions are not being held in a participating school district during the months of June, July, August, and September. 2.    a.  There is established in the Department of Education the Non-Certificated School Employee Summer Assistance Program. The purpose of the program shall be to provide non-certificated school district employees with up to one dollar for each dollar that the employee elects to withhold from the employees paychecks during the regular assignment period for future disbursement during the summer recess period pursuant to the provisions of the program. b.    (1)  A school district shall notify the Department of Education of its intention to participate in the program for the subsequent school year and the number of eligible employees in the district no later than January 1 of each year. Once a school district has notified the department of its intention to participate in the program, the school district shall be prohibited from reversing its decision to participate in the subsequent school year, provided that State funds are appropriated for the program. (2) A school district shall, no later than January 15 of each year, notify an eligible employee of the districts participation in the program in the subsequent school year and of the employees eligibility, contingent upon program appropriations. c.     An employee shall be eligible to participate in the program if the employee: (1) has been employed with the school district in a non-certificated position for at least one full school year prior to the last date on which an employee notifies the school district of participation in the program; (2) receives a salary that is less than or equal to $62,400 at the time of program enrollment; and (3) elects to withhold a portion of the employees paychecks during the regular assignment period for delayed disbursement during the summer recess period, in accordance with the provisions of N.J.S.18A:29-3. d.    An employee who elects to participate in the program in the following school year shall: (1) notify the school district within 90 days of the notification issued by the school district pursuant to paragraph (2) of subsection b. of this section; and (2) specify the percent of the monthly pay, not to exceed 10 percent, that the employee authorizes to be withheld during the regular assignment period. e.     The department shall notify a participating school district in writing, by August 1 during a fiscal year in which moneys are appropriated for purposes of this section, of the estimated amount of State match funding that a participating employee can expect to receive as a result of participating in the program. If the funding provided for purposes of this section is insufficient to provide a dollar for dollar match for amounts that have been withheld from a participating employees paychecks during the regular assignment period, the department shall notify the school district of the expected prorated amount of State match funds that a participating employee can expect to receive as a result of participating in the program. f.     A participating school district shall notify participating employees, by August 15 during a fiscal year in which State moneys are appropriated for purposes of this section, of the amount of estimated State match funds that a participating employee can expect to receive as a result of participating in the program. After receiving such notification, a participating employee may withdraw their election to participate in the program or reduce the amount to be withheld from their paycheck pursuant to subsection d. of this section by notifying the district no later than 30 days after the start of school instruction for the applicable school year. 3.    a. A non-certificated employee who separates from employment with a participating school district during the applicable school year may request from the district any pay withheld from their paycheck pursuant to this act. b.    A non-certificated employee, due to economic or personal hardship, may request from the participating school district any pay withheld from their paycheck pursuant to this act. c.     A non-certificated employee who requests any pay withheld by the participating school district pursuant to subsections a. and b. of this section shall not be entitled to receive any State match funds provided pursuant to this section. 4.    a.  The department shall apportion funds to participating school districts to provide the State match for program participation no later than 15 days from the end of the regular assignment period.  The apportionment of State match funds to be distributed pursuant to the program shall be determined for each participating district by the department on the basis of the amount that has been withheld from the paychecks of participating employees. b.    If the total amount requested by participating school districts exceeds the amount appropriated for purposes of this section, the department shall prorate the amount apportioned to participating school districts accordingly. c.     The department shall disburse the matching funds to participating districts no later than 21 days from the end of a participating districts regular assignment period. 5.    a. A participating school district shall pay a participating employee during the summer recess period the amounts withheld in accordance with the employees choices, plus the amount of State match funds that is attributable to the amount withheld from the employees paychecks during the regular assignment period and which has been disbursed pursuant to section 4 of this act.  Payment shall be disbursed to the employee no later than 28 days from the end of a participating districts regular assignment period. b. Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, the State match payment received by participating employees pursuant to this section shall not be considered compensation for purposes of determining State retirement benefits. 6.    There is established in the Department of Education a non-lapsing fund which shall be known as the Non-Certificated School Employee Summer Assistance Program Fund.  All monies appropriated annually by the Legislature, federal and other grants received by the State, and any other monies made available for the purposes of the fund shall be deposited in the fund.  Monies deposited in the fund shall be used by the Department of Education to provide State match funding for payments made to eligible non-certificated school employees pursuant to the provisions of this act. 7.    The State Board of Education shall promulgate rules and regulations pursuant to the "Administrative Procedure Act," P.L.1968, c.410 (C.52:14B-1 et seq.) necessary to effectuate the provisions of this act. 8.    The Legislature shall annually appropriate such funds as are necessary to implement this act. 9.    This act shall take effect immediately and shall first apply in the second full school year beginning after the date of enactment. STATEMENT This bill establishes in the Department of Education the Non-Certificated School Employee Summer Assistance Program. The purpose of the program will be to provide non-certificated school district employees with up to one dollar for each dollar the employee elects to withhold from the employees paychecks during the regular assignment period for future disbursement during the summer recess period pursuant to the provisions of the program. The bill establishes requirements for districts to notify the department and employees of participation in the program as well as eligibility requirements for employees.  Under the bill, an employee will be eligible to participate in the program if the employee: (1) has been employed with the school district in a non-certificated position for at least one full school year prior to the last date on which an employee may notify the school district of participation in the program; (2) receives a salary that is less than or equal to $62,400 at the time of program enrollment; and (3) elects to withhold a portion of the employees paychecks during the regular assignment period for delayed disbursement during the summer recess period. The bill requires an employee who elects to participate in the program in the following school year to: (1) notify the school district within a specified timeframe; and (2) specify the percent of the monthly pay, not to exceed ten percent, that the employee authorizes to be withheld during the regular assignment period. The bill directs the department to notify a participating school district in writing, by August 1 during a fiscal year in which moneys are appropriated, of the estimated amount of State match funding that a participating employee can expect to receive as a result of participating in the program.  If the funding provided is insufficient to provide a dollar for dollar match for amounts that have been withheld from participating employee paychecks, the department will notify the school district of the expected prorated amount of State match funds that a participating employee can expect to receive as a result of participating in the program. Under the bill, a participating school district will notify participating employees, by August 15 during a fiscal year in which State moneys are appropriated, the amount of estimated State match funds that a participating employee can expect to receive as a result of participating in the program. After receiving such notification, a participating employee may withdraw their election to participate in the program or reduce the amount to be withheld from their paycheck by notifying the district no later than 30 days after the start of school instruction for the applicable school year. The bill provides that a non-certificated employee who separates from employment with a participating school district during the applicable school year may request from the district any pay withheld from their paycheck pursuant to the bills provisions. The bill also provides that a non-certificated employee, due to economic or personal hardship, may request from the participating school district any pay withheld from their paycheck pursuant to the bills provisions.  Under these circumstances a non-certificated employee would not be entitled to receive any State match funds. The bill directs the department to apportion funds to participating school districts to provide the State match for program participation no later than 15 days from the end of the regular assignment period.  If the total amount requested by participating school districts exceeds the amount appropriated, the department is directed to prorate the amount apportioned to participating school districts accordingly. Finally, the department is required to disburse the matching funds to participating districts no later than 21 days from the end of a participating districts regular assignment period. The bill directs a participating school district to pay a participating employee during the summer recess period the amounts withheld in accordance with the employees choices, plus the amount of State match funds that is attributable to the amount withheld from that employees paychecks during the regular assignment period and which has been disbursed by the department. The bill provides that the State match funding received by participating employees will not be considered compensation for purposes of determining State retirement benefits. The bill directs the Department of Education to establish a non-lapsing fund which shall be known as the Non-Certificated School Employee Summer Assistance Program Fund.  Monies deposited in the fund shall be used by the Department of Education to provide funding for State match payments made to eligible non-certificated school employees awarded pursuant to the bills provisions.
