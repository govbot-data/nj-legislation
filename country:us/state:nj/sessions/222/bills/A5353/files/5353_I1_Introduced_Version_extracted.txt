A5353 ASSEMBLY, No. 5353 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED JUNE 30, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  CAROL A. MURPHY District 7 (Burlington) SYNOPSIS Appropriates $500,000 to New Jersey Ireland Trade Commission. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning the New Jersey Ireland Trade Commission and making an appropriation. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    There is appropriated from the General Fund to the New Jersey Ireland Trade Commission the sum of $500,000, for use by the commission to effectuate the purposes of P.L.2023, c.64 (C.52:16A-137 et seq.). 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill appropriates $500,000 to the New Jersey Ireland Trade Commission in order to carry out the commissions purposes as set forth in current law.
