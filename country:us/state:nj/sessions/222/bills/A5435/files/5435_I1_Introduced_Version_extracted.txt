A5435 ASSEMBLY, No. 5435 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblywoman  CAROL A. MURPHY District 7 (Burlington) SYNOPSIS Supplemental appropriation of $35 million from General Fund to DHS to support Crisis Receiving and Stabilization Centers within 9-8-8 Continuum of Care. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. A Supplement to the annual appropriations act for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, P.L.2026, c.    (pending before the Legislature as Senate Bill No. 2027 of 2026/2027 and Assembly Bill No. 5327 of 2026/2027). Be It Enacted by the Senate and the General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    In addition to the amounts appropriated under P.L.2026, c.   (pending before the Legislature as Senate Bill No. 2027 of 2026/2027 and Assembly Bill No. 5327 of 2026/2027), the annual appropriations act for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, there is appropriated from the General Fund the following amount for the purpose specified: 54 Department of Human Services 20 Physical and Mental Health 23 Behavioral Health Services 7700 Division of Mental Health and Addiction Services Grants-in-Aid 08-7700  Community Services .......................................... $35,000,000 Total Grants-in-Aid appropriation, Behavioral Health Services ........................... $35,000,000 Grants-in-Aid: 08    9-8-8 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.......... ($35,000,000) Of the amounts appropriated to the 9-8-8 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline account, $35,000,000 shall be allocated to support Crisis Receiving and Stabilization Centers operating within the 9-8-8 continuum of care. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT The bill makes a supplemental appropriation to the FY 2027 Appropriations Act of $35 million from the General Fund to the Division of Mental Health and Addiction Services in the Department of Human Services to support the States Crisis Receiving and Stabilization Centers.  Crisis Receiving and Stabilization Centers, one of three components that compose the 9-8-8 continuum of care, operate 24 hours a day/365 days a year and provide short-term crisis stabilization services to individuals experiencing mental health crises.
