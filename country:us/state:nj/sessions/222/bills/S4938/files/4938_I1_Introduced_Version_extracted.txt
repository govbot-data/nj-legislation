S4938 SENATE, No. 4938 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED NOVEMBER 23, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  KRISTIN M. CORRADO District 40 (Bergen, Essex and Passaic) SYNOPSIS Requires MVC and American Automobile Association to contract to allow for provision of certain motor vehicle services at American Automobile Association facilities. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning the provision of certain motor vehicle services and supplementing Title 39 of the Revised Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  Notwithstanding any law, rule, or regulation to the contrary, the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission shall enter into a contract with the American Automobile Association to allow for the provision of certain motor vehicle services to American Automobile Association customers at certain American Automobile Association facilities throughout the State.  The contract shall, at a minimum and to the extent practicable, allow for the following motor vehicle services to be provided at certain American Automobile Association facilities: (1)   motor vehicle registration renewals, replacements, and duplicates; (2)   the titling and registration of, and the issuance of temporary registrations for, used motor vehicles; (3)   drivers license renewals, duplicates, and upgrades to a REAL-ID license; (4)   non-driver identification card renewals, duplicates, and upgrades to a REAL-ID identification card; (5)   license plate surrenders; and (6)   vision tests. b.    No later than one month after the contract agreement is signed by the commission and the American Automobile Association, pursuant to subsection a. of this section, the commission shall publish on its Internet website information concerning the motor vehicle services to be offered at American Automobile Association facilities throughout the State pursuant to subsection a. of this section. 2.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the third month following the date of enactment. However, the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission may take such anticipatory actions as may be necessary to implement this act. STATEMENT This bill requires the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (commission) to enter into a contract with the American Automobile Association (AAA) to allow for the provision of certain motor vehicle services to AAA customers at certain AAA facilities throughout the State.  The contract would be required, at a minimum and to the extent practicable, to allow for the following motor vehicle services to be provided at certain AAA facilities:  (1) motor vehicle registration renewals, replacements, and duplicates; (2) the titling and registration of, and the issuance of temporary registrations for, used motor vehicles; (3) drivers license renewals, duplicates, and upgrades to a REAL-ID license; (4) non-driver identification card renewals, duplicates, and upgrades to a REAL-ID identification card; (5) license plate surrenders; and (6) vision tests.  The bill also requires the commission, no later than one month after the contract agreement is signed by both the commission and AAA, to publish on its Internet website information concerning the motor vehicle services to be offered at AAA facilities throughout the State.
