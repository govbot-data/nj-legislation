A4993 ASSEMBLY, No. 4993 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED MAY 7, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  ALEX SAUICKIE District 12 (Burlington, Middlesex, Monmouth and Ocean) Co-Sponsored by: Assemblywoman Fantasia SYNOPSIS Deems farming permitted and inherently beneficial use in all municipal zoning districts under MLUL. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning farming as a permitted use and supplementing P.L.1975, c.291 (C.40:55D-1 et seq.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.     For the purposes of this section: Farming means the production of agricultural or horticultural products, consistent with applicable law. b.    Notwithstanding any law, ordinance, rule, or regulation to the contrary, farming on a parcel or parcels of land, whether contiguous or noncontiguous, shall be a permitted use in all municipal zoning districts and shall be considered an inherently beneficial use. 2.    This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill deems farming a permitted use in all municipal zoning districts and provides that farming is to be considered an inherently beneficial use.  For the purposes of this bill, farming is defined as the production of agricultural or horticultural products, consistent with applicable law.
