STATEMENT This bill would provide a gross income tax credit for a portion of the employment-related dependent care expenses incurred by resident taxpayers with New Jersey taxable income of $300,000 and under. Under federal law, certain taxpayers are allowed a credit, more commonly referred to as the Child and Dependent Care Tax Credit (CDCTC), based on a portion of the employment-related expenses incurred for the care of a qualifying individual during the taxable year.  A qualifying individual includes, among others, a dependent of the taxpayer who has not attained the age of 13.  For purposes of the CDCTC, employment-related expenses are expenses incurred to allow a taxpayer to remain gainfully employed.  Specifically, eligible employment-related expenses include those for household services and the care of a qualifying individual. The amount of the credit that a taxpayer receives is contingent upon the taxpayers adjusted gross income, the number of qualifying individuals, and whether the taxpayer also receives the federal Dependent Care Assistance Tax Credit. For purposes of the State gross income tax, resident taxpayers who receive the CDCTC are also allowed a State CDCTC equal to a percentage of the federal credit.  The State credit is only available to taxpayers with New Jersey taxable incomes under $150,000 and is reduced as certain income thresholds are exceeded.  According to the Fiscal Year 2027 Tax Expenditure Report, published by the Department of the Treasury, approximately 116,600 resident taxpayers claimed the States CDCTC in Tax Year 2024, with an average credit amount of $198. The bill would provide an additional credit for resident taxpayers with certain dependents. Specifically, the bill allows resident taxpayers with incomes not greater than $300,000 to claim a credit for up to 20 percent of the taxpayers employment-related expenses incurred for each dependent who is under the age of 13.  The amount of the credit would be provided in accordance with the following schedule, but not to exceed $5,000 per dependent per taxable year: New Jersey Taxable Income Credit Amount Not over $75,000 20% of employment- related expenses over $75,000 but not over $150,000 15% of employment- related expenses over $150,000 but not over $225,000 10% of employment- related expenses over $225,000 but not over $300,000 5% of employment- related expenses For purposes of the bill, employment-related expenses are defined the same as those expenses are defined under federal law for purposes of the CDCTC, except not including expenses for household services, such as those for the services of a cook, maid, or housekeeper. The bill also prohibits a resident taxpayer from simultaneously claiming both the State CDCTC and the credit established under the bill.  Thus, if the resident taxpayer is eligible for both credits, they would be required to choose which credit to claim for the taxable year.
