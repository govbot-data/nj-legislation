A5429 ASSEMBLY, No. 5429 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 Sponsored by: Assemblyman  ALEX SAUICKIE District 12 (Burlington, Middlesex, Monmouth and Ocean) SYNOPSIS Provides additional State transportation aid for certain school districts providing transportation services to significant numbers of nonpublic school students. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning transportation aid for school districts and supplementing P.L.2007, c.260 (C.18A:7F-43 et al.). Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.  A school district shall receive additional State transportation aid in any school year in which: a.  the number of regular nonpublic school students eligible for transportation pursuant to N.J.S.18A:39-1 exceeds the number of regular education public school students eligible for transportation pursuant to N.J.S.18A:39-1.  The additional State transportation aid for a school district under this subsection shall equal 50 percent of the difference between the amount of State transportation aid received by the school district on a per pupil basis and the actual cost of providing transportation to nonpublic school students who are eligible for transportation pursuant to N.J.S.18A:39-1 on a per pupil basis, multiplied by the number of nonpublic school students who are eligible for transportation pursuant to  N.J.S.18A:39-1; or b.  the number of regular nonpublic school students eligible for transportation pursuant to N.J.S.18A:39-1 exceeds the number of regular education public school students eligible for transportation pursuant to N.J.S.18A:39-1 by 25 percent or more.  The additional State transportation aid for a school district under this subsection shall equal 100 percent of the difference between the amount of State transportation aid received by the school district on a per pupil basis and the actual cost of providing transportation to nonpublic school students who are eligible for transportation pursuant to  N.J.S.18A:39-1 on a per pupil basis, multiplied by the number of nonpublic school students who are eligible for transportation pursuant to N.J.S.18A:39-1. 2.  This act shall take effect immediately. STATEMENT This bill provides additional State transportation aid to school districts in which the number of nonpublic school students who are eligible for transportation services exceeds the number of public school students eligible for transportation. If the number of nonpublic school students eligible for transportation services exceeds the number of public school students eligible for transportation, then the district would receive an additional amount of State aid equal to 50 percent of the difference between the amount of State transportation aid received by the school district on a per pupil basis and the actual cost of providing transportation to nonpublic school students on a per pupil basis, multiplied by the number of nonpublic school students who are eligible for transportation.  In the case of a school district in which the number of nonpublic school students eligible for transportation services exceeds the number of public school students eligible for transportation by 25 percent or more, the district would receive 100 percent of the difference between those amounts.
