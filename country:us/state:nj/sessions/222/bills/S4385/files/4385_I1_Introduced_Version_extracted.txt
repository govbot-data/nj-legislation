S4385 SENATE, No. 4385 STATE OF NEW JERSEY 222nd LEGISLATURE INTRODUCED JUNE 1, 2026 Sponsored by: Senator  VIN GOPAL District 11 (Monmouth) Senator  ANGELA V. MCKNIGHT District 31 (Hudson) SYNOPSIS Requires school districts to permit students excused absences for student illness. CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT As introduced. An Act concerning student attendance and supplementing chapter 36 of Title 18A of the New Jersey Statutes. Be It Enacted by the Senate and General Assembly of the State of New Jersey: 1.    a.  A school district in developing, adopting, and implementing policies and procedures regarding attendance of students at school shall define an excused absence from school to include an absence for an illness of the student, provided that the parent or guardian of the student submits supporting documentation to the school district upon the students return to school in accordance with subsection b. of this section. b.  The Commissioner of Education, in consultation with the Commissioner of Health, shall develop guidelines on defining an excused absence from school for a students illness. The guidelines shall include the supporting documentation that may be required by the school district upon the students return to school after an absence for an illness for the absence to be considered an excused absence. 2.    This act shall take effect on the first day of the first full school year next following the date of enactment, except that the Commissioner of Education may take any anticipatory administrative action in advance as may be necessary for the implementation of this act. STATEMENT This bill requires school districts to permit students excused absences for student illness. Under the bill, a school district in developing, adopting, and implementing policies and procedures regarding attendance of students at school is required to define an excused absence from school to include an absence for an illness of the student, provided that the parent or guardian of the student submits supporting documentation to the school district upon the students return to school. The Commissioner of Education, in consultation with the Commissioner of Health, will develop guidelines on defining an excused absence from school for a students illness.  The guidelines will include the supporting documentation that may be required by the school district upon the students return to school after an absence for an illness for the absence to be considered an excused absence.
